Scoreboard

The crew scoreboard: how each team is doing on the jobs that are all the way done. Office only for now.

In short

The board scores only jobs that Simpro shows complete and 100% invoiced, with real costs posted. In-progress jobs are listed for context and never count. Everything is a percentage; there are no dollar figures anywhere. Numbers come in from Simpro every night.

What you're looking at

1

Podium and race to 15%

The three residential teams (or the Commercial team on that tab) ranked by score, then one bar per team chasing the 15% net-margin goal. Green past the line, amber 10 to 15, red under 10.

2

This month

Jobs that closed and fully invoiced in the month you pick, who ran them, whether they beat the margin they were bid at, and the month's leader.

3

Teams and people

Tap a name to open their finished jobs and their in-progress jobs. The strip of squares is one square per finished job: green beat the bid, red missed it, hollow still in progress.

4

In progress

Every active Simpro job, split into still in rough, post-rough and finishing. Context only. A job moves onto the board once Simpro marks it complete and it is fully invoiced.

The score

Out of 100

Margin 70: average net margin divided by 30, so 15% earns half the points and 30% earns all. Beat the bid 30: share of finished jobs whose actual margin met the margin it was bid at. Labor hours, material and QC items show on the cards as information and are not scored: a job that hits its margin is a win even if the hours ran over. A team is ranked (1st, 2nd, 3rd) once it has 4 finished jobs; until then it shows against the goal.

Taking a job off someone

1

Open edit mode

Scroll to the bottom of the board and tap Fix who gets credit.

2

Tap a name on the job row

Tap a person's chip to take the job off them, or use the picker on the row to add someone. The board recalculates at once.

3

Save

Tap Save for everyone. The change is stored with the app, so every admin sees it, and it survives the nightly refresh. Undo is on the same panel.

Watch for

Credit comes from the job's foreman and lead in this app, matched to Simpro by job number. If the wrong person shows, the quickest lasting fix is correcting the foreman on the job itself; the edit mode is for the cases where the job record is fine but the credit still shouldn't land there.

Questions

Why isn't a finished job on the board?

It must be complete in Simpro, invoiced to exactly 100% (99.7% does not count), have costs posted, and be at least $2,500 unless you turn small jobs on in the header. Temp peds and extras stay out unless you turn them on.

Why did a finished job's margin change?

Late costs keep posting in Simpro for a few weeks after a job completes. The board shows what Simpro shows today.

How fresh are the numbers?

The header shows the last Simpro pull. It runs every night. If it is more than a week old the header says so in red; tell Koy.
