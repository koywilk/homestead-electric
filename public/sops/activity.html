Activity

Everything still open on the job, plus its running story

The 30-second version

Activity is two things on one screen. Top half: To do on this job — every open item rolled up in one place: change orders, return trips, punch, inspections, materials, questions. Bottom half: the timeline — CO status flips, return trips, job statuses, notes, and daily updates, newest first. Read it before you drive out and you arrive knowing what's open and what changed.

To do on this job — the top half

1

One screen, everything open

Collapsible groups roll up whatever's still outstanding: open change orders, open return trips, open punch across Rough, Finish, and QC, scheduled or failed inspections with open items, a Matterport scan that's needed, materials that need ordering or are awaiting pickup, open questions, and stranded QC items. Tap a group to expand it.

2

Watch the WAITING badges

The punch group shows a count, and if items are blocked you'll see an amber "N waiting" pill on the group plus a WAITING badge on each stuck item. Those are the ones you can't just knock out — find out what they're waiting on.

3

Green means done

If the job is truly clear, the top half shows a green "Nothing outstanding" state. That's the goal. Anything else is your worklist.

Every row is a shortcut

Tap any to-do row or any timeline event and the app jumps you straight to the tab it came from — a punch row opens Rough, a CO row opens Change Orders. See it, tap it, land where you fix it. No hunting through tabs.

The timeline — the bottom half

1

Catch up before you roll

Two minutes here replaces the "so where are we at?" phone call. The timeline shows CO status flips, return trips scheduled and signed off, job statuses, notes, and daily updates — newest first. Punch and photos don't post here; those live in their own tabs.

2

Answer "when did that change?"

GC asks about the added cans? The timeline shows the CO's latest status flip with a date — but no name, and it's the status date, not when the CO was created. For who added it and when, open the Change Orders tab. That's where the full record lives.

3

Going back further than 60 days

The timeline shows the last 60 days by default. Proving something from months ago? Tap Load older events at the bottom — the full history opens up, grouped by month.

Status times are approximate

Status rows show the job's last update time, not when that status actually flipped — the app doesn't keep per-flip timestamps. A rough status set weeks ago can show yesterday's date because someone edited anything on the job. Don't quote a status row's time as proof of when the status changed.

Quick answers

Do I have to post to this feed?

No — it writes itself from what you do in the app. Flip a CO, schedule a return trip, post a daily update: the timeline is the byproduct.

I closed punch and it's not in the timeline. Broken?

No — punch closes and photos don't post to this timeline. Closed punch drops off the To do rollup up top, and the item itself lives on the phase tab it belongs to.

The timeline shows nothing for days. Is the app broken?

More likely nothing changed in the app — which is its own signal. If work IS happening on site but the timeline is quiet, the work isn't being captured, and that's worth fixing.
