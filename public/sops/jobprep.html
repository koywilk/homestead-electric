Job Prep — Office Guide

One board for everything that has to happen before a job breaks ground. Office only (admin + manager). Every lane starts collapsed — tap a lane's header bar to open it (the counts on the header stay visible either way, and searching opens both lanes automatically).

Office Admin lane (Justin)

Every active job shows three chips: ACCOUNT, PRE-LIEN, TEMP PED. Tap a chip to mark it done; tap the small ▾ for the menu — including Not needed (N/A) for jobs that genuinely don't need one (gray chip, counts as handled). When Temp Ped is done, pick the Ped # right on the row. When all three are done or N/A, the row drops into the ✓ complete strip.

Temp Peds Out lane (Justin)

Every temp ped sitting on a job right now, one row per ped, on any job in any stage (a ped often stays after the finish, so this lane doesn't drop finished jobs). A ped counts as out when a job has Temp pedestal on site ticked, or a temp ped install job is marked Completed. An install job and the full job at the same address with the same Ped # are the same ped, so they share one row. The header shows how many are out, how many need a look, how many have been out 90+ days, and how many have a pickup scheduled.

Each row shows the big Ped #, the job, foreman and address, days out (red past 90), and whether a pickup is scheduled. Sort by Longest out or Ped #; the search box and foreman filter work here too (type 14 or #14 to find Ped #14). Needs a look sits on top: a ped with no number (pick it right on the row) or the same Ped # on two different jobs (one of them is wrong). Peds that were already out before this lane existed say Out since not recorded; set the date on the row. New ones start counting the day the box is ticked.

Schedule pickup creates a Temp Ped Pickup quick job for that ped and opens it so you can set the date and crew. When that pickup job is completed, the ped moves to Returned recently on its own (tagged auto). Picked up does it by hand: confirm, and it moves there with your name and today's date. Wrong one? Tap Undo in Returned recently and it goes back on the list. A pickup quick job made the old way (+ Quick) isn't linked to a ped, so use Picked up for those.

Temp ped card, then the job card. The temp ped card is usually made before the quote is signed, so there's no job card yet. Once the job card exists (imported from Simpro or made with + New), the ped's row shows Job card found with the job's name and why it matched (same name, same address, or same Simpro #). Nothing links until you say so: Link to this job ticks Temp pedestal on site on the job card, copies the Ped # and the out date over, and the row switches to the job card. Not this one hides that match for good. The same question shows at the top of the job card's Job Info tab, so whoever imports the job can link it right there. Linked by mistake? Tap Unlink on the row (or next to the Ped # in Job Info) and the job card goes back to how it was.

Pre-Job Prep lane (Koy)

The five prep checklist items are tappable right on the board — redline plans, cabinet plans, appliance specs, plans uploaded, hand-off. The row shows the prep stage, progress, and what's next. Like Justin's chips, each has a ▾ menu with Not needed (N/A) for items a job genuinely skips (progress then counts only the items that apply), and a PREP NOT NEEDED button marks everything remaining N/A for jobs that skip prep entirely. Hit it by mistake? A job that's complete because of N/A items shows ↩ UNDO NOT NEEDED — one tap puts all the items back to outstanding. Jobs started on an override move into their own amber CLEARED TO START dropdown inside this lane, so the main list is only what's still held.

Redline walk strip

Each prep row shows the job's redline walk right under the chips — the same walk that lives on the Change Orders board, so editing it here updates it there and vice versa. If there's no walk yet, tap ＋ Schedule Redline Walk to create one linked to the job. Once scheduled, pick the walk date (just for reference — it doesn't send reminders) and tap WALK DONE after you've walked it, which moves it to Walk Done — Cleaning Plans. Scheduled one by mistake? While it's still just scheduled, tap the small ✕ and confirm Remove? to delete the walk (the row goes back to ＋ Schedule). Once you hand off and Jeromy takes it to CO Owed / Sent / Signed, the strip just shows that status — you manage it from the CO board from there. The walk is for tracking only; it doesn't hold or clear the job on its own — the five checklist items still do that.

Edits to a walk save field by field: if two people are on the same walk — one setting the date, one typing what was added — both land. A save that fails shows a red toast; fix the signal and try again.

Start Without Full Prep (the override)

When a job needs to start but something (usually cabinet plans) hasn't arrived: hit START WITHOUT FULL PREP, add a note if useful, and confirm. The job immediately shows as Rough — Not Started on Upcoming Jobs — but the missing items STAY red on this board with an amber OVERRIDE badge until they're actually checked off. Nothing gets forgotten. Undo any time. The override records who cleared it and when.

Counts up top

Held in prep = jobs the gate is still blocking. Started on override = running jobs with prep items still owed. Cleared to start = fully prepped or overridden. Totals are company-wide even when you filter.
