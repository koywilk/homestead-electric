Job Prep — Office Guide

One board for everything that has to happen before a job breaks ground. Office only (admin + manager). Both lanes start collapsed — tap a lane's header bar to open it (the counts on the header stay visible either way, and searching opens both lanes automatically).

Office Admin lane (Justin)

Every active job shows three chips: ACCOUNT, PRE-LIEN, TEMP PED. Tap a chip to mark it done; tap the small ▾ for the menu — including Not needed (N/A) for jobs that genuinely don't need one (gray chip, counts as handled). When Temp Ped is done, pick the Ped # right on the row. When all three are done or N/A, the row drops into the ✓ complete strip.

Pre-Job Prep lane (Koy)

The five prep checklist items are tappable right on the board — redline plans, cabinet plans, appliance specs, plans uploaded, hand-off. The row shows the prep stage, progress, and what's next. Like Justin's chips, each has a ▾ menu with Not needed (N/A) for items a job genuinely skips (progress then counts only the items that apply), and a PREP NOT NEEDED button marks everything remaining N/A for jobs that skip prep entirely. Jobs started on an override move into their own amber CLEARED TO START dropdown inside this lane, so the main list is only what's still held.

Redline walk strip

Each prep row shows the job's redline walk right under the chips — the same walk that lives on the Change Orders board, so editing it here updates it there and vice versa. If there's no walk yet, tap ＋ Schedule Redline Walk to create one linked to the job. Once scheduled, pick the walk date (just for reference — it doesn't send reminders) and tap WALK DONE after you've walked it, which moves it to Walk Done — Cleaning Plans. Scheduled one by mistake? While it's still just scheduled, tap the small ✕ and confirm Remove? to delete the walk (the row goes back to ＋ Schedule). Once you hand off and Jeromy takes it to CO Owed / Sent / Signed, the strip just shows that status — you manage it from the CO board from there. The walk is for tracking only; it doesn't hold or clear the job on its own — the five checklist items still do that.

Start Without Full Prep (the override)

When a job needs to start but something (usually cabinet plans) hasn't arrived: hit START WITHOUT FULL PREP, add a note if useful, and confirm. The job immediately shows as Rough — Not Started on Upcoming Jobs — but the missing items STAY red on this board with an amber OVERRIDE badge until they're actually checked off. Nothing gets forgotten. Undo any time. The override records who cleared it and when.

Counts up top

Held in prep = jobs the gate is still blocking. Started on override = running jobs with prep items still owed. Cleared to start = fully prepped or overridden. Totals are company-wide even when you filter.
