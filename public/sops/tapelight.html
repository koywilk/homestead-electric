Tape Light

Every run tracked: location, length, driver — ordered, then installed

The 30-second version

One card per tape-light location. Type the length and the app sizes the driver for you. Two checkboxes carry the whole story — Driver Ordered, then Driver Installed — and the strip at the top counts what's still outstanding, so a glance shows what's missing before the ceiling closes.

Setting up a location

1

+ Add Tape Light Location

One card per run — "Kitchen uppers", "Stair treads", "Master toe-kick." Name it by where it lives.

2

Enter the footage — the driver sizes itself

Type the length and the driver size fills in automatically at 1.5W per foot, rounded up to the next stock driver (20 · 40 · 60 · 96 · 192 · 288W). Past 288W it flags "288W+" — that's a split-the-run conversation, not a bigger driver. No footage yet? While the size is blank you can pick the driver by hand from the dropdown (20W–288W) — handy when the office already specced it.

3

Note the driver location + track type

Where the driver hides (cabinet, closet shelf, attic) and which track the install needs — that's what makes the card orderable.

Which track to order — the GM rule

Routered-in or visible → track with flange. Tucked behind a cabinet lip → standard GM track. It's printed on the tab's spec box too.

Your name goes on the box

Check Driver Ordered or Driver Installed and a "✓ by Name · date" line appears under the checkbox — who did it and when, stamped the moment you tick it. That's how "who bought this / when did it go in" gets answered without anyone asking around. Only check it when it's actually true.

·

The summary strip is the punch line

"6 locations · 4/6 drivers ordered · 2/6 installed" — that top strip is how the office knows what to buy and how you know what's left without opening cards. Check the boxes the day it happens and the strip stays true.

Quick answers

The driver size looks wrong.

It's math off the footage — 1.5W per foot, next driver up. Check the length you typed first; fix the footage and the size follows.

It says 288W+.

The run is past the biggest stock driver — talk to the office about splitting it into two runs / two drivers before anything gets ordered.

Job has no tape light at all.

There's a "no tape light" toggle on the tab — it just moves the tab to the end of the bar so it's out of the way. Nothing is deleted, and any rows already typed stay put.
