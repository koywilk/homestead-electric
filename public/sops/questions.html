Job Questions

How to ask, tag, share, and answer job questions

The 30-second version

Every job has Questions under Rough and Finish, split by floor. When you hit something you need an answer on — panel location, fixture pick, anything — you type it in and tag WHO it is for. The moment it's tagged, it shows up on that person's live link. Their answer comes back automatically and lands in the amber Incoming from Links panel at the top. That's the whole loop.

Adding a question

1

Open the job and find the right floor

Go to the Questions tab (or the Rough / Finish tab) and find the section for the floor you're on — Upper, Main, or Basement.

2

Type the question and press Enter

Use the "Add a question…" box. Write it the way you'd say it out loud.

3

Tag who it's for

Tap the + recipient tag on the question and pick or type who it's for — Designer, GC name, homeowner. This is the single most important step.

Why the tag matters

A tagged question appears on that person's live link within seconds — no re-sharing, no new link, nothing else to do. An untagged question goes nowhere. It sits in the app until somebody shares it by hand. To sweep them, tap the Unassigned (N) chip up top — one tap shows every untagged question.

Questions can also come off the plans

Question or Problem pins dropped on a live plan (the FieldInk crew link) flow into this same Questions list. Those rows carry a plan badge — blue Pinned on plan means it's located on the plan but not answered yet; green Answered on plan means it is.

Shortcut

If you're already viewing one person's questions (their filter chip is active up top), anything you add is tagged to them automatically.

Getting questions to the designer / GC / client

Each person gets ONE link for the job, made once, used the whole job.

1

Open the Share picker

It's in the Questions area, at the top of the section.

2

Pick that person's questions

Questions are grouped by recipient — use Select all on their group, or hand-pick.

3

Save it as a NAMED link

Name it after the person — "Dusty", "Designer Sarah". The name is what makes future tagged questions flow to it automatically.

4

Copy it and send it once

Text or email it to them. Done — that link is theirs for the life of the job.

You never re-send the link

New questions you tag for them appear on their existing link automatically. The Saved Links list shows every link that's out — deleting one kills it immediately and they see nothing. The Shared / Not shared badge on each question tells you whether the link people can currently see it. Each saved link also has a Preview button next to Copy link — it opens that person's exact page in the app, read-only, so you can check what they see before you text it, or when a designer says "I don't see that question."

The Incoming from Links panel — your inbox

At the top of each phase's Questions there's an amber panel: INCOMING FROM LINKS. Everything the link people just sent collects here, so you never scroll floor-by-floor hunting for it:

·

Answers they submitted

Green box — applies to the question automatically.

·

"Ask for more info" notes

They need something from you before they can answer.

·

Discussion replies where they spoke last

The ball is in your court.

The one subtle rule: answers only auto-apply to OPEN questions

A link answer lands on a question that's still open → it applies itself, done. But if you already closed the question yourself and THEIR answer arrives after, the app will not overwrite your answer — the late answer shows on the question for you to adopt or ignore. Nothing is lost, nothing is clobbered silently; when two answers exist, a human picks.

How you know you're clear

Handle the item — reply, answer, or check it off — and the card drops out of the panel back to its floor. Empty panel = nothing waiting on you.

Finding your way around

·

Status chips

All / Open / Needs reply / Answered. Tap Needs reply to see only the questions where someone is waiting on the crew.

·

Answered is collapsed

Answered questions fold into one ✓ ANSWERED (N) line. Tap to expand.

·

Compact cards

Open questions show just the question and the answer box. Tap More for the note-to-GC, photo attach, reminder, or to start a discussion.

·

The green NEW badges know what you've seen

The Questions tab label carries a green "N NEW" pill and answered questions get a NEW ANSWER badge — counted since YOUR last visit on YOUR device, so what's new to you is exactly what's flagged. Expanding the answered list marks it seen.

·

The bell reminds a TEAMMATE — not the link person

The bell on a question opens a REMIND WHO? picker of Homestead team members and sends that person an in-app reminder. It cannot reach the designer, GC, or homeowner the question is tagged to. Use it to put a stalled question in front of whoever on the crew or office needs to chase it.

Answering a question yourself

1

Type it in the ANSWER box

Plain language is fine.

2

Record how you got it

Under More → HOW ANSWERED, tap how you got the answer — in person, phone, text — and add context if it's useful. That's the paper trail.

3

Check the box to close it

Unchecking reopens it. Nothing is lost either way.

Photos, files, and discussions

·

Photo on a question

More → Add photo. Snap the area in question so the person on the link sees exactly what you mean instead of guessing which wall.

·

Photos on their answer

When they attach photos or files, those show right under the answer. Their answer stays live to their link — if they edit the text or pull a photo later, the change syncs into the app, and clearing everything reopens the question.

·

Discussion

Every question has a reply thread — both sides can go back and forth with attachments, unlimited turns. Their replies light up Needs reply and the Incoming panel.

Do / Don't

Do

  • Tag every question with who it's for
  • Make ONE named link per person and reuse it all job
  • Check the amber Incoming panel when you open the job
  • Add a photo when the question is about something physical
  • Use the discussion thread for back-and-forth

Don't

  • Leave questions untagged — they go nowhere
  • Make a new link every time you have a question
  • Let GC answers and notes sit unread
  • Make the designer guess which wall you mean
  • Answer over text message where nobody else can see it

Quick answers

Do I re-share when I add a question?

No — if it's tagged to them, it's already on their link.

No link exists for that person yet?

Make one in the Share picker, name it after them, send it once.

What happens when they answer?

It applies automatically — the question goes green, marked answered "via link," with their attachments included.

They couldn't answer and asked us something instead?

That's the "Ask for more info" note. It lands in your Incoming panel — reply in the discussion.

The GC says they answered on their portal?

Questions shared to a GC also show on their GC Portal. The office applies portal answers from its inbox — the answer lands on the exact question, marked answered via the portal.

Need to cut someone off?

Delete their link in Saved Links. It stops working immediately.
