Job Start — Commercial Pre-Con Guide

Every commercial job goes through the same 12 steps before anyone is on site. Each step is a phase. A job's current phase is the first one that still has open items. The work rarely happens in order, so you can check an item in any phase at any time. Brady and Justin own the pre-con phases; Zane and Abe own phase 6 (site coordination). Foremen can open the board to see where a job is, but only the office and the people wearing a commercial hat can check items.

The 12 phases

1 Contract award · 2 Project setup · 3 Request submittals · 4 Submittal review · 5 Release long-lead gear · 6 Site coordination · 7 Site takeoff & ordering · 8 Temporary power · 9 Ufer · 10 Foundation / blockouts · 11 Building underground · 12 Slab coordination. The bar on each card shows all 12: teal = done, blue = the current phase, amber = closed with items owed, grey = coming up. Under the job name, the percentage shows how much of all 12 phases is done or marked not needed (for example "62% · 58 of 94"). Owed items count as not done.

The board

On the Job Start board every job starts folded to one line under its current phase: the name, the foreman, an OWED or GEAR LATE flag when there is one, and the percentage. Tap the line to open the full card; tap ▼ next to the name to fold it again. The job's own Job Start tab always shows the full card.

Checklist or Phases

Each card has a Checklist | Phases switch above the bar. The app remembers your pick on your device. Checklist (the default) lists all 12 phases as checklists, one item per row. Phases with items left start open, so you see everything still to do at once. Finished phases fold to one line with DONE and their count; tap a phase header to open or fold it, or tap its number on the bar to open it. Phases is the compact view: the current phase's items as chips. Tap a number on the bar to look back at a finished phase or ahead at a later one; later phases can be checked from there too.

Checking items

Tap a row (or a chip in the Phases view) to cycle ○ open → ✓ done → — not needed. Done rows show who checked them and when. When the last item in a phase is checked, the job rolls into the next phase on its own and the card moves to that phase's section. Un-checking an item in an earlier phase honestly pulls the job back. Some items are linked to a log: the Drive folder (phase 2), long-lead requested and lead times (phase 3), RFI list, sent to GC and all approved (phase 4), and released + PO, ship dates, procurement log and ship mode (phase 5). Tap them to check them by hand like any other item; the row says which log it's tied to. They also check themselves when the Gear & Submittals log, the RFI log or the Drive folder shows it, whichever comes first. Photo items (Ufer, underground) take pictures right under the chips; date items (inspections, energized) take a date.

A note or a doc on one item

Every chip ends with a small note mark. Tap it and a panel opens under the chips for that one item: a note (say what the award doc was, who sent it, when, for how much, who signed) and Attach doc for the file itself (PDF, photo, anything). The mark lights up on chips that carry a note or a doc, with the doc count next to it, so you can see at a glance which items have paperwork behind them. The phase note at the bottom of the current phase is still there for the phase as a whole.

Move on with items owed

When the site is ready but something in the current phase is still outstanding (usually a submittal), tap MOVE ON WITH ITEMS OWED, add a note, and confirm. The job moves to the next phase now, but the owed items stay red with an OWED badge inside their phase and keep their My Day rows until they are actually checked. Nothing gets forgotten. Undo any time.

Gear & Submittals and RFIs

The Gear & Submittals tab is one row per long-lead item (switchgear, transformers, panelboards, generator, lighting package, fire alarm…). Fill in requested date, lead time, status, PO, released date, promised ship, required-on-site and delivery; the phase 3 and 5 chips check themselves from these rows. A released item past its promised ship with no delivery shows LATE and lands on the pre-con My Day. Tap Mark checked after the weekly vendor call. The RFIs tab tracks questions to the GC or engineer and what each one blocks.

Drive folder and plans

A commercial job imported from Simpro creates its Google Drive folder and pulls the Simpro plans by itself (same folder and naming as residential). A job made by hand does the same once its Simpro # is typed in. The folder re-pulls every Monday through pre-con so revisions and addenda arrive on their own; Pull from Simpro on Job Info still works any time.

Ready to Start

When all 12 phases are closed the job leaves Job Start and shows under Ready to Start on the Job Board with a reminder to assign a foreman and crew. Set In Progress on Job Info (Commercial section → Stage) when the crew mobilizes.
