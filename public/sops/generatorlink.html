The Generator Link

The homeowner picks which loads ride the generator — themselves, on their phone

The 30-second version

Instead of walking a homeowner through their load choices over the phone, you Send to Homeowner and text them the link yourself — the app copies the link, it never sends anything on its own. They open the page, confirm or remove what Homestead recommended, add what else matters, put it in priority order, sign, and submit. Their picks land back in the app's generator section where you see "N of M on the generator." No transcription, no guessing what they meant.

Link rules (same as every link): one link per job's homeowner · re-copy the same link if they lose it · it shows only the generator page · Reset & Resend is the do-over.

Sending it

1

Get the load list right — then star your recommendations

The list builds itself from Home Runs (the Synced with Home Runs pill, with Re-sync now), and + Add Manually covers loads that aren't home runs. Then hit the ★ on the loads Homestead recommends — the star is the only thing that shapes their page. Checkboxes and Select all only change your office list and counts; nothing arrives pre-picked on their end.

2

Send to Homeowner — then text them the link yourself

Send to Homeowner preps their page and copies the link — you'll see ✓ Link copied! The app never texts or emails anything: you send that link, by text or email, every time. Preview shows you their exact page before you do.

Their side of the link

Your starred loads lead the page as RECOMMENDED BY HOMESTEAD — they have to hit ✓ Confirm or Remove on each one. Everything else sits below, off until they add it. A counter tracks panel spaces as they pick.

3

Their answer comes back on its own

When they submit, their selections land in the app — Check for response hurries a look. And it's more than checkboxes: their list comes back in the priority order they set with the ▲▼ arrows — the numbers are their power priority, not random — plus any note they attached to a load. Read the notes; they carry install detail.

4

Update Dedicated Loads — the button that drives the panel work

Once picks land, Update Dedicated Loads (n) is the next step: one confirmable click stamps every chosen circuit's Home Run row onto the panel's Dedicated Loads, and reverts rows for loads they took off. It's reversible.

Plans changed? Reset & Resend

New generator size, new load list, or they want a redo — Reset & Resend clears their old submission and puts a fresh page on the same link. Their old answer doesn't linger to confuse anyone.

Their picks are the record — and it's signed

They can't submit without typing their full name and a date, and the app shows Signed: name · date on the banner and in the response. So once the homeowner has submitted, that's what they chose — signed, in writing, on their own phone. If they call later saying they wanted the freezer on it, the submission settles the "did we ask?" question. Don't re-do their picks for them in the app without a Reset & Resend paper trail.

Quick answers

They say they never got it.

Copy the link again and text it straight to them — same link, no reset needed if they haven't submitted.

They call about a red warning on their page.

The page counts panel spaces as they pick — "N of 40 spaces." Go over and it says "That's more than a 40-space panel holds — Homestead will confirm the panel size." It doesn't block their submit; you sort panel size after.

They want to change their answer.

Reset & Resend. They get a clean page; the new submission replaces the old one.

We picked the loads together on site instead.

Fine — set them in the generator section yourself. The link is for when they're deciding without you standing there.

What do they see besides the loads?

Just the generator page for their job. No pricing, no other tabs, nothing else.
