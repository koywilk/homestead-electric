The Live View Link

Real-time Home Runs progress for anyone who asks "how far along are you?"

The 30-second version

The Share button on Home Runs copies a view-only link showing the job's pull status in real time — what's pulled, what's left, by panel and floor. Send it to the GC or super who keeps calling for status, and the calls stop: they watch the numbers move as your crew checks runs off.

Link rules (this one's different): view-only, always current · one link serves everyone who wants status — no per-person links, no delete-to-kill like the other share links · shows Home Runs only, nothing else on the job.

Using it

1

Home Runs → Share

Link's on your clipboard. Text it with one line: "Live pull status for [job] — updates itself."

2

They see your check-offs — live

Same runs, same pulled marks, updating as your crew works. One heads-up: the link's percentage counts every row on the sheet — blank seeded rows included — so it reads lower than your in-app tab, which only counts named runs. Expect the gap. And keep check-offs same-day: this link makes your pull tracking public-facing.

View-only means view-only

Nobody on this link can change anything, and nothing else on the job is visible — no questions, no notes, no money. It's a scoreboard, not a door.

When to reach for it

A GC who calls twice a week for status. A super planning trades behind you. A builder who wants proof of progress without a site walk. One text, zero future calls.

Quick answers

Can they mess with our numbers?

No. It's read-only — the only people who can check runs off are in the app.

The GC says the numbers look behind.

Check your own tab first. The link's percentage counts the blank seeded rows your tab skips, so the link always reads lower than the app — that gap is the blank rows, not late check-offs. If your tab looks behind too, get the crew checking off same-day.

Who should NOT get this?

It stays inside Home Runs — no questions, notes, or money. But it's more than a count: they see every named run by name, panel, wire size, and floor, each with its pulled checkmark, plus the job name and address up top. Send it to people who should see that detail — and never in place of a real conversation when a job's genuinely behind.
