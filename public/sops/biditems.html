Bid Items

What the Simpro bid calls for, and what's been assigned against it

The 30-second version

This tab is the job's Simpro bid: every cost center and line item, quantities included. Check it before you schedule material, pull wire, or write a change order, because it answers "is this in the bid?" Names and quantities only, no pricing. It sits right next to Plans & Links (it used to be a folded section at the top of that tab).

Using it

1

Search it first

Type a part or task in the search box. A match means it's in the bid. "No match — looks like this would be a change order" means it isn't, and the app just answered the CO question for you.

2

Required vs Assigned

Sections start folded; tap one to see its cost centers, then tap a cost center to see every material line with Required (what the bid calls for) next to Assigned (what's been allocated to it from the shop, a van, or a delivered PO). Red over means more was assigned than bid, with the overage; orange to go means still short; green means on the number.

3

Wire at a glance

Wire and cable sit first in every cost center, and the Wire & cable — whole job box at the bottom adds every cost center together so footage is one glance. Over only and Wire only narrow the list.

4

Refresh when Simpro changed

The bid loads when you open the job and is kept for 12 hours. Refresh stock re-reads Simpro right now. The old bid lines (assemblies and one-offs) are still there under Bid lines inside each cost center.

5

Orders: every PO on the job

Orders sits at the top of this tab, folded, with the count on its header. It lists every PO card from Rough and Finish Material Tracking: PO number, supplier, Need to Order, Order Sent or Picked Up, who and when. Open ones sit first. Order material asks Rough-In or Finish, then adds a new PO card on that tab's Material Tracking and takes you there to fill it in. POs made only in Simpro Mobile don't show here unless their number is on a card.

Deductions aren't overages

A grey deduction is a Simpro line that takes material back out of the base bid. It is never counted as "over".

Quick answers

It says "Add a Simpro Job # to this job to see bid items."

The job has no Simpro job number yet. Add it on Job Info. A quote shows this too, because a quote has no bid on Simpro's jobs side until it converts.

The numbers don't match what I see in Simpro.

Tap Refresh stock; the tab may be showing the copy from earlier today. Still off after a refresh → tell the office which cost center and line.

Where did Bid Items go on Plans & Links?

It moved here, one tab over. Plans & Links has a line at the top that jumps straight to this tab.
