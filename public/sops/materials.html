Materials — Commercial ordering

Every commercial job card has a Materials tab, next to Bid Items. It's the same Material Tracking the residential crew uses on Rough and Finish, with one list for the whole job.

Order cards

Each card is one order: what's on it, the supplier, the PO number, the pickup date, and Picked Up when it's in hand. Add a card, type or paste the list, pick the supplier.

Charge to: pick the cost center

A commercial job has many Simpro cost centers (Site Underground, Power Feeds, Power Rough In, Lighting Rough In…). When you tap Send on a card, pick the cost center the order is charged to under Charge to. Send stays grey until you pick one, so nothing lands on the wrong cost center. The pick is saved on the card, so a resend uses the same one. If the list is empty, tap Load them (or open Bid Items) to pull the job's cost centers from Simpro.

Order from the bid

New order from the bid shows the job's bid lines grouped by cost center. Pick what you need; quantities come from the bid. Each cost center becomes its own PO, already charged to the right place, and they all go to CED on one email.

After it's sent

The card shows the PO number and who sent it. Cards update themselves from Simpro once the PO is received, same as residential. Open orders show up in the office's unsent-PO list and in Open Items, marked Materials. The Bid Items tab lists every PO card on the job, and its Order material button opens this screen.
