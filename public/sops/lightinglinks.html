Lighting Links

Three links, three outside companies — which one goes to whom

The 30-second version

The Panelized Lighting tab can mint three different links, each for a different partner: the collab link (the LV company works IN it), the Lutron hub link (the plans company sees EVERY Lutron job on one page), and the loads link (a read-only list for an AV programmer). Send the wrong one and the right person can't do their job — this page is the sorting hat.

Link rules (same as every link): one link per company · send once, it stays current · each shows only its own slice of the job.

Why Lutron jobs have no loads link

Lutron designed the system — they sent US the loads list. Sharing it back at them is noise, so the button only shows on Control 4 / Crestron jobs where Homestead does the engineering and a programmer genuinely needs it.

Using them

1

Collab link — when the LV company comes aboard

Send it as soon as they're on the job. Their module/channel entries land in the app's lists — nobody retypes a spreadsheet later.

2

Hub link — once, ever

The plans company gets it one time. When a new Lutron job starts, it's already on their hub — do not send them per-job links, that's the exact problem the hub kills.

3

Loads link — when the programmer asks for the list

Copy, send, done. It stays current as loads change, so they're never programming off a stale export.

Quick answers

The LV company emailed a spreadsheet of modules instead.

Point them back at the collab link — entered there, it lands in the app where the crew works from. A spreadsheet is retyping debt.

New Lutron job — does the plans company need anything?

No. It's on their hub already. That's the feature.

Which link for the GC?

None of these — GCs get question links and the portal. These three are for the lighting partners only.
