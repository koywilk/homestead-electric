Question Links

The link a GC, designer, or homeowner uses to answer job questions

The 30-second version

Each person gets ONE named link for the job — made once in the Share picker, named after them, sent once. From then on, any question you tag for them appears on their link within seconds, and their answers come back into the app automatically. You never re-send anything.

Link rules (same as every link): one per person · send the link, never a screenshot · they see only their questions · delete = dead instantly.

Making one

1

Open the Share picker

In any Questions section — the "?" you tapped is sitting right next to it.

2

Pick their questions

Questions are grouped by recipient — Select all on their group, or hand-pick.

3

Save it as a NAMED link

Name it after the person — "Dusty", "Designer Sarah". The name is the magic: it's what makes future questions tagged to them flow onto this link automatically.

How the wiring actually works: the recipient TAG on a question and the link's NAME are matched as the same words. The tag lives right on the question row — tap + recipient on the row, and it reads For: Dusty once set. Tag a question "Dusty" and the link named "Dusty" picks it up; tag it "Dustin" and it goes nowhere. Capitalization doesn't matter — spelling does. Pick one form of each person's name and stick to it.

4

Copy, send once, done

Text or email it with one line of what it is. That link is theirs for the life of the job.

"Share all" is a different animal

The Share all button in the same modal copies one generic link that shows every question on the job — no name, no scoping. Fine for someone who genuinely needs the whole list; wrong for a designer who should only see theirs. When in doubt, make a named link.

See exactly what they'll see

Every saved link has a Preview button — it opens their exact page, read-only, without leaving the app. Thirty seconds of preview beats an awkward "why can I see that?" phone call.

What THEY can do on it

·

Answer, with photos and files

Their answer applies to the question automatically — it goes green in the app, marked answered "via link," attachments included. Their page saves as they type, so partial answers and notes can show up in the app before they ever hit Submit — Submit is just the formal "done."

·

Ask for more info

Can't answer without something from you? Their note lands in the amber Incoming from Links panel — reply in the question's discussion thread.

·

Go back and forth

Every question has a discussion thread both sides can use, unlimited turns, attachments included. Their replies light up Needs reply so nothing waits silently.

·

Change their answer later

A link answer stays tied to their page. If they edit the text or pull a photo, the change syncs into the app — and if they clear everything, the question reopens. Crew-typed answers can't be touched from a link.

Late answers don't get lost

If they answer a question the crew already closed, the app doesn't overwrite anything — the late answer surfaces on the question so you can adopt it or leave the crew's answer standing. Your call, made visibly.

Managing the links

·

Saved Links is the roster

Every link that's out is listed there. The Shared / Not shared badge on each question tells you whether the link people can currently see it.

·

Delete is the kill switch

Person's off the job? Delete their link — it stops working that second, and they see nothing.

Quick answers

They lost the link.

Copy the SAME one from Saved Links and send it again. Never mint a second link for the same person.

Do I re-share when I add a question?

No. Tag it to them and it's already on their link.

Their answer isn't good enough.

Uncheck the question to reopen it — the stamps clear and it goes back in front of them on their link. The app remembers the answer you rejected, so the exact same answer can't close it again; a genuinely different one will.

Two people at the GC want it.

Two named links — one each. Their answers stay attributable, and you can cut either off without touching the other.
