Change Orders

Any work outside the bid gets captured here — before it gets done

The 30-second version

A customer asks for anything that's not in the bid → you make a Change Order on the job, before you do the work. Field captures it (who it's for, what it is, time, tasks, materials, photos) and hits Submit Change Order — the office gets the push, sends it, tracks the yes/no, and bills it. The status pill row is the whole system: set it right and everyone downstream knows what to do next.

The one rule

Capture the change the moment it comes up — before the work happens. An unwritten change becomes free work: nobody remembers the details, and the office can't bill what was never written down.

Is it even a change order?

·

Check Bid Items first

Open the job's Bid Items panel and search the part or task. A match → it's in scope, no CO. "No match — looks like this would be a change order" → the app just answered for you.

Field: creating a CO on site

1

+ Add Change Order

Bottom of the Change Order log. A new card appears, already on Needs to be Sent (red).

2

Who and what

Send CO To — the customer or GC contact name. Description of Task — ONE line, e.g. "Add 4 cans in dining room." That line becomes the email subject and what everyone sees on dashboards, so keep it short and specific.

3

Time, tasks, materials

Estimated Time — "2 hrs", "half day". Task (In Field) — one bullet per real task; that's what the crew reads when the work goes live. Material Needed — parts list, and set the source (Shop, Home Depot, CED, Platt, Amazon) so the office knows whether to pull stock or cut a PO.

4

Photos / files

Snap the area or the existing condition — photos travel with the CO email. Got a spec sheet, cut sheet, or supplier quote PDF? Add photo / file takes PDFs and Word/Excel docs too — no need to photograph a screen.

5

Hit Submit Change Order

The amber bar on the card reads "Draft — add a description, then submit" until the description has text, then "Ready — submit so the office picks it up" — that's also what unlocks the green Submit Change Order button. Tap it: you'll see "Change Order submitted — the office has it," the job's coordinator and Jeromy get the push, and the card shows a green submitted-by line with your name and the date. You don't email anyone.

Saving is not submitting. A CO left as a draft gets flagged DRAFT — NOT SUBMITTED on the office CO board with a Chase button, and the 8am chase calls out any crew draft a day old. Submit before you leave.

The better way: promote from a Job Note

Wrote the change down in a Job Note during the walk? Select the lines → Convert → Change Order → New CO (or Add to existing). Bullets become the Task list, materials merge in, and the note stays linked. The chip on the note line shows the CO's status color forever — glance at the note weeks later and see whether the CO went through.

The undo has two speeds, on purpose: the 10-second toast fully undoes the promote. After that, the small × on the note's chip removes just that line's contribution — and if the office already edited the CO or moved its status, the CO itself survives the ×. You can't accidentally torch office work by cleaning up a note.

Show it on the plan

Drew the CO on a plan in FieldInk? The CO card grows a blue Marked up on plan — view chip — one tap opens the marked-up sheet. Way better than describing the spot in words, and the office sees exactly where the work goes.

The status ladder

StatusWhat it means
Needs to be Sent Drafted, not sent. Every new CO starts here — and it stays a crew draft until Submit Change Order is tapped. A Send-By date can be set — it drives the office reminder.
Sent — Pending Approval Email went out; waiting on the yes/no.
Approved Customer said yes. From here: crew on site closes it, or it converts to a Return Trip.
Scheduled Approved with a date set (usually via its Return Trip). Shows on the calendar and Crew Planner.
Work Completed Physically done. Card collapses to a green bar; an invoice task fires for the office.
Converted to RT The work now lives on a Return Trip card. The CO stays in the log as history — edits on the RT don't flow back.
Denied Customer said no. Work does not happen; the CO stays on record. This is NOT the same as Remove.

Office: send, track, bill

1

Polish the draft

The push lands the moment the field hits Submit Change Order. Read the description, tasks, materials, photos — sharpen anything thin. The email goes out next, as-is. A CO sitting unsubmitted shows DRAFT — NOT SUBMITTED on the CO board — hit its Chase button instead of waiting.

2

Send it

Email CO pre-fills the whole thing (Chat for an internal heads-up; Simpro copies a clean summary and opens the SimPro page). Then set Sent — Pending Approval.

3

Stamp the Quote #

Once the CO is sent from Simpro, type the Simpro quote number into the Quote # field right next to the CO title. It stamps who added it and when, and it's how COs get found by number later. Clearing the field clears the stamp.

4

Record the answer

Yes → Approved (lead + foreman get the push). No → Denied — never Remove; denied stays on the record.

5

Get the work done

Crew on the job today, per the Crew Planner? A green Mark Work Completed button sits on the approved CO — one tap in the field closes it. Nobody scheduled today, or it's a weekend? Convert to Return Trip — the RT pre-fills from the CO with everything on it, photos included; schedule it through the Crew Planner.

6

Bill it

On Work Completed, a "merge or invoice" task lands on the Tasks dashboard. Pull the CO into the SimPro invoice, dismiss the task.

Do / Don't

Do

  • Create the CO before the work happens
  • Promote from Job Notes when the change is already written there
  • One line in the description, real bullets in the task list
  • Photos of the area — they ride with the email
  • Hit Submit Change Order — a saved draft isn't submitted
  • Use Denied for a customer no

Don't

  • Do the work first and "write it up later"
  • Remove a denied CO — Remove is only for created-in-error
  • Handle approval over text where nobody can see it
  • Forget the status pill — it's how the office knows what's next

Quick answers

Mark Work Completed isn't showing.

It only appears when the CO is Approved AND a crew is on the job TODAY per the Crew Planner. Nobody scheduled today (or a weekend) shows Convert to Return Trip instead. Phase status doesn't matter — flipping rough or finish changes nothing.

I completed / converted by accident.

Both have undo. Completed: the ↩ Undo Complete button sits on the collapsed green bar itself — don't expand the card looking for it (once expanded, tap the Approved status pill instead). Converted: on the gray card → Undo Convert — it asks you to confirm first (the created RT stays — delete it separately). While that RT exists, the CO keeps its CONVERTED TO RT badge on its own; only a confirmed Undo Convert takes it back to Approved.

Can I edit after it's been sent?

Every field stays editable until Completed — but the customer has the version they got. Re-tap Email CO to send the current one.

Who gets notified?

Submit → the job's coordinator + Jeromy. Approved → lead + foreman. Work Completed → foreman, admins, managers. It's automatic — nobody has to ping anyone.

My CO is stalled — not sent, or no answer yet.

Tap the remind bell on the card — one tap sends a "Change order reminder" push to Jeromy, pre-filled with the job and the CO description. No texting needed.

The CO isn't on the calendar.

Calendar shows COs with a scheduled date that aren't Completed, Denied, or Converted. Set the date or schedule its RT.
