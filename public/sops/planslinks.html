Plans & Links

The job's paper trail — plans, files, and every link the job depends on

The 30-second version

This tab is where the job's documents live: the live plans from FieldInk, the Bid Items search, the linked Drive files, uploaded files, and the links — plan rooms, supplier pages, the crew's live link. If someone on the job would ever ask "where's the…?", the answer should be a labeled row on this tab, not a text thread.

Using it

1

Work off the plans that are HERE

The plans on this tab are the job's current set. A photo of last month's plans on your camera roll is how walls end up in the wrong place — when plans change, this tab is what gets updated. The freshest set is the LIVE PLANS — FIELDINK section: every plan shared live from FieldInk shows up there on its own.

2

Open the live set, check the stamp

Hit Open on a Live Plans row for the marked-up, pin-tappable viewer. A PINS badge means the plan has pins on it, and the freshness stamp ("just updated", "2h ago") tells you how current it is. When a plan gets superseded, use Hide so nobody works off the old sheet — Show brings it back.

3

Check the bid before you call it a CO

At the very top of the tab is Bid Items (Simpro) — open it and search every cost center and line item on the job's Simpro bid, quantities included. It's the first stop before scheduling material, pulling wire, or writing a CO: it answers "is this in the bid?" Names and quantities only — no pricing.

4

Drive files load once the folder is linked

The Drive Files section fills in on its own only after a Drive folder is linked to the job — linking is the manual part. Paste the folder URL into the box on this tab, hit Create Drive folder, or have an admin run Sync Drive from the util menu. You can also upload files straight from the phone — inspection cards, spec sheets, the counter drawing the GC handed you.

5

Add links with real labels

Every link gets a label. "Lighting plan room" beats a bare URL — six months from now nobody remembers which link was which. There are four fixed sections — Plans, Lighting Schedules, Panel Schedules, Matterport Link. Everything else — supplier pages, plan rooms — gets its own group: hit + Add Section, name it, and stack labeled links inside.

The crew live link

The Crew link button sits right on the job's Live Plans rows. It ties back into the app — Question and Problem pins made from it land in this job's Questions. Copy it from here when someone needs it; don't mint new links when one exists.

Office: push to Simpro

New plans landed in Drive? The Push to Simpro button on the Google Drive Plans header adds them to the linked Simpro job — add-only, nothing in Simpro gets removed.

Quick answers

The GC handed me new plans on paper.

Photograph or scan them and get them on this tab (upload), then tell the office so the official set gets swapped. Don't let the only copy live in your truck.

A file I need isn't here.

Check the Drive Files section first — it may just be in the linked folder. If all you see is a box asking for a folder URL, no Drive folder is linked yet — ask the office to link one. Still missing → ask the office to drop it in Drive; it'll appear here once the folder is linked.

Can I delete an old link?

If it's dead, yes — but a superseded plan link is better relabeled "OLD — do not use" than deleted, so nobody re-adds it thinking it's missing.
