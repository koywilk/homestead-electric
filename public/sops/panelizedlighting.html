Panelized Lighting

Lighting control systems — loads, keypads, and the plan-changes loop

The 30-second version

Jobs with a lighting control system get tracked here: pick the system from the pills (Control 4, Lutron, Savant, Crestron, or Other), hit Lock system, then keep three lists true — Loads, Keypads, and Panel Loads. On Lutron jobs, the Changes From Original Plan log at the top is how the field and Tech Lighting stay honest with each other about what moved — every change gets a line, and Tech Lighting marks it green when it's folded into the plans and quote.

The system pick

·

Pick the system, then lock it yourself

Five pills at the top: Control 4, Lutron, Savant, Crestron, Other — the pick colors the whole tab (Lutron rides blue). Pick Other and a System Name field appears for the oddball platforms. Nothing locks on its own: until someone hits the Lock system button next to the pills, one stray tap flips the whole job to another system. Hit it — once it reads Locked, the other pills go dead. If the job genuinely changed platforms, that's an office conversation, not a re-tap.

Savant jobs get a different screen

This guide describes the Lutron / Control 4 / Crestron layout. Pick Savant and the whole tab swaps to a per-panel slot list instead — tap an empty slot to add a load, tap an occupied slot to edit it, tandem feeders handled right in the list. The keep-it-true discipline is the same; the screen is just built around panels and slots.

Changes From Original Plan — the log that keeps everyone honest

1

Every change gets a line, in the right room

Rooms match the plan labels. Each change is typed as Added, Moved (with from → to), Removed, or Changed — written at the moment it happens on site, not reconstructed at trim.

2

Mislabeled a line? Pencil it

Every logged change has a pencil — inline edit fixes the change type, item type, location, from-location, and notes after the fact. A removal tagged Added doesn't have to stand, and don't delete-and-retype either — fix the line and the thread stays intact. Edits show on Tech Lighting's link too.

3

Use the discussion thread on a change

Tech Lighting asks, crew answers — with photos and files, right on the change. That thread replaces the email chains where this stuff used to get lost. Questions that aren't about one change — schedule, whole-plan stuff — go in the job-level thread, same photo and file attach.

4

Green "Incorporated" = the loop is closed

When Tech Lighting folds a change into the plans and the quote, the line goes green. A change without that line isn't done — it's just written down.

Why the log matters

An unlogged lighting change is a keypad that's wrong at trim and a quote that's wrong at billing. If a load moved and there's no line for it, the plans are now fiction — the log line takes thirty seconds.

Need the list in an email? One tap

The Copy button on the Changes From Original Plan header copies the whole room-by-room change list as formatted text — paste it straight into an email to the builder or Tech Lighting instead of retyping it. You'll see "Copied — ready to paste into an email" when it's on the clipboard.

Loads, keypads, panel loads

·

Keep the three lists true to the wall

Loads — every controlled load. Keypads — buttons per floor section. Panel Loads — what lands in the lighting panel. Status flows with the work; update as you pull and terminate. The Set baseline / Update baseline button on the Loads header snapshots the current loads as the original-plans baseline — the read-only loads share the AV programmer gets is locked to that snapshot, so refresh it once the list is real.

Migrating a list from a doc? Bulk paste

Next to Add Load and on each keypad section there's a Bulk paste — it takes tables copied straight out of Google Docs (tabs and all), bullets, or one-name-per-line, and it skips what's already on the list, so pasting the full doc twice can't double anything. If it detects nothing, it says so instead of quietly adding zero.

The one gotcha: LCP module numbers in a paste show in the preview but do NOT stick to the loads — the modal says so right there. After the paste, run the Assign to module flow to put loads on their modules. Paste gets the names in; assignment is its own step.

Quick answers

The job has no panelized lighting.

Flip the "no panelized lighting" toggle — it just pushes the tab to the end of the bar. Nothing is deleted; anything already typed stays.

A change I logged is still not green weeks later.

Poke it in that change's discussion thread — that's exactly what the thread is for. Green is Tech Lighting's move, not yours.

Whose tab is this — ours or Tech Lighting's?

Both, on purpose. Crew writes what happened on site; Tech Lighting answers, updates plans, and closes the loop. The tab is the meeting point.
