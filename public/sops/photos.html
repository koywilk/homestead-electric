Photos

Every photo on the job, one gallery — and how to shoot ones worth keeping

The 30-second version

Photos get attached where the work is — on a punch item, a change order, a return trip, an inspection, a question. The Photos tab rolls up nearly all of them into one gallery — punch, return trips, COs, plan files, Matterport, inspections, notes, daily updates. One catch: question photos stay on the question and don't show here. Tap any photo to go full screen — pinch, scroll, or double-tap to zoom — and Go to source → takes you to the tab it came from.

Attaching photos

1

Attach at the source, not "somewhere"

Use Add photo on the thing itself — the punch item, the CO, the RT, the question. The picker takes multiple photos at once, so grab the whole batch in one select. A photo attached to its item explains itself forever; a photo floating in a text thread is lost by Friday.

2

Shoot so a stranger could find it

Step back far enough to show WHERE it is, then close enough to show WHAT it is. Two photos beat one when the one makes someone guess the room.

The gallery

·

Find one photo in hundreds

On a photo-heavy job, don't scroll — use the search box up top (Search by filename, source, or uploader…) or tap the source chips (Punch, Return Trips, Change Orders, Plan files, and the rest) to hide or show whole groups. Each chip shows its count, and the header keeps an "N of M" tally so you know what's filtered out.

·

It's more than photos

The header says All photos & files for a reason — plan files, Matterport scans, and inspection reports show up as tappable cards here too. Tap Open file ↗ and it's one stop for the plans or a scan, no hunting through other tabs.

Question photos don't land here

Photos attached to a job question (or its answer) stay on the question — they never show in this gallery. If you attached to a question and can't find it here, it's not lost. Go back to the question to see it.

The viewer

·

Zoom works everywhere

Pinch on the phone, scroll on a computer, or double-tap to jump in and back out. Drag to pan while zoomed. Open-original is there when you need the full file.

·

The source chip tells you where it came from

In the viewer, each shot carries its source chip — that's a label, not a button; tapping it does nothing. To go there, tap Go to source → right next to it. That switches you to the source tab — punch, COs, RTs — and you scroll to the item from there. Arrows move you through the whole job's photos without backing out.

When photos pay for themselves

Before drywall. Panel labeled and torqued. The fix on any QC or inspector item. Anything a GC might question in six months. Thirty seconds of photos is the cheapest insurance the company buys.

Do / Don't

Do

  • Attach to the item the photo is about
  • Wide shot for where, close shot for what
  • Shoot walls before they close up
  • Photo the fix when you close a called item

Don't

  • Let job photos live only on your phone
  • Text photos that belong on an item
  • Shoot one blurry close-up and call it documented

Quick answers

Where did my photo go?

To the item you attached it on — and for almost every source it also shows in this tab's gallery automatically. The exception is question photos: those stay on the question only and never show here.

Uploaded a blurry or wrong-item photo — can I delete it?

Yes — go back to the item you attached it on and tap the × on that thumbnail. The gallery is read-only; you remove photos at the source.

Zoom isn't doing anything.

Tap the photo first so it opens full screen — zoom lives in the viewer, not on thumbnails. Then pinch, scroll, or double-tap.

Can I attach a PDF the same way?

Yes — non-photo files attach the same way and open in a new tab instead of the zoom viewer.
