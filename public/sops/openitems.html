Open Items

The job's note front door — jot it here, triage it into real work, sign off return trips

The 30-second version

Open Items is two things. On top: Job Notes — every note on the job, rough, finish, and general, in one list. Below: the job's return trips, with ✓ Sign Off right on the row. Jot scratch notes here, then flip on Triage and promote the real ones to Punch, Return Trip, Change Order, PO, or Question. Looking for everything still owed on the job? That rollup lives on the Activity tab — not here.

What's on this tab

·

Job Notes — all of them

Rough-scoped, finish-scoped, and general notes in one place. The Rough and Finish tabs each show only their own phase's notes — this is the one view with everything, and the fastest read when you're picking up a job you've never walked.

·

Return trips

Every RT on the job — each row shows its punch-item count, photo count, the assigned foreman, and the scheduled date. Unscheduled ones are the red flags. ✓ Sign Off and Reopen sit right on the row.

What's NOT here

Open punch, change orders, inspections, materials, and open questions never show on this tab — so an empty Open Items does not mean the job is done. The everything-outstanding rollup — "To do on this job" with COs, return trips, stranded QC, punch, inspections, Matterport, materials, and questions — is on the Activity tab. Check there before any handoff or "done" call to a GC.

Turn a note into real work

1

Jot it

Free-form scratch lines, right here. This is the job's capture front door — get it out of your head and onto the job before it rides home in the truck.

2

Flip on Triage

Then multi-select the lines that describe real work.

3

Promote

Pick what each one actually is: Punch, Return Trip, Change Order, PO, or Question. The line becomes that item.

4

Follow the chip

A promoted line gets a linked chip that jumps straight to the item it created. Tap it to finish the details on its home tab.

Don't let notes age

A note that describes real work shouldn't sit here as a note. Triage it the same day — a scratch line nobody promoted is work nobody scheduled.

Sign off a return trip

1

Check the row first

The punch-item count and photo count on the row tell you whether the trip is actually complete before you close it.

2

Tap ✓ Sign Off

Same write path as the Return Trips tab, so it counts everywhere. Fat-fingered it? Reopen puts it right back.

Quick answers

Where's the full list of everything still owed on this job?

The Activity tab — "To do on this job" rolls up COs, return trips, stranded QC, punch, inspections, Matterport, materials, and questions. Open Items is notes and return trips only.

Where do open questions live?

Not here. Work them on the Questions tab; the rough-plus-finish open-questions rollup is on the Activity tab.

Where do I check Stranded QC Items?

On the Activity tab's "To do on this job" list. If that row has anything in it, QC items lost their crew — make the return trip now.

Is this the same as the punch list?

No — punch lives on Rough/Finish. But a note that IS a punch item gets promoted from here: Triage it to Punch and follow the chip.
