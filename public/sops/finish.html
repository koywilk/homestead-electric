Finish Tab

Same system as Rough — with the QC gate and the homeowner at the end

The 30-second version

The Finish tab works exactly like Rough — stage status, punch by floor and room, Questions with tags, same rules. The difference is what's waiting at the end: finish is what the homeowner sees, the punch is device-by-device, the phase closes through a QC walk, not just a checkbox — and the final inspection gets recorded right on this tab, pass or fail. Everything in the Rough guide applies here; this one covers what's different.

Same as Rough — the short list

·

Status ladder, punch rules, questions, materials, notes, updates

Awaiting Start Date → Start Date Set → Scheduled → In Progress → On Hold → Complete. Close punch the day you do it (it feeds the Daily Update). Never delete rooms or lists. Tag every question with who it's for. Finish materials, POs, and the tally work exactly like rough's — including adding a part to the PO straight from a punch item. Daily Updates work the same too: closed punch rolls in on its own, you can add a manual update for anything else that happened, and the update can be emailed out. Same with Job Notes — jot things on the tab during the walk, then multi-select notes and promote them to punch, a return trip, or a PO. If any of that is fuzzy, open the "?" on the Rough tab.

What's different on finish

1

The punch is device-level — write it that way

"Master bath — 2nd sconce crooked" closes; "fix bathroom lights" doesn't. Trim, plates, devices, fixtures: one item per real thing, named by room, so anyone can close it without a tour guide.

2

Fixture picks and client answers land here

Finish questions carry the designer / homeowner answers — fixture picks, trim colors, mounting heights. Check the amber Incoming from Links panel when you open the job; an unread answer is a wrong install waiting to happen.

3

The phase ends with a QC walk

Finish doesn't close on the last punch check — it closes when the finish QC walk comes back clean (or its items are fixed). Get QC scheduled before the crew rolls off, not after. The QC tab's "?" has the full walk guide.

4

Leave-it-clean photos

Finish is the phase where photos earn their keep — panels labeled, rooms trimmed, anything the GC might question later. Thirty seconds of photos beats a return trip.

The finish stage status carries the handoff

"Finish Complete" tells the office to line up QC and the close-out — flip it when the work is actually done. And the app holds this gate itself: with finish punch or QC items still open, both the Complete status and the 100% stage setting refuse with a "Cannot mark Finish as complete" warning until the open items are cleared. Open QC-walk items count against it too, not just your finish punch.

The final inspection lives on this tab

1

Set the target date, record the result

The FINAL INSPECTION block sits right in the Finish stage section. Put the target date in as soon as it's known, and when the inspector leaves, hit Pass or Fail — same day, either way. Every result snapshots into the attempt history, so the office sees exactly what happened and when. Don't leave the office blind on the job's biggest gate.

2

Failed? List the items, photo the report

A fail gets a failed-items checklist — one line per thing the inspector called — plus photos of the inspection report. Write it standing there, while the inspector's words are fresh.

3

One tap turns the fail into a return trip

The → Create Return Trip button takes the open failed items and builds the return trip for you — scope reads "Failed final inspection items." Hit it before you leave the site, so the fix gets scheduled instead of remembered.

Do / Don't

Do

  • Write punch device-by-device, by room
  • Read Incoming answers before you install
  • Get the QC walk scheduled before roll-off
  • Record the final inspection result the day it happens — pass or fail
  • Photo the finished rooms and labeled panels

Don't

  • Try to force Finish Complete past open punch or QC items — the app refuses
  • Install a fixture with an unread answer sitting in the panel
  • Leave QC for "whenever" — it's the gate, not a formality

Quick answers

Rough punch is still open — does it block finish?

It doesn't block you from working, but it shows on the Job Activity tab's to-do list and as an open count on the phase tabs — it reads as unfinished work on the job. Close what's real, flag what's stale.

The QC walk called items on my finish work.

If you're still on site, the list is yours — close the items like punch. If the crew's rolled off, it becomes a return trip. That call gets made at the walk.

Homeowner asked for a change while I'm trimming.

That's a Change Order, before the work — the Change Orders tab's "?" has the whole flow. Don't eat scope on finish.
