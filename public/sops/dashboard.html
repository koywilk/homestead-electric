Homestead Electric · Tab Guide

Dashboard

One live screen over every job (what happened, what's coming, how the work is holding up, who is on it) plus what your Claude automations produced

The 30-second version

The Dashboard is the office page at homestead-electric.vercel.app/dashboard. It is not a tab in the app, and it opens for any admin's login (Koy, Josh, Brady, Justin). It reads the same jobs, team and My Day tasks the rest of the app already has, so it is live: when someone closes a punch item or logs an inspection, the numbers move. It follows the Residential / Commercial switch like every other screen. The page wears the Homestead brand (v562): the longhorn and the slab wordmark at the top, a warm off-white page, white cards, and the copper from our purchase-order form for the active tab and buttons. Needs you at the top of the Overview is the short list of decisions only you make; the rest of the page is what is slipping. Tap any line, card or job to see the list behind it. The Goals tab (v562) puts the Team Goals sheet beside today's numbers, and Job Costs (v564) splits every job's hours, materials and margin into rough and finish across every cost center.

Only as good as what gets logged. Every number comes from what the crew enters in the app: punch check-offs, inspection results, daily updates, change order statuses, dates on the job card. A job with no dates or no logged results shows up as "Needs a date" or simply isn't counted.
Opening it

Go to homestead-electric.vercel.app/dashboard. It asks for your PIN like the app does. On a phone, use Share → Add to Home Screen while on that page: the icon is called Dashboard and opens straight to it, separate from the crew app's icon.

Tap anything

Every job opens a panel with everything open on it (punch, change orders, return trips, inspections, open questions, plans waiting, dates, recent activity) and an Open in app button. Every number card opens the list behind it, every person opens their tasks and jobs. The row under the tabs picks one foreman (or No active foreman), one coordinator (the job's own, else its foreman's book; or No coordinator), switches between 7, 14 and 30 days, or finds a job by name, number, address or GC. The day buttons only change the Overview chart and the Activity tab, so they go grey on the other tabs. On a phone those sit behind one Filters button. The small ? opens this guide.

Four buttons that do something

Everything else only reads. These four act, and each one asks first: File it and Not ours on a plan waiting to be filed (Automations), Ask <foreman> to import on an appliance job (adds one My Day task on that job), and Run now on the Wednesday Lead Meeting prep and Friday Strategy prep cards (makes the real doc now).

The tabs

1

Overview

Needs you (only when something is waiting): plans waiting to be filed, time-off requests to decide, your urgent My Day tasks, crew questions not answered, jobs with no active foreman (and why: no foreman set, a deactivated team member, an outside contractor), QC walks that need a walk, automated docs that didn't run, and Closed in Simpro — close it here?: jobs Simpro has invoiced in full that are still open in the app. Those jobs no longer count as active, in open punch, quiet jobs, no-foreman jobs, crew questions or needs-a-date; each row says what is still open on it. Open each one and mark it finished. In Jobs with no active foreman each job shows Simpro's technician; tap Reassign to … to make that person the foreman (it asks first, they get a push, and Undo puts it back). Each line opens its list; a time-off request or task opens straight on My Day in the app. Below that: active jobs (with the count per stage under the bars), inspections in the last 30 days (and how many passed their target with no result logged), open change orders split by state (to send, waiting on the GC, approved, scheduled, on finished jobs), and open punch. Before noon the greeting reports yesterday. The chart is everything logged per day. Needs attention lists the chases worst first. There is no Ready to invoice line any more: invoicing comes from Simpro. Every weekday morning the Simpro sync marks a job invoiced (in full at 99.5%), marks a job Simpro has closed out as complete here, and completes the approved change orders on a job invoiced in full. Changed by the Simpro sync this week in Needs you lists what it did; a job's card shows each change with Undo (it won't redo one you undid). Change orders waiting on the GC 60+ days show their Simpro quote $ (each quote counted once). An approved change order on a job Simpro has billed in full says Billed — mark done? instead of waiting to be booked (the sync clears these each morning). Most activity names the busiest job with its open questions and punch. Foremen has one card per foreman, worst first, each with the jobs that are quiet 5+ days, return trips past their date and inspections not logged; the header says how many posted today.

1b

Jobs

Every active job in one table: foreman, where the work is, open punch, open change orders, next date and the last work logged. Where it is goes by the crew's rough and finish status. A rough whose inspection failed with no pass since shows Rough inspection failed with "re-inspect not booked" or the new date, not "Rough done". On hold or awaiting a date, the row shows what it is waiting on (from the line the crew fills in under the status). The change-order cell splits to send and waiting (red when one is still to send). Next date says Rough insp. not called in red at 85% rough with no target, and goes red when an inspection is booked within a week on a job that isn't ready. Chips: Quiet 21+ d (underway, nothing logged in three weeks) and Finished, COs open. A job with a plan waiting to be filed says so under its name.

2

Activity

Everything logged on the jobs, newest first. Rows that only carry a date (return-trip sign-offs, change orders from Simpro, photo batches) sit at the top of their day under Also today, change orders first. A run of one person's punch or questions on one job folds into one row ("Louis · 14 punch closed, 6 punch added · 8:55–10:19 PM"); tap it for the rows. Show 200 more pages further back. Cards: everything logged, punch closed against added, open questions (oldest first) and failed inspections out of those logged; a failed one says whether a re-inspection is booked. "Daily updates" means the foremen's daily and status updates only.

3

Schedule

The next 7 days as columns. Starts name the foreman (red No foreman when blank) and say Scan not done when the pre-drywall scan isn't logged. Past their date splits inspections from return trips, oldest first, each saying how many days late. Next 30 days shows scans and return trips both ways: dated, and not booked or past their date. Needs a date is the same list on every tab: jobs on the board with no next date, sorted by the least recent work, with the GC's needs-by window (Upcoming-tab jobs with no date are counted apart). Each rough or finish start in Starts (and in a job's Coming up) has Move / unconfirm: pick the new date, say whether the customer or GC confirmed it, or just unconfirm it; it saves on the job with Undo. Scans due show the rough %, whether one is booked, and red when the rough is done or the finish starts soon.

4

Quality

First-pass is green at 90% or better, pink 75–89%, red below, with how many failed the first time. Return trips are counted one way on every tab (open · past date · booked · to book · no date), late first. Inspections next 7 days shows which have a QC walk logged before them. Punch on jobs near final lists the punch that blocks a final. Failed inspections shows one row per job and phase with what happened since (passed, re-inspect booked, or not re-booked) and, when the Fail was tapped before the items were typed, "No correction list — ask the foreman". Punch by foreman shows jobs and punch per job, pink when open beats closed.

5

People

"On the app today" leaves out people on approved time off and people not seen in 30 days; tap it for who isn't on. Team pulse lists quiet people first (a foreman or lead on an active job with nothing logged in 3+ days, shown red) with their title and jobs. "Last on a job" is when they last opened or logged on a job; app opened in the device list is when their phone last opened the app. On the app, no job touched is the call list: phones that opened the app today whose owner hasn't touched a job in 3+ days. Time-off requests show what that person has dated in those days and who else is off; tap one to decide it on My Day.

6

Automations

Automated docs: one tap opens each doc or folder, and each card says made Tue 6:02 AM in green, did not run Tue 6:00 AM or the error in red, or "could not list the folder". The three meeting pulls land in one Drive folder, Meeting Prep: Monday Scramble prep (Claude routine, Mon 3:00 PM), Wednesday Lead Meeting prep (Tue 4:00 PM) and Friday Strategy prep (Fri 6:00 AM — Notes, next week's Scheduled / Need to be scheduled / Starts as small trees with a Plan line under each job, dates that moved, the calendar, people, GC and office blocks, Coming up with QC to schedule; a new doc each week that Koy pastes into Josh's meeting notes, nothing is pushed). The Wednesday and Friday cards have Run now. The Friday one makes a "TEST — …" doc, and right after it finishes the Friday card says Latest: TEST run and opens that doc, until Friday's 6:00 AM run makes a newer real one. The Friday Packet and the Tuesday 6 AM Lead Meeting Prep are gone. Plan Intake: a plan stays Waiting until it is filed, dismissed or matched, not just seen. Tap one to file it right there (best guess filled in, with a second guess from the sender's past files) or to say Not ours. A plan the Routine couldn't place gets another look once its new job or quote shows up. Appliance Loads: "To check in Home Runs" (not in plus unconfirmed) and Specs to confirm (amps assumed as typical). A line lists jobs in rough with no appliance package yet. Each job row names the foreman; tap it for Ask <foreman> to import.

7

Goals

The Team Goals 2026–2027 sheet (Koy's copy of Josh's), read when the tab opens, with today's number beside each goal. Our goal is the mission line as typed. 2027 scoreboard: one card per measure with Sheet says, Simpro today (or App today), the 2027 goal, a bar and a pill: At goal, Moving, Off track, Not tracked yet (needs a cost-center read from Simpro or a hand count) or No Simpro pull yet. Open How it is counted on a card for the rule. Simpro numbers follow Josh's Builder Scorecard rule: every job with an invoice dated this year that is fully invoiced with costs posted; margin is total net profit over total contract (not a per-job average), hours are total used over total estimated, each foreman is Simpro's technician on the job, days to collect is issue-to-paid on this year's paid invoices with the open book beside it. That is on purpose a different rule from the Scoreboard tab, so the two pages do not show the same margin. Rest of 2026: the sheet's AREA · GOAL · WHO · BY rows; the pill is what is typed in the sheet's column F STATUS (Not started / On track / At risk / Done), and the app adds a due flag from BY ("Due in 8 d", "Overdue"). Tap a row to open the sheet on it; edit there, then Refresh here. 2027 by quarter marks the next quarter. Measured by the app, not on the sheet yet (folded) lists what this page already tracks (first-pass inspections, change orders waiting 60+ days, quiet jobs, open questions, return trips, failed inspections not re-booked, plans waiting): add a scoreboard row in the sheet that names one and it moves up into the cards. Pull from Simpro now (admins) runs the nightly pull on demand; the right rail says when it last ran and turns red past two days. Nothing on this tab writes to the sheet, to Simpro or to a job.

8

Job Costs

Rough and finish, counted the way the job was sold. Crews are scheduled and materials are ordered on Base → Rough In, so that one cost center carries every hour while the extras' estimates sit on their own cost centers. This tab counts every cost center on the job — the base bid and every change order — and splits each one into rough, finish and untagged item by item: an item ending (Rough In) goes to rough, (Finish) to finish; an item with neither follows its cost center's type (Residential (Rough In) / (Finish)); a cost center with no phase (Change Order, Generators) is Untagged. Simpro's total for each cost center is the budget; the items only decide the split, and a credit comes off its phase. What was spent is recorded per cost center, so it splits the same way as that cost center's estimate; spent dollars include committed (ordered, scheduled), the way Simpro figures margin. Pick Hours, Materials, Labor $ or Margin, and All jobs, Active or Invoiced. The boxes show jobs over estimate and used vs estimate for rough and finish; on Margin, the whole job (on jobs at punch list, ready to invoice or invoiced), rough (jobs past rough) and finish, each weighted (total sell less total spent, over total sell — big jobs count more) with the plain average of each job's margin smaller beside it, and how many are under 15%. Every job lists rough and finish side by side, worst first; Est (all) is the estimate from every cost center, not just the base one. Tap a job to see its cost centers and how each splits; Open job opens it in the app. Untick Count to leave a job out: a What-if bar names what is off and the boxes and the foreman list leave it out (Turn all back on to undo; nothing in Simpro or the app changes, and it resets when you leave). By foreman / By lead (above the job table) adds up each person's jobs; tap a name and the boxes become theirs, with the company number under each. The foreman is the one on the Job Board, counted only when titled Foreman on Team Members — a jr foreman, lead or office person named in the foreman field shows as No foreman (name, why); fix the job's foreman field to move it. This is not the Goals tab's rule (Simpro's technician), so the two tabs can credit a job to different people. The numbers are pulled from Simpro every night (2:15, 3:15 and 4:15 AM; read-only); Pull from Simpro now (admins) runs it on demand. Nothing on this tab writes to Simpro or to a job.

Tip

The tab you were on is remembered on that device, so the Dashboard opens where you left it.

Questions

It says "This page is private."

The page only opens for login of an admin on Settings → Team. Pick an admin on the PIN screen, or open the app instead. Each admin sees the same page; "Your urgent My Day tasks" is your own, and Koy's bonus tracker and 90-day plan show only for Koy.

Where do the dollar amounts come from?

From Simpro, once a day on weekdays: the job's total including tax. Change orders carry no amount in the app, so a change-order line shows the job's value as a guide to which call is worth the most.

A job says "No active foreman" but it has a foreman.

The name on the job isn't an active foreman on Settings → Team (deactivated, an outside contractor, or Paul / TBD). Reassign the job, or fix the person's record in Settings → Team.

A person shows "Not seen in 30 days" but they use the app.

The dashboard sees what people log on jobs and when they open a job card. Someone who only reads My Day and never opens a job or logs anything won't show as active. Their phone still shows under "app opened".
