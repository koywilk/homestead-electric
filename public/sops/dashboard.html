Homestead Electric · Tab Guide

Dashboard

One live screen over every job (what happened, what's coming, how the work is holding up, who is on it) plus your Claude Command Center

The 30-second version

The Dashboard tab is a read-only office view. It only shows for people who have the Dashboard tab box ticked in Settings → Team → Tool access (Koy, for now). It reads the same jobs, team and My Day tasks the rest of the app already has, so it is live: when someone closes a punch item or logs an inspection, the numbers move. It follows the Residential / Commercial switch like every other screen. Tap any job row or card to open that job. Nothing on this screen changes a job.

Only as good as what gets logged. Every number comes from what the crew enters in the app: punch check-offs, inspection results, daily updates, change order statuses, dates on the job card. A job with no dates or no logged results shows up as "Needs a date" or simply isn't counted.
Its own page

Open homestead-electric.vercel.app/dashboard for the dashboard by itself, full screen, without the app's tabs. It asks for your PIN like the app does. On a phone, use Share → Add to Home Screen while on that page to keep it one tap away. Tapping a job there opens it in the app.

The tabs

1

Overview

Active jobs, inspections passed in the last 30 days, open change orders (and how many are waiting on the GC), and open punch items. The chart is the number of updates logged per day for the last two weeks. Busiest job is the job with the most updates today (or this week when today is quiet). Foremen today shows each foreman's active jobs, open punch and updates per day for the last week. On the right: this month's calendar with every dated start, inspection, QC walk and return trip, and the next 14 days of scheduled work.

2

Activity

Everything logged on the jobs in the last 30 days, newest first: punch items added and closed, change orders added and moved, inspection passes and fails, return trips, daily and status updates, and photo uploads (one line per person, job and day). Use the filter buttons or type a job, person or word to narrow it. Team pulse on the right shows who has been on the app today.

3

Schedule

The next 7 days as columns, with starts (orange), inspections (green), QC walks (blue), Matterport scans (purple) and return trips (red). Starts show projected until the date is confirmed on the job card. On the right: anything past its date, jobs that need their next date, and Matterport scans that are due (rough at 85% or more, finish not started).

4

Quality

First-pass counts each job's first rough and first final inspection: did it pass the first time. The chart shows that rate by month against a 90% line. Failed inspections lists the latest fails with the items to fix. Punch by foreman compares open punch with items closed in the last 30 days. On the right: open return trips and open QC walks.

5

People

Who has been on the app today, open My Day tasks (and how many are urgent), time-off requests still waiting, and how many phones are on the latest version. Updates by person is a 7-day grid of how much each person logged. My Day load is open tasks per person. On the right: the time-off requests (decide them on My Day as usual) and the devices that are behind on updates.

6

Command Center

Your Claude automations (Routines) and the Claude sessions that aren't finished, so you can check them from your phone. Needs you are sessions waiting on an answer, Ready to review have work to look at; tap one to open it in Claude. The right side lists every automation with its schedule and whether its last run worked (green), failed (red), hasn't run (gold) or is off (gray), and flags any name set up twice. An hourly Claude routine copies this list in; the line under the title says how fresh it is.

Tip

The tab you were on is remembered on that device, so the Dashboard opens where you left it.

Questions

I don't see the Dashboard tab.

It needs the Dashboard tab box ticked for you in Settings → Team → Tool access. Tick it, then the tab shows right after My Day.

Why is there no money tab?

Billing totals and what GCs owe live in Simpro, not in this app. Change order dollar amounts aren't stored on the job card either, so the dashboard counts change orders instead of adding up dollars.

A photo count looks low.

Only photos that carry the time they were taken are counted. Older uploads without a time stamp still show on the job, just not here.

A person shows "Not seen in 30 days" but they use the app.

The dashboard sees what people log on jobs and when they open a job card. Someone who only reads My Day and never opens a job or logs anything won't show as active.
