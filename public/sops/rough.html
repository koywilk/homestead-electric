Rough Tab

The rough phase's home: stage status & %, punch list, questions, job notes, daily updates, 4-way inspection

The 30-second version

Everything about the rough phase lives on this tab. Keep the stage status and stage % honest (that's what the whole company plans off), work the punch list by floor and room, put anything you can't answer into Questions with a tag, jot the rest in Job Notes, and record the 4-way inspection when it happens. If it's true on this tab, the office never has to call you to ask.

The stage status — keep it honest

StatusWhat it tells everyone
Awaiting Start Date We don't have a date from the GC yet.
Start Date Set GC gave a date — it's on the pill.
Scheduled Crew is assigned for that date. Shows in the Crew Planner.
In Progress Crew is on it. (The CO "Mark Work Completed" button does NOT key off this — it shows when crew is on the job in today's Crew Planner.)
On Hold Stopped — weather, GC, materials. Say why in a note or update.
Complete Rough is done. QC and inspection ride on this being real.
Why it matters more than it looks

Forecast, Crew Planner, the Huddle, and half the office's planning read this one pill. A job sitting on "Scheduled" while the crew is mid-pull tells everyone the wrong story. Flip it the day it changes.

The stage % is the real planning signal

Right next to the status sits the Rough Stage % selector (0–100%). The Forecast reads the % — not just the pill — so move it as the work moves. It also does real work on its own: any % above 0 flips the status to In Progress for you, 80% fires the Schedule-QC task, 85% fires ready-to-invoice, and 100% sets the status to Complete (blocked while punch is still open). A pill kept honest with a stale % still leaves the company planning off bad numbers.

The punch list

1

Find your floor, then your room

Punch is organized Main / Upper / Basement, then by room. Work your room's list top to bottom.

2

Close items as you finish them — same day

Check the box the day the work happens. Closed items roll into the day's Daily Update automatically, so closing punch IS your paperwork.

3

Blocked? Say so on the item

If you can't close it — waiting on another trade, missing material — note that on the item instead of leaving it silently open. An open item with no note reads as "nobody got to it."

Rolling off the job with it still open? Convert the item to a Return Trip so it gets scheduled instead of forgotten. The row keeps a chip for its linked trip — tap it to jump straight there.

4

Photo where it proves the work

Attach a photo on anything someone else will want to see closed — inspector items, GC callouts, anything behind drywall soon.

5

Hand out work by assigning items to a name

A punch item can be assigned to a person — that's how a foreman splits up the list. Everything assigned to you collects under the Assigned tab in the foreman view, across all your jobs, so check it before you pick a room at random.

Never delete a room or a list

Mark items done instead. Deleting has destroyed lists before, and the office relies on the history. If a room genuinely doesn't apply, tell the office — don't clean it up yourself.

Share the list — don't screenshot it

The punch list can go out to the GC as a live share link — filter it first so they only see what's theirs. And it works both ways: items the GC adds land in their own external section on this tab, so rows you didn't write aren't a glitch — they're the GC talking to you.

Questions — get answers without a phone tree

·

Hit something you can't answer? Type it in, tag who it's for

The Questions section here is the same system as the Questions tab: add the question on the right floor, tag the person it's for, and it lands on their live link in seconds. Their answer comes back to the amber Incoming panel. Full details are in the Questions tab's own "?" guide.

Job Notes — write it down while you walk

·

Anything that comes up in the field goes in Job Notes

The Job Notes section sits right in the Rough Stage section. Jot a line the moment something comes up — extra cans, a wall that moved, a part you're short. Notes here are rough-scoped, so they stay with this phase.

Then promote: multi-select the lines and send them where they belong — a CO, a Return Trip, a Punch item, or a PO. A note in the app becomes real work in two taps; a note in a text thread becomes a callback.

Materials & POs

·

The rough materials list lives here too

Materials for the phase, the POs against them, and the material tally. When a punch item needs a part, add it to the PO straight from the punch item — that's how "we're out of straps" becomes an order instead of a memory. If it's not on the list, it's not coming on the truck.

Daily Job Updates

·

Closed punch writes itself — the rest you type

The Daily Job Updates section on this tab is the day's story. Punch you closed today rolls in automatically. Anything beyond that — inspector came by, GC moved a wall, weather stopped the pull — add it as a manual update.

Then email it: the section can send the daily update to the office or the GC, so "what happened out there today" never turns into a phone call.

The 4-way inspection

·

Set the 4-Way Target date as soon as you have it

Put the inspection date in the 4-Way Target field the moment you know it. That's what lets the office plan around the inspection instead of finding out after the fact.

·

Record Pass or Fail the day it happens

Don't sit on a result — mark it the same day. Every attempt gets kept in the history, so a second or third try shows up as exactly that.

·

Failed? YOU type the list — nothing builds itself

A fail does not turn anything into punch on its own. Use the + Item button to type each thing the inspector called, one per line. Record a fail and walk away, and the re-walk list is empty.

Attach the inspector's report (photo or PDF) under Inspection Report too — it gets copied onto the return trip built from the fail, so the fix crew has it on site.

·

Tap "→ Create Return Trip" to make the fix trip real

Once the failed items are typed in, hit → Create Return Trip. That puts the open items on a new Return Trip's punch list — that's where the re-walk lives, not on this tab's punch. No tap, no trip.

A fail doesn't schedule itself

Recording a fail creates nothing by itself. Type the items, attach the report, and tap → Create Return Trip before you leave the driveway — otherwise the fail sits there with no fix trip behind it.

Do / Don't

Do

  • Flip the stage status — and move the stage % — the day it changes
  • Close punch the day you do the work
  • Note WHY on anything blocked; convert it to a Return Trip if you're rolling off
  • Tag every question with who it's for
  • Set the 4-Way Target date as soon as you have it
  • Record inspections same day, pass or fail

Don't

  • Delete rooms or lists — mark items done instead
  • Batch a week of punch closes on Friday
  • Leave the status on Scheduled while you're mid-pull
  • Carry answers in your head that belong in Questions or Job Notes
  • Expect a failed inspection to build its own list — you type the items and create the Return Trip

Quick answers

Who sees what I close here?

Everyone who needs to — closed punch rolls into the Daily Update, and the office dashboards read this tab live.

The GC moved the date. What do I touch?

Update the date on the status pill (or tell the office). That one edit moves the Forecast and Crew Planner with it.

An item was assigned to me specifically.

It also shows under the Assigned tab in the foreman view — every item assigned to you, across all your jobs. Knock those out first.

We failed the 4-way and I made the Return Trip. Where's the re-walk list?

On that Return Trip's punch list — the Return Trips tab, not this tab's punch. If you never tapped → Create Return Trip, there is no list yet.
