My Day

What's on you, what's on the office, and the + to ask for anything

The 30-second version

My Day is the screen you land on. Mine is everything on you: needs and tasks people gave you, punch items assigned to you, and (for foremen) the app's own reminders for your jobs. On Koy is what the office still owes on your jobs. Tap the + to ask for anything — two taps and it lands on the right person's list with a push. Tap the check when something is done. That's the whole loop.

Reading your list

1

Mine

Sorted for you: Overdue first, then Today, then This week. The colored stripe on the left says which. The small tag says where it came from — Need or Task someone gave you, Punch from a job's punch list, Auto from the app.

Tap the row to open the job right where the item lives.

2

On Koy

Things the office owes on your jobs — a QC walk, a start PO, a need you sent up. You can't check these off (they're not yours), but you can see they're in the queue instead of wondering. Tap the group name to open or fold it.

3

Done, Snooze, Undo

Tap the check when it's done. Tap the clock to snooze it 3 days, a week, or to a date you pick — it disappears and comes back that morning. Either way a dark Undo bar shows for ten seconds if you fat-fingered it.

Asking for something

1

Tap the + and type

On a phone it's the round + bottom-right. On a laptop it's the + Need button. Inside a job there's a + Need next to Refresh, and it fills the job in for you.

2

Check who it's going to, then Save

The To: chip is already set one step up the chain: a crew member's goes to their foreman, a foreman's goes to Koy. Tap it to send it to someone else — anyone in the company, including yourself. The other chips are optional: Need or Task, a job, and when it's due (Tomorrow is the default).

Save. They get a push, it shows on their My Day, and it shows under On Koy (or whoever) on yours so you can see it's still open.

Where things go

Everything you add here also shows on the office Needs page under the person it's on. There are no more "books" — a need is on a person, full stop.

Nothing on you?

Then Mine says All clear. That's the goal. Your jobs are still one tap away in the strip under the title, and Job Board / My Crew are in the header.
