My Day

What's on you, what's on the office, and the + to ask for anything

The 30-second version

My Day is the screen you land on. Mine is everything on you: needs and tasks people gave you, punch items assigned to you, and (for foremen) the app's own reminders for your jobs. On Koy is what the office still owes on your jobs. Tap the + to ask for anything — two taps and it lands on the right person's list with a push. Tap the check when something is done. That's the whole loop.

Reading your list

1

Mine

Everything on you, folded into categories — Tasks on me, Punch, Invoicing, Start POs, Change orders, Return trips, Scheduling, QC walks. Each line shows its count and an overdue badge; the category with the most urgent item sits at the top. Tap a category to open it; inside, rows run overdue → today → this week → later. Tasks someone put on you, and punch items assigned to you, are always here. If you're a foreman, the automatic office tasks (POs, invoices, scheduling) are not yours anymore — they sit with Koy until he pushes one to you, and then it shows up here like any other task with the job on it.

Tap the row to open the job right where the item lives.

Order inside a category: anything marked Urgent (red flag) first, then overdue → today → this week → later, and inside each of those the soonest due date first. Anything marked Low sinks to the bottom. A category with an urgent row in it jumps to the top of the list.

1b

Urgency

Every task has an urgency: Urgent, Normal, or Low. Set it on the + sheet (the Urgency field) or later with Edit. Urgent rows get a red left edge and a red URGENT tag, sort first, and the "assigned to you" push says URGENT in the title. Low means "when you get to it" — it sorts last and the morning chase leaves it alone.

2

On Koy

One line per job: "Koy has 4 things on this job." Tap it to see what he owes on your job — his tasks, his automatic ones, the QC walks and start POs. It's read-only. Need something from him on that job? + Add for Koy is right there, already set to that job and to him.

3

If you're Koy: Push

Every automatic task on every job lands on you. On each one: → Gage pushes it to that job's foreman in one tap, Pick person sends it to anyone. The row stays on your list as with Gage · 2d (Take back, Re-push). When they mark it done it comes back as done by Gage · verify — tap Done to clear it, or Send back if it isn't really done. QC walks and start POs show once, as the duty row.

4

Done, Snooze, Undo

Tap the check when it's done. Tap the clock to snooze it 3 days, a week, or to a date you pick — it disappears and comes back that morning. Either way a dark Undo bar shows for ten seconds if you fat-fingered it.

5

Reply and "waiting on"

Tap Reply on any task to post an update. Reply is a one-liner ("called the GC, no answer yet"). Waiting on… says who or what you're stuck on and, if you set a Back on date, snoozes the task until then — the person who asked sees waiting on Rexel quote · back 9/25 under it instead of nothing. Either way the other side gets a push and can reply from their own list. Tap the blue “N replies ▾” button (or the latest line) to open the whole thread, oldest first; Hide replies folds it back.

5b

Edit your reply

Typo? Open the thread and tap the ✎ next to one of your own replies. Fix the text and tap Save. It shows (edited) afterwards. Editing does not send another push. You can only edit your own replies, and not the automatic "voided" / "changed" lines.

6

Sent

Everything you asked someone else for, with their latest update under each one. Starts folded; the count on the header says how many are still open and how many are on hold. A reply from you wakes a task the other side snoozed.

Each Sent row shows a blue To: <name> ▾ pill — tap it to move the task to someone else on the spot — and a Nudge button that sends them a reminder push which opens their My Day right on that task. Nudge is the same manual reminder as everywhere else in the app, so it respects their "manual reminder" notification setting.

7

Sent · finished

A folded group right under Sent, showing things you asked for that someone else finished in the last 30 days — newest first. If you sent it and closed it yourself, it's in your own Done instead; a doc only shows in one place, never both.

Changing or cancelling something you sent

1

Who can

The person who sent the task (or made it for themselves) sees Edit and Void under it, in Sent or in Mine. Koy (whoever runs the head board) can Edit or Void any open task. The person it's on doesn't get Void. They use Done or Snooze, or Reply to say it isn't needed.

1b

Move it to someone else

The quickest way: tap the blue To: <name> ▾ pill on the row (you see it on tasks you sent, tasks you made for yourself, and — if you run the head board — on any open task). A list of everyone in the company opens under the row; tap a name and it moves, with the usual "assigned to you" push. The automatic rows on Koy's board work the same way: → Gage is one tap to the job's foreman, Pick person ▾ opens the same list.

2

Edit

Tap Edit to change the wording, the due date, who it's to, the urgency, or the job ("No job" is allowed). Tap Save. The other person gets a Task changed push, and the thread shows what changed, e.g. changed: wording, due 10/3. If you only move it to a different person, the new person gets the usual Task assigned to you push. Nothing else is added.

3

Void

Tap Void, add a reason if you want ("duplicate", "GC cancelled"), then Void task. It leaves every list right away. You get the usual ten-second Undo bar. The other person gets a Task voided push with your reason. Void is not a delete. The task stays on record.

4

Where a voided task shows

For you (the sender): in Sent · finished, tagged VOIDED in red, with who voided it, when, and why. Tap Reopen to put it back. The other person gets a "Reopened" update. For the person it was on: in their Done, reading voided by <name>. They can't reopen it. Voided tasks never count as "done this week" in Team pulse.

Photos on tasks

1

Add a photo when you create a task

On the + sheet, tap 📷 Photo to pick one or more photos — take a new one with the camera or choose from your photo library — before you save. The chip shows 📷 N once you've picked some, with a small preview of each and an ✕ to drop one before saving. Save works the same as always; the photos upload right after, and a toast shows Uploading N photos… until they're attached.

2

Add or view photos on an existing row

Any row with photos shows small thumbnails under it — tap one to open it full-size. Tap the 📷 on your own tasks or ones you sent to add photos, from the camera or your photo library. A toast shows Uploading N photos… while they upload (the row's 📷 steps aside until they're done). Finished (Done) rows show their photos too, but read-only — no add, no remove.

3

Removing a photo

The person who added a photo, or the head, can tap its ✕ to remove it. A dark Undo bar shows for ten seconds — only the most recent remove can be undone, so if you remove a second photo the Undo for the first one is gone.

Search, views, and Select

1

Search

The search box at the top of My Day filters everything — Mine, On <head>, Sent, Sent · finished, Done, and the person groups — by task, job, or person. While you're typing, any group, category, or job line with a match opens on its own so you don't have to hunt for it; clear the box and everything folds back to normal.

2

Category / Job / Person

Next to search, a toggle changes how your list is grouped. Category is the normal view (Tasks on me, Punch, Invoicing, and so on). Job groups everything by job site instead. Person (Head of Residential only, or whoever's covering) shows one group per person — you first, then everyone with something open, including people who aren't on your board yet. Your choice is remembered on this device.

3

Select

Tap Select to turn your own Mine and Focus rows into checkboxes (rows on other people — On <head>, Sent, Done — can't be batched, so they don't get one). Tick a few, then Done, Snooze 3d, or Push to… to send them all somewhere at once. Rows that can't take the action are skipped and the summary toast says how many. Only the very last one is Undo-able, so double-check before you go. Tap Cancel to back out.

Focus today

1

Pin your must-dos

Tap the ★ on any Mine row to pin it into the Focus today strip at the top of the page — up to 3 at a time. A pinned row drops out of its normal category so it isn't listed twice; unpin it (tap the star again) to send it back. Try to pin a 4th and you'll get a nudge to unpin one first.

2

It follows you, and resets each morning

Your Focus picks are saved to your account, so they're the same on your phone and your laptop. Focus clears every morning — if something from yesterday is still open, the strip shows "From <date>: N still open · Re-pin" so you can carry it forward in one tap instead of losing track of it.

Hidden (stale) rows

1

Old automatic rows fold themselves away

Some rows nobody's going to act on anymore — a reminder on a job that's been archived, deleted, or is still just a quote; a redline walk over 45 days old; an automatic task or duty row over 60 days overdue — get pulled out of your list into a folded "N hidden (stale) · Show" strip at the very bottom. Tap Show to look at them; they're read-only there, no buttons. Nothing is deleted — if the job comes back to life the row comes back to normal too. Real tasks someone assigned to you never go stale this way; they stay in your list until you close them. Same for an automatic row that has a real task riding on it (one you pushed to someone, or one waiting on you to check it off) — it never hides.

Money never hides

Anything about getting paid stays in your list no matter how old it gets: ready to invoice, overdue invoices, material deposits, change orders that need to be sent, CO or return-trip work that needs merging or invoicing, and redline COs still owed. They only fold away if the job itself is archived, deleted, or still just a quote.

What's gone

The "In Between — Over 2 Months" and "Get Finish Start Date" reminders don't show up anymore. Koy gets finish dates when they come in, so the app no longer nags for them.

Hats: who gets what

1

Some categories route to a person, not to Koy

A few categories have a company "hat" — a job that's always one person's, no matter whose job it is. Right now: Invoicing, Change order quotes, QC walks, Redline walks, and Matterport scans. Whoever wears a hat sees that whole category on their own Mine list, company-wide — not just their own jobs. Invoicing means every billing reminder: ready to invoice, invoices overdue 5+ days, rough and finish material deposits, and CO work that's complete and needs merging or invoicing. Categories with no hat (Start POs, Job prep, every return-trip row including "complete, merge or invoice", approved-CO follow-ups) still land on Koy as head, same as always.

2

QC and redline walks are shared

QC walks and redline walks can have more than one hat holder. A shared row shows on every holder's board at once. QC rows have no Done button — the row clears on its own, for everyone, once the QC walk is logged on the job. A redline walk shows Walk done (with an Undo) while it's scheduled, and once the walk turns into a change order it moves to Write redline CO, which opens the Change Orders board.

3

If you're Koy: "With others"

A folded With others group at the bottom of your board shows the rows that routed away to a hat holder — read-only, so you can see it's covered without it cluttering Mine. Anything with no hat holder still lands in your own Mine as usual.

4

Covering, when Koy is out

Covering is for the Head of Residential only. When Koy is out, he can hand all of his hats and tasks to one person for a stretch, right on his own row in Settings → Team: pick who's covering and a "through" date. Everything that would've routed to Koy routes to that person instead, automatically, through that date — then it snaps back on its own the next day. Other hat holders (Invoicing, CO quotes, QC, Redlines, Matterport) don't have their own Covering control — if one of them is out, reassign that hat in Settings → Team instead.

5

The morning push

Weekday mornings at 6:45 you get a "☀️ Your day" push with a quick count of your overdue and due-today tasks, plus invoicing, CO quotes, and redline walks for hat holders. Nothing to report, no push. (QC walks and Matterport scans aren't in this count — scans have their own separate 8am reminder.)

Team pulse (head only)

1

A folded card, just for the head

If you're the Head of Residential (or covering for him), a Team pulse card shows on your board: "Team pulse · N people · M overdue." Tap it open to see one line per person — every active person in the company, including the ones with nothing on them — Open, Overdue, Oldest, and Done this week. A person's Overdue or Oldest turns red once it's worth a look (2+ overdue, or something sitting 7+ days).

2

Tap a name

Tap anyone with open work to jump straight into Person view on their group. A name with nothing open — just finished work this week — isn't tappable. A shared QC or redline walk counts for every hat holder on it, not just whoever's board it lives on.

Asking for something

1

Tap the + and type

On a phone it's the round + bottom-right. On a laptop it's the + Need button. Inside a job there's a + Need next to Refresh, and it fills the job in for you.

2

Check who it's going to, then Save

Under the text box is a row of fields, each with a little arrow: To, Urgency, Due, Job, Type, and Photo. Tap any of them to change it — the options open right underneath. To is already set one step up the chain (a crew member's goes to their foreman, a foreman's goes to Koy); tap it to pick Koy, yourself, your foreman, or anyone in the company from the list. Urgency is Normal unless you say otherwise. Due is Tomorrow unless you pick This week or a date.

Save. They get a push, it shows on their My Day, and it shows under Sent on yours so you can see it's still open.

Getting to it faster

1

You land here

Everyone in the company opens the app on My Day now — office included. Job Board is one tap away in the header.

2

The red number on the tab

The My Day tab in the header shows a red count: your tasks that are overdue, due today, or marked urgent. No number means nothing is pressing.

3

A push opens the task itself

Tap a task push ("assigned to you", "Task update", a Nudge, the morning chase) or its line in the bell inbox and My Day unfolds straight to that row, scrolls to it, and outlines it in blue for a few seconds so you don't have to hunt.

4

The morning chase

Weekday mornings, right after the "Your day" summary: if a task on you is 2 or more days overdue and you haven't replied on it in the last two days, you get a "Still open on you" push — every other day it stays overdue (2, 4, 6…). The person who sent it gets a "Still waiting on someone" push on day 5, 10, 15… Reply, snooze it, or mark it done and the chase stops. Snoozed tasks, tasks with no due date, and Low-urgency tasks are never chased. You can turn the chase off in Settings → Notifications ("Overdue task chase").

Where things go

Everything you add here also shows on the office Needs page under the person it's on. There are no more "books" — a need is on a person, full stop.

Nothing on you?

Then Mine says All clear. That's the goal. Your jobs are still one tap away in the strip under the title, and Job Board / My Crew are in the header.

QC walks (admin / manager)

The QC tracker that used to be its own QC tab now lives here, inside Mine's QC walks category (Residential mode). Open that category and your own QC rows come first, then All QC walks — the count, a need action pill (failed, past due, needs scheduling), a scheduled pill, and the full list bucketed the same way as before: Failed — needs return, Past due — log result, Needs scheduling, Scheduled, Has QC items, Passed / done. Each row still has Schedule (pick a date — it marks the walk scheduled in the app and opens Google Calendar prefilled) and the status select; tap the job name to open the job. Search filters the rows. The category stays on Mine even on a day with no QC row on you, so the tracker is always one tap away.

Commercial rows

In Commercial mode My Day shows the commercial jobs' rows. Each job in pre-con has one Phase n row on the board of whoever wears that phase's hat: Commercial pre-con (Brady and Justin) for phases 1–5 and 7–12, Commercial site coord (Zane and Abe) for phase 6; nobody wearing the hat → the Head of Commercial (Brady). Items left owed by a move-on get their own row until checked. A weekly Gear check row appears while any released gear hasn't been delivered, a red Gear late row when a promised ship date passes, Ufer before pour / Underground before slab when the footing or slab date is within a week and that phase isn't closed, and Ready to start — assign a foreman on the Head of Commercial once all 12 phases are closed. Push, snooze and Done work exactly like every other auto row.
