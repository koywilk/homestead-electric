Homestead Electric · Crew Guide

My Day

What's on you, what you're waiting on, and the + to ask for anything

The 30-second version

My Day is the screen you land on. It has four tabs: To do (everything on you, soonest first), Waiting on others (everything you asked someone for), Team (the head only), and Done. Each thing is one quiet row: a circle, a title, and one grey line. Tap the row for the buttons. Tap the circle when it's done. Snoozed things hide in a Snoozed fold until their day. Tap the + to ask for anything — two taps and it lands on the right person's list with a push. That's the whole loop.

The four tabs

1

To do

Everything on you, in one list, soonest first. This is where you land. The number on the tab is how many things are on you; it turns red when something is overdue.

2

Waiting on others

Everything you asked someone else for that is still open, in the order you should chase it. See "Waiting on others" below.

3

Team (Head of Residential, or whoever is covering)

One line per person with what they have open. Nobody else sees this tab.

4

Done

What got finished in the last 30 days.

Nothing shows twice

A task lives in one place. If it's snoozed it's in Snoozed, not also in the lane. If you pinned it, it's in Pinned, not also in the lane. If it's finished it's in Done.

Reading your To do list

1

Four lanes, soonest first

Overdue, Today, This week, Later. Overdue and Today start open, because that's what needs you now. This week and Later start folded; tap the header to open one. Every header shows how many are in it, plus small tags when something inside is urgent, working, or new. A lane with nothing in it disappears, except Today, which says Nothing due today.

Inside a lane the order is: anything marked Working on it first, then Urgent, then the soonest due date. Anything marked Low sinks to the bottom.

2

What's on you

Tasks and needs people gave you, punch items assigned to you, job questions assigned to you (Rough / Finish tab → Assign to — tap Answer on the row and the answer lands on the question in the job, or Reply to post in the question's discussion without answering it; the row shows the latest discussion line and N in discussion opens the whole thread), time-off requests waiting on you (Approve / Deny right on the row, same as the Time Off page; the person who asked gets a notification with the answer), and, for foremen, the app's own reminders for your jobs (POs, invoices, scheduling, QC walks). If you're a foreman, the automatic office tasks are not yours until Koy pushes one to you; then it shows up here like any other task with the job on it.

3

One row

Every row is quiet on purpose: a circle to finish it, the title, and one grey line under it. The grey line says the job, who it's from (or with someone, or automatic), what kind of thing it is, and when it's due. A blue dot before the title means it's new since your last look, or someone else just sent it to you. A red flag means urgent. A blue Working pill means someone is on it.

The job name is a link. Tap it to open the job. Tapping the rest of the row opens the row's buttons (next step).

4

Tap a row to see what you can do

Tap anywhere on a row and it opens. One row is open at a time. The main buttons are: Working on it, Reply / Note, Snooze, Open job, and whatever decision the row needs (like → Gage on an automatic task, or Approve / Deny on time off). Reply and Snooze open right inside the row, so you don't lose your place.

Tap More… for the rest: Mark urgent, Pin, Photo, Move to… (or Push to…), Edit, Void, and Nudge (on a Not started ask, Nudge sits right in the row instead). Only the buttons that apply to that row show up.

5

Swipe

Swipe a row right to finish it, left to snooze it. Same as the circle and the Snooze button, just faster.

6

Pinned

Open a row, tap More… then Pin to keep your must-dos for today at the very top, up to 3 at a time. The Pinned section only shows when something is pinned. A pinned row leaves its lane so it isn't listed twice; unpin it to send it back. Try a 4th and you'll be asked to unpin one first. Pins are saved to your account, so they match on your phone and your laptop, and they clear every morning. If something from yesterday is still open, a line under Pinned says From <date>: N pinned still open · Re-pin so you can carry it forward in one tap.

7

Urgency

Every task is Urgent, Normal, or Low. Set it on the + sheet (the Urgency field), with Edit, or with More… → Mark urgent on any row (it flips Urgent and back). That works on every kind of row, not just tasks people typed. The mark is shared, so everyone who sees that row sees it urgent. Urgent rows get a red flag, sort first (right after anything marked Working on it), and the "assigned to you" push says URGENT in the title. Low means "when you get to it": it sorts last and the morning chase leaves it alone.

8

If you're Koy: Push

Every automatic task on every job lands on you. Open a row: Note adds a comment that stays under the row (same box as a need), the circle clears it, → Gage pushes it to that job's foreman in one tap, and More… → Push to… sends it to anyone. The row stays on your list as with Gage · 2d (Take back, Re-push). When they mark it done it comes back as done by Gage · verify: tap Done to clear it, or Send back if it isn't really done. QC walks and start POs show once, as the duty row.

9

Working on it

Starting on a task? Open the row and tap ▶ Working on it. The row picks up a blue Working · just now pill and moves to the top of its lane. The person who sent it sees Working · your name on their Waiting on others tab (and on the Needs board). No push; it just shows where they already look. Tap it again to stop. It clears on its own when you check the task off, hand it to someone else, or it gets reopened; Undo after Done puts it back. If you post Waiting on… after starting, the row shows Waiting instead. Automatic rows (QC walks, invoicing, start POs) don't have it — only tasks and needs people send.

10

Done and Undo

Tap the circle when it's done (or swipe right). A dark Undo bar shows for ten seconds in case you fat-fingered it; the bar shrinks as the ten seconds run out.

11

Snooze, and the Snoozed fold

Open the row and tap Snooze (or swipe left). The choices — 3 days, a week, or a date you pick — open right inside the row. The row leaves your lane and goes into the folded Snoozed section at the bottom of To do, marked back Mon (or whatever day). It comes back to its lane that morning.

Changed your mind? Open Snoozed, then tap Wake now on the row and it jumps straight back to your list. This works on automatic reminders too: a snoozed reminder really stays out of the way until its day. One exception: a reminder someone finished that's waiting on you to check it (done by Gage · verify) never hides in Snoozed; it stays on your list.

12

Reply and "waiting on"

Tap Reply / Note on any task to post an update. Reply is a one-liner ("called the GC, no answer yet"). Waiting on… says who or what you're stuck on and, if you set a Back on date, snoozes the task until then — the person who asked sees waiting on Rexel quote · back 9/25 under it instead of nothing. Either way the other side gets a push and can reply from their own list. Tap the latest reply line to open the whole thread, oldest first; Hide replies folds it back.

13

Edit your reply

Typo? Open the thread and tap the ✎ next to one of your own replies. Fix the text and tap Save. It shows (edited) afterwards. Editing does not send another push. You can only edit your own replies, and not the automatic "voided" / "changed" lines.

14

The bottom folds

Under the lanes, four folds that all start closed: Snoozed (above), Older reminders (see below), QC tracker (for the Head of Residential and whoever wears the QC walks hat; see the QC walks section at the bottom), and Office messages, everything the office has sent you, with unread, new replies, and need Got it counts on its header (see "Messages from the office").

Waiting on others

1

In the order to chase

Everything you asked someone else for that is still open. Each row names who it's on (To Gage on the grey line, or Unassigned), and the letter circle on the left is that person. Three sections, all open: Not started (oldest first, these are the ones to chase), Waiting (they're stuck, or they snoozed it; their waiting on… line shows under the row), and Working (they've started). A reply from you wakes a task the other side snoozed.

2

Chase it, or move it

On a Not started row, open it and Nudge is right there, one tap. On a Waiting or Working row it's under More…. Nudge sends the person a reminder push that opens their To do right on that task (it's the same manual reminder as everywhere in the app, so it respects their "manual reminder" notification setting). Move to… (under More…) moves the task to someone else on the spot. Edit and Void are in there too (see "Changing or cancelling something you sent").

3

On Koy (everyone except the head)

Underneath, a folded On Koy section: one line per job, like "Koy has 4 things on this job." Tap a job to see what he owes on it — his tasks, his automatic ones, the QC walks and start POs. It's read-only. Need something from him on that job? + Add for Koy is right there, already set to that job and to him.

Team (head only)

1

One fold per person

If you're the Head of Residential (or covering for him), the Team tab has one folded section per person on the team numbers, even if they have nothing open (you first, then A to Z). Each header shows a red overdue count whenever someone has anything overdue, and how many they finished this week. A person with nothing open says Nothing open right now when you open them.

2

Tap a name

Tap a person to see their rows. They're read-only here: look, but do it from To do or Needs. Rows that routed away to someone else's hat (see "Hats") show under that person too. A shared QC or redline walk shows under every hat holder on it. Voided tasks never count as "done this week".

Done

1

One list, last 30 days

Newest first. It holds what you finished, and also things you asked for that someone else finished (those say you asked). Tap Reopen to put one back where it's allowed. A doc only shows once, never in two places.

Changing or cancelling something you sent

1

Who can

The person who sent the task (or made it for themselves) sees Edit and Void under it, in Waiting on others or in To do (open the row, then More…). Koy (whoever runs the head board) can Edit or Void any open task. The person it's on doesn't get Void. They use Done or Snooze, or Reply to say it isn't needed.

1b

Move it to someone else

The quickest way: open the row, tap More…, then Move to… (on tasks and needs; on other kinds of row it says Push to…). You can do this on tasks you sent, tasks you made for yourself, and — if you run the head board — on any open task. A Move it to list of names opens under the row; tap a name and it moves, with the usual "assigned to you" push. The automatic rows on Koy's board work the same way: → Gage is one tap to the job's foreman, More… → Push to… opens the same list.

2

Edit

Open the row, tap More… then Edit to change the wording, the due date, who it's to, the urgency, or the job ("No job" is allowed). Tap Save. The other person gets a Task changed push, and the thread shows what changed, e.g. changed: wording, due 10/3. If you only move it to a different person, the new person gets the usual Task assigned to you push. Nothing else is added.

3

Void

Open the row, tap More… then Void, add a reason if you want ("duplicate", "GC cancelled"), then Void task. It leaves every list right away. You get the usual ten-second Undo bar. The other person gets a Task voided push with your reason. Void is not a delete. The task stays on record.

4

Where a voided task shows

For you (the sender): in Done, tagged VOIDED in red, with who voided it, when, and why. Tap Reopen to put it back. The other person gets a "Reopened" update. For the person it was on: in their Done, reading voided by <name>. They can't reopen it. Voided tasks never count as "done this week" on the Team tab.

Photos on tasks

1

Add a photo when you create a task

On the + sheet, tap Photo to pick one or more photos — take a new one with the camera or choose from your photo library — before you save. The chip shows Photo N once you've picked some, with a small preview of each and an ✕ to drop one before saving. Save works the same as always; the photos upload right after, and a toast shows Uploading N photos… until they're attached.

2

Add or view photos on an existing row

Any row with photos shows small thumbnails under it — tap one to open it full-size. Open the row, tap More… then Photo on your own tasks or ones you sent to add photos, from the camera or your photo library. On a computer you can also drag photos straight onto the task row. A toast shows Uploading N photos… while they upload (the row's Photo button steps aside until they're done). Finished (Done) rows show their photos too, but read-only — no add, no remove.

3

Removing a photo

The person who added a photo, or the head, can tap its ✕ to remove it. A dark Undo bar shows for ten seconds — only the most recent remove can be undone, so if you remove a second photo the Undo for the first one is gone.

Search, Group, and Select several

1

Search

Tap the magnifying glass at the top to open the search box. It filters every tab by task, job, or person. While you search, only the sections with a match show, already open (the QC tracker and Office messages folds step aside), and the numbers on the tabs show how many matches each tab has. Tap the magnifying glass again to clear the search and close the box; everything folds back to normal.

2

Group

The sliders button next to search opens the Group menu. It changes how the To do list is grouped: Time is the normal one (Overdue, Today, This week, Later). Job makes one folded section per job site. Type makes one per kind of thing (Tasks on me, Punch, Questions, Invoicing, Start POs, Change orders, Return trips, Scheduling, QC walks). Who sent it makes one per person who sent it to you (automatic reminders go under Automatic). The Job, Type, and Who sent it sections are A to Z and start folded; each header still shows its count and an overdue tag. Your choice is remembered on this device.

3

Select several

At the bottom of the Group menu, tap Select several… to turn your own To do rows into checkboxes (rows on other people — Waiting on others, Team, Done — can't be batched, so they don't get one). Tick a few, then Done, Snooze 3d, or Push to… to send them all somewhere at once. Rows that can't take the action are skipped and the summary toast says how many. Only the very last one is Undo-able, so double-check before you go. Tap Cancel to back out.

Older reminders

1

Old automatic rows fold themselves away

Some rows nobody's going to act on anymore — a reminder on a job that's been archived, deleted, or is still just a quote; a redline walk over 45 days old; an automatic task or duty row over 60 days overdue — get pulled out of your list into the folded Older reminders section at the bottom of To do. Tap it open to look. They're read-only there: no circle to finish and no Snooze. Open one and you can still add a Note or tap Open job. Nothing is deleted — if the job comes back to life the row comes back to normal too. Real tasks someone assigned to you never go stale this way; they stay in your list until you close them. Same for an automatic row that has a real task riding on it (one you pushed to someone, or one waiting on you to check it off) — it never hides.

Money never hides

Anything about getting paid stays in your list no matter how old it gets: ready to invoice, overdue invoices, material deposits, change orders that need to be sent, CO or return-trip work that needs merging or invoicing, and redline COs still owed. They only fold away if the job itself is archived, deleted, or still just a quote.

What's gone

The "In Between — Over 2 Months" and "Get Finish Start Date" reminders don't show up anymore. Koy gets finish dates when they come in, so the app no longer nags for them.

Hats: who gets what

1

Some categories route to a person, not to Koy

A few categories have a company "hat" — a job that's always one person's, no matter whose job it is. Right now: Invoicing, Change order quotes, QC walks, Redline walks, and Matterport scans. Whoever wears a hat sees that whole category on their own To do list, company-wide — not just their own jobs. Invoicing means every billing reminder: ready to invoice, invoices overdue 5+ days, rough and finish material deposits, and CO work that's complete and needs merging or invoicing. Categories with no hat (Start POs, Job prep, every return-trip row including "complete, merge or invoice", approved-CO follow-ups) still land on Koy as head, same as always.

2

QC and redline walks are shared

QC walks and redline walks can have more than one hat holder. A shared row shows on every holder's board at once. QC rows have no Done button — the row clears on its own, for everyone, once the QC walk is logged on the job. A redline walk shows Walk done (with an Undo) while it's scheduled, and once the walk turns into a change order it moves to Write redline CO, which opens the Change Orders board.

3

If you're Koy: rows that went to someone else's hat

Rows that routed away to a hat holder don't clutter your To do list. You can see that they're covered on the Team tab, under that person's name (read-only). Anything with no hat holder still lands in your own To do as usual.

4

Covering, when Koy is out

Covering is for the Head of Residential only. When Koy is out, he can hand all of his hats and tasks to one person for a stretch, right on his own row in Settings → Team: pick who's covering and a "through" date. Everything that would've routed to Koy routes to that person instead, automatically, through that date — then it snaps back on its own the next day. Other hat holders (Invoicing, CO quotes, QC, Redlines, Matterport) don't have their own Covering control — if one of them is out, reassign that hat in Settings → Team instead.

5

The morning push

Weekday mornings at 6:45 you get a "Your day" push with a quick count of your overdue and due-today tasks, plus invoicing, CO quotes, and redline walks for hat holders. Nothing to report, no push. (QC walks and Matterport scans aren't in this count — scans have their own separate 8am reminder.)

Asking for something

1

Tap the + and type

On a phone it's the round + bottom-right. On a laptop it's the + Need button. Inside a job there's a + Need next to Refresh, and it fills the job in for you.

2

Check who it's going to, then Save

Under the text box is a row of fields, each with a little arrow: To, Urgency, Due, Job, Type, and Photo. Tap any of them to change it — the options open right underneath. To is already set one step up the chain (a crew member's goes to their foreman, a foreman's goes to Koy); tap it to pick Koy, yourself, your foreman, or anyone in the company from the list. Urgency is Normal unless you say otherwise. Due is Tomorrow unless you pick This week or a date.

Save. They get a push, it shows on their To do, and it shows under Waiting on others on yours so you can see it's still open.

Getting to it faster

1

You land here

Everyone in the company opens the app on My Day now — office included. Job Board is one tap away in the header.

2

The red number on the tab

The My Day tab in the header shows a red count: your tasks that are overdue, due today, or marked urgent. No number means nothing is pressing.

3

A push opens the task itself

Tap a task push ("assigned to you", "Task update", a Nudge, the morning chase) or its line in the bell inbox and My Day opens on the right tab, unfolds straight to that row, scrolls to it, and outlines it in blue for a few seconds so you don't have to hunt (a snoozed task opens in Snoozed, something you asked for opens in Waiting on others). This works whether the app was closed or already open. Tapping the push also marks it read in the bell. If a push never shows up on your phone, the same notification is still waiting in the bell — the bell is the record, the push is just the tap on the shoulder.

·

Messages from the office

The office sends three kinds of message, and they sit in their own From the office group at the top of the bell with a colored tag, so they don't get lost between change orders and return trips. Tap one to read it, photos and files included. Only what still needs you stays open in the bell: once you tap Got it, open an announcement, or read a discussion's new replies, it folds into one ✓ N done line. Tap that line to see the newest five, or See all in My Day for the rest; that opens the Office messages fold at the bottom of To do.

Every message, kept: at the bottom of To do, the folded Office messages section (its header shows how many are unread, how many new replies, and how many need your Got it) holds everything the office has sent you, in tabs: Important, Announcements, Discussions. What still needs you is at the top of each tab; older finished ones sit behind Show N older. Tap any row to open it. Inside a message, the ‹ › arrows by the tag step to the newer or older message of the same kind, so you can read straight through without going back. You can tap Got it right on an Important row.

Announcement (blue): one-way news. Just read it. Important (red): tap the big Got it button so the office knows you've read it. Until you do, it stays pinned at the top of My Day, and you'll get a reminder the next morning. Discussion (purple): a group thread. Type in Reply to the group (you can add a photo) and tap Send reply. Every reply notifies everyone in the discussion.

Office only: to send one, open the bell and tap Send (foremen don't have the button). Pick What kind, then a group (Everyone, Office, Foremen, Leads & Jr. Foremen, Crew), or type names in Or pick people (each one shows as a chip you can remove; groups and names mix; See everyone opens the whole list). Write a headline and the message, and use Add photos or files (on a computer you can drag them onto it). Send me a test shows it on your own phone first; Send to N people asks once more before anyone's phone buzzes. Afterward it shows who got it on their phone and who only has it in their bell. Under Recently sent, tap Got it from X of N (Important) or Opened by X of N to see who has confirmed or opened it and when, and Remind the N who haven't pings only the ones still missing. The same list is on My Day under Office messages → Sent (office only), one tap away. Only the office can start a message or a discussion; everyone in a discussion can reply.

4

The morning chase

Weekday mornings, right after the "Your day" summary: if a task on you is 2 or more days overdue and you haven't replied on it in the last two days, you get a "Still open on you" push — every other day it stays overdue (2, 4, 6…). The person who sent it gets a "Still waiting on someone" push on day 5, 10, 15… Reply, snooze it, or mark it done and the chase stops. Snoozed tasks, tasks with no due date, and Low-urgency tasks are never chased. You can turn the chase off in Settings → Notifications ("Overdue task chase").

Where things go

Everything you add here also shows on the office Needs page under the person it's on. There are no more "books" — a need is on a person, full stop.

Nothing on you?

Then To do is quiet: Today says Nothing due today (or, if you've grouped by Job, Type or Who sent it, the card reads Nothing on your list.) and there are no red numbers. That's the goal. Job Board / My Crew are in the header.

QC walks (Head of Residential and the QC walks hat)

1

The QC tracker is a fold at the bottom of To do

The QC tracker that used to be its own QC tab now lives at the bottom of To do, in the folded QC tracker section, for whoever wears the Head of Residential or QC walks hat (Settings → Team → Company hats). It shows a need action count on the header. Nobody else sees it: a foreman only sees the QC rows that are actually on them, as ordinary rows in their lanes. If you switch the Group menu to Type, the tracker moves inside the QC walks section instead. Open it and you get All QC walks — the count, a need action pill (failed, past due, needs scheduling), a scheduled pill, and the full list bucketed: Failed — needs return, Past due — log result, Needs scheduling, Scheduled, Has QC items, Passed / done. Each row has Schedule (pick a date — it marks the walk scheduled in the app and opens Google Calendar prefilled) and the status select; tap the job name to open the job. While you search, the QC tracker fold steps aside (search only shows sections with a matching task).

Commercial rows

1

Commercial mode

In Commercial mode My Day shows the commercial jobs' rows. Each job in pre-con has one Phase n row on the board of whoever wears that phase's hat: Commercial pre-con (Brady and Justin) for phases 1–5 and 7–12, Commercial site coord (Zane and Abe) for phase 6; nobody wearing the hat → the Head of Commercial (Brady). Items left owed by a move-on get their own row until checked. A weekly Gear check row appears while any released gear hasn't been delivered, a red Gear late row when a promised ship date passes, Ufer before pour / Underground before slab when the footing or slab date is within a week and that phase isn't closed, and Ready to start — assign a foreman on the Head of Commercial once all 12 phases are closed. Push, snooze and Done work exactly like every other auto row.
