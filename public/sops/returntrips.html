Return Trips

A scheduled trip back for known work — with the list riding on the card

The 30-second version

A Return Trip is how we go back to a job for known, written-down work — QC items after the crew rolled off, an approved change order with nobody on site, something the GC needs done, a failed inspection, punch that got stranded, or anything else that needs a trip back. The RT card carries the scope, the item list, and photos. It gets a date, the work gets done, the items get checked off, the RT gets marked complete. Nothing rides on memory.

Where RTs come from

·

Made directly — anything that needs a trip back

+ Add Return Trip on this tab. The GC calls with something they need done, a warranty item surfaces, work turns up after the crew rolled off — you don't need a change order or a QC walk to justify the trip. Give it a scope, put the items on its list, get it a date. If it's billable work, pair it with a CO; if it's just work owed, the RT alone is the record.

·

A QC walk that wasn't clean

When a walk ends Passed with Items or Fail and nobody's on site, the walker picks Create return trip — the RT is built with the walk's items already on it.

·

An approved Change Order with no crew on site

Convert to Return Trip on the CO — scope, tasks, materials, and time pre-fill from the CO. From that moment the work lives on the RT; the CO grays out as history.

·

A failed inspection

Nothing spawns on its own. While a failed 4-way or final still has open inspection items, its card shows → Create Return Trip — tap it and the RT is built carrying "Failed 4-way inspection items" or "Failed final inspection items". Nobody taps it, no trip. Same as QC: return trips are made by choice, never automatically.

·

Punch items linked by hand

The punch linker attaches existing rough/finish punch items to an RT — for collecting stragglers into one trip instead of three.

·

Promoted from Job Notes

Field notes that turn out to be trip-back work don't get retyped. In Job Notes, multi-select the lines and hit Promote to Return Trip (the button counts the lines you picked) — they land on a brand-new RT or onto an existing RT's item list, your choice.

Every trip has a name on it

The ASSIGNED TO picker on the RT card puts a foreman or lead on the trip. Once it's assigned and still open, a Remind button sends that person a "Return trip reminder" push carrying the scope — the app can chase the trip so nobody has to.

Working an RT

1

Read the card before you roll

Scope, the item list, and the photos are the whole assignment. If something on it doesn't make sense, ask before the drive, not from the driveway.

2

Close items as you finish them

Check each item off on the RT. Linked punch items are the same item in both places — closing it on the RT closes the real thing, no double entry.

3

Photos where they prove the fix

Attach them to the RT. For QC-born items especially — the photo is what lets the office close the loop without another drive. If the RT has linked items, use the PHOTO GOES TO: chooser to drop the shot straight onto that item instead of the RT's general strip.

4

Sign it off

All items closed → fill in the name and date in the SIGN OFF box and hit the green Sign Off button (it stays disabled until both are filled). One tap marks the RT signed off and flips it to RT Complete. Don't just flip the status by hand — an unsigned RT keeps sorting with the active trips, and a QC-born job stays flagged until the RT is signed off.

Completing the RT is what fires the office's "Return Trip #N Complete — merge or invoice" task — an RT left open is money not billed. It doesn't touch the CO: a converted CO stays grayed as Converted to RT no matter what happens on the RT.

Send the trip, don't retype it

Chat, Text Trip, Email Trip, and Simpro buttons on the RT card send a formatted summary — scope, material needed, open punch items, and who it's assigned to. Whoever's driving out gets the whole list without anyone typing it twice.

The RT is a snapshot, not a mirror

An RT converted from a change order copies the CO at that moment. Edits on the RT don't flow back to the CO, and CO photos don't carry over — attach what the crew needs directly to the RT.

Do / Don't

Do

  • Get every RT a date — undated RTs are the ones that rot
  • Close items on the RT as you go, not from the truck later
  • Photo the fixes, especially QC items
  • Sign the RT off the day the work's done

Don't

  • Make a return trip for a job that still has crew on site — hand them the list instead
  • Do RT work without opening the card — the list is the assignment
  • Leave a finished RT unsigned — the merge-or-invoice task never fires and the work doesn't get billed

Quick answers

When is it an RT vs just doing the work?

Crew on site → do it now, no RT. Nobody on site → RT, every time. Same rule the QC prompt asks.

The RT has no date. Who schedules it?

Unscheduled RTs sit on the Forecast's Attention view under READY TO SCHEDULE — the office works that list. If it's urgent, say so; don't assume the date will find itself.

I signed off the RT — why does the CO still say Converted to RT?

That's correct, and it never changes. Completing the RT doesn't touch the CO — the grayed card is history. The billing moves through the RT's own "merge or invoice" office task, which fires when the RT completes. If something looks wrong, tell the office rather than editing the CO by hand.

A converted CO was a mistake.

The CO card has Undo Convert — it goes back to Approved. The RT it made stays and gets deleted separately.
