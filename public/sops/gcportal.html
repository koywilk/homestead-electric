The GC Portal

One link per GC — all their jobs and everything waiting on them, in one place

The 30-second version

Question links are per-person, per-job. The portal is per-GC: one link that lists every job that GC has with us and what's waiting on their answer, all in one page. Made by the office, sent to the GC's office once — as new jobs start with that GC, they appear on the portal by themselves. What the GC sends back lands in the requests inbox on the Contractors tab — nothing touches a job until you Apply it.

Link rules (same as every link): one portal per GC company · send once, jobs flow onto it automatically · revoke kills it instantly · everything the GC submits waits in the requests inbox until the office Applies it.

Creating one

1

Type the GC's name and check the job list

The form tells you how many matching jobs will be included — uncheck any to hide a job from this GC. Job filed under a different GC spelling? Use "+ include a job filed under a different GC spelling" to force it on without touching the job record.

2

Add contacts if you want email going out

"Contacts (optional)" on the form — those emails feed the notification and digest engine. Hit "Send test digest to me" first, so you know email works before a real GC sees anything.

3

Create portal link → copy and send it to their office

This is a company-level link — send it to whoever runs their jobs, once. New jobs with this GC show up on the portal without anyone doing anything.

What the GC sees and does

·

Their jobs, front to back

Each job shows rough/finish status and dates, return trips, Homestead's own QC-walk receipts, Matterport 3D links, CO counts, and the open questions tagged their way — co-branded. It's a full status page, not just a question list.

·

It's two-way

GCs can answer questions, suggest or confirm dates (finish start, Matterport, return trips), add items, message the crew, and attach photos or PDFs to what they send. They can also assign or change their own supers per job — that drives the super filter and per-super email routing. Everything they submit files into the requests inbox below.

Portal vs question link — when to use which

A single super on a single job → a named question link. A GC's office juggling several of our jobs → the portal, so they stop bookmarking five links. One difference in the flow: a question-link answer applies itself to the question; a portal answer waits in the requests inbox until you Apply it.

Membership is what gates the view

A portal shows the jobs that belong to that GC — which is driven by the GC name on each job. Keep Job Info's GC field clean and consistent, or a job can sit off their portal (wrong name) when it should be on it. The hide and force-include checkboxes on the link form override the name match when you need them to.

The requests inbox

·

Nothing touches a job until you Apply it

Everything a GC submits — answers, added items, date suggestions, messages, contact changes — files into the requests inbox on the Contractors tab (admin only). Apply lands an answer on the exact question it belongs to, or adds a punch item with a GC badge; attachments carry through. Dismiss what doesn't belong.

Work the inbox or answers rot

Portal answers do not apply themselves. A GC who answered on Monday looks ignored by Friday if nobody hit Apply. Make the inbox a regular stop.

Managing it

·

Edit beats recreate

Open an existing link and Edit it — contacts, branding, and hidden jobs all change in place. Same URL, nothing new to send.

·

Revoke is the kill switch — Reactivate is the way back

Revoking a portal link kills it immediately — the old URL shows nothing. When they're back in good standing, hit Reactivate: the same link comes back live with their jobs on it. No need to create and re-send a fresh one.

Quick answers

The GC forwarded the portal inside their office.

That's fine — it's a company link by design. If a specific person needs a tighter view, give them a per-job question link instead.

A job isn't showing on their portal.

Check the GC name on that job's Job Info — portal membership follows it. Or Edit the link and use "+ include a job filed under a different GC spelling" to force it on without touching the job record. While you're there, make sure the job isn't unchecked (hidden).

Do crews touch this?

No — the portal is created and managed office-side, and everything GCs submit goes through the office's requests inbox. Crews see an answer on the job only after the office Applies it.
