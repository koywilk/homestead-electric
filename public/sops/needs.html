Needs

Every open need company-wide, who it's on, and when

The 30-second version

Needs is the office view of every open need and task in the company. Mine shows what's on you; All shows everything. Each card says who it's on — change the To: to hand it to anyone and they get a push. Two lanes: Tomorrow and This week. Check it off when it's done; it moves to Done today and clears overnight.

Working the board

1

Mine vs All

Mine = needs on you. All = every open need company-wide, whoever it's on. Use All to see what the field is waiting on before the morning call.

2

Hand it to someone

The To: dropdown on each card is the whole roster. Pick a name and it lands on that person's My Day with a push. Pick Unassigned to put it back in the pool. The automatic office tasks on My Day work the same way — Koy pushes one to a person and it becomes a task on their day.

3

Plan, move, close

Type a plan in the box on the card (saves when you tap away). Move to week / Pull to tomorrow changes the lane. The check closes it; the x deletes it with a ten-second Undo — no confirm popup.

4

Adding one from here

The + Need button (or the round + on a phone) opens the same sheet as My Day. Type it, check the To: chip, Save.

Carried over

A red stripe and a Carried over pill means it's older than a day and a half. Either close it, move it, or hand it to someone who can.

Office worklist

Below the lanes is the folded office worklist: QC walks, start POs, and job prep the app derives from each job's stage. Those aren't cards you can delete — they clear when the job moves.
