Job Info

The job's basics — who, where, what stage, and who to call

The 30-second version

Job Info is the cover page: job name, address, the GC with their contact and phone, Simpro number, who's running it (foreman + lead), the Status Update, the Matterport scan links, and the access notes. When any of it is wrong — a new GC contact, a gate code that changed — fix it here, because every other screen in the app reads from this one. One thing that's NOT here: rough and finish status and their dates. Those are set on the Rough and Finish tabs.

What lives here

·

The basics

Job Name, Address, General Contractor, GC Contact, GC Phone, and the Simpro Job # (Simpro Quote # on a quote). The address is what maps and share links use — typos here follow the job everywhere.

Let Simpro do the typing

Enter the Simpro number and tab out — the app auto-fills the blank fields from Simpro (site address + site contact). The Pull button next to the number forces a refresh. It fills blank fields only and never overwrites typed text — so a wrong typed value won't fix itself. Clear the field, then Pull again.

·

Who's on it

Foreman and lead. This drives whose Crew Planner the job shows in and who the notifications go to.

·

GC contact + phone

The name AND number of the person to actually call — so nobody's texting around asking "who's the GC on this one?" After a Simpro pull, the GC Contacts list shows every contact on the GC — supers, billing, PM — with tap-to-call phone and email links. Tap a name to mark them "Running this job"; it drops their name and number into the GC Contact / GC Phone boxes (tap again to clear). Give contacts a label like Super or Billing — labels survive re-pulls.

·

Status Update box

A free-text status for the whole company — "needs a lift", "waiting on framer". It shows on every job card (Board, Forecast, Upcoming), stamped with who set it and when. Hit Clear when it's handled. If you learn something about the job that everyone should see at a glance, this is where it goes.

·

Matterport scans

The job's 3D scan links live here — add as many as you need with labels (+ Add), and Open jumps straight into a scan. Below the links sits the Scan Status dropdown with its date: "Scan Needs to be Scheduled" / "Scan Scheduled" / "Scan Complete". When rough hits 100% the status flips to needs-scheduled on its own and kicks off a task. The scan is the record of what's behind the drywall — at finish, open it here to answer "where does that wire run" without cutting holes. GCs see the scan status on their portal, so keep it honest.

·

Pre-Job Prep + Admin

The office's prep checklist lives here too — it ends in "✓ Prep Complete — Handed Off to Foreman", so a foreman can see whether prep finished before the crew rolls. Admin holds the job-account and pre-lien checkboxes, plus the "Temp pedestal on site" toggle with its ped # picker — flip it and pick the number when a pedestal lands on your site.

·

Access notes

Gate codes, lockbox, "dog in the yard", "park on the street" — the stuff that saves the first twenty minutes of a stranger's visit. If you learned it the hard way, write it down for the next guy.

Why this tab matters more than it looks

Every dashboard, packet, and share link starts from what's typed here. A stale GC number or a wrong address doesn't stay on this tab — it goes out in emails and handoffs with the company's name on it.

Quick answers

The GC gave us a new super.

If they're in the GC Contacts list, tap their name — it marks them "Running this job" and drops name + number into the GC Contact / GC Phone boxes. Not in the list yet? Type it. Either way, do it right then — the old number stops being called the moment it's replaced.

I need to move a rough or finish date.

Not on this tab. The pill in the job header here is read-only — it just shows where the job sits. Status and date windows are set on the Rough and Finish tabs.

Where do I put the gate code?

Access note on this tab — not a text thread, not your memory.

Whose job is it to keep this current?

Whoever learns the new fact first. It's a 20-second edit that saves everyone else the phone call.
