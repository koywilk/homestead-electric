Commercial Mode — Guide

The app has two divisions: Residential and Commercial. The RESI | COMMERCIAL switch in the header picks which one every screen shows. It only changes what you see on this device; it never changes a job.

The switch

Tap COMMERCIAL and the header turns teal, the tabs shrink to the commercial set (My Day, Job Board, Needs, COs, Job Start, Contractors, Safety, Forecast), and every board shows only commercial jobs, and the Job Board's Crew Schedule strip shows only the crew on commercial jobs that week (a person on a residential job that day simply is not listed). Tap RESI to go back to exactly what you had before. If a job is open when you flip, its card closes because it belongs to the other division. A push notification about a job in the other division flips the switch for you and opens the job.

Where you land

Settings → My Preferences → Land in picks the division the app opens in when you log in. The office can set it for a person under Team Members → Lands in. Most people land in Residential; the commercial crew can land in Commercial.

Which division a job is in

It comes from Simpro's Business Group on the job (Residential / Commercial / Multi Family) when the job is imported — the Simpro Inbox row shows a COMMERCIAL or RESI pill before you import. A job made with + New Job while in Commercial mode is commercial. Admins and managers can move a job either way from Job Info → DIVISION; nothing is deleted, the job just changes boards. Settings → COMMERCIAL DIVISION → Check divisions against Simpro lists any job whose division disagrees with Simpro so they can be fixed in one tap each.

What is different for a commercial job

Its card has its own tabs (Job Info, Activity, Photos, Plans & Links, Job Start, Gear & Submittals, RFIs, Change Orders, Open Items) — no Rough / Finish / QC. Job Info has a Commercial section (GC PM and super, contract value, permit, plan set, site hours, badge, parking, laydown), Scope by system, and Milestone dates. The Job Board groups by Pre-Con → Ready to Start → In Progress → On Hold → Closeout → Complete. Pre-con work runs through the Job Start tab (see its own guide). Residential reminders never fire on commercial jobs.
