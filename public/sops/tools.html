Tools

Field calculators that live inside the app: Generator Sizing, Appliance Loads, and Service Size

The 30-second version

The Tools tab holds standalone calculators. Pick a tool from the strip at the top and it opens right there. Nothing you type in a tool is saved anywhere — it recomputes as you go, and it works offline once you've opened it online at least once. To print or save a job sheet on your phone, tap Open full screen first, then use the tool's Print / Save as PDF button.

Planning aid, not a stamped calc. Every number a tool gives you is a planning figure. Verify the final size, fuel, pad, clearances, transfer switch and feeder against the specific model's spec sheet, its install drawing, and the AHJ's adopted code before quoting or installing.

Generator Sizing — top to bottom

1

Pick the generator family

Auto tries air-cooled first and steps up to liquid-cooled only when the load needs it. Force Air-cooled or Liquid-cooled when the customer already knows what they want. The big number at the top is the recommended kW; the ladder under it shows every size in that family, the running demand fill, and the surge tick.

2

01 — General load of the served panel

Conditioned square footage the generator actually backs up, plus the small-appliance and laundry circuits. This is NEC 220.82(A) — 3 VA per ft² plus 1,500 VA per circuit. Only count what rides the generator.

3

02 — Fixed appliances & 240V loads

Add each fastened-in-place load from the picker (A/C, heat pump, water heater, range, EV charger, well pump…). Fix the nameplate VA if you have it, use Qty for identical units, and tick motor / soft-start so the surge check is honest. The surge check assumes only the largest motor starts at once.

4

03 — Calculated demand

Read-only. Shows the 220.82(B) demand math (100% of the first 10 kVA, 40% of the rest) plus the larger of heating or cooling at 100%, and which motor drives the surge.

5

04 — Fuel & gas supply

Choose Natural gas or Liquid propane. For NG, enter the longest pipe run and any other gas appliances on that line — it returns full-load ft³/hr, the minimum schedule-40 pipe size (NFPA 54 low-pressure table) and the inlet pressure the unit wants. For LP it returns gal/hr and a minimum tank size. Vaporization, not volume, is the cold-weather limit — confirm with the propane supplier.

6

05 — Concrete pad & siting

Footprint, minimum pad, area, yards of concrete and unit weight, with Generac's default reveal (3 in air-cooled, 6 in liquid-cooled). The two drawings show the pad in plan view and the minimum keep-clear envelope — 18 in to the structure, 60 in from any operable opening, 36 in service access, 60 in overhead.

7

06 — Transfer switch & connections

Service size, whole-home vs essential circuits, feeder length and conductor material. It picks the Generac ATS, sizes the feeder breaker and conductor with a voltage-drop check, and states the neutral / bonding condition. The single-line is a planning schematic for the permit packet, not a point-to-point wiring diagram.

8

07 — Job sheet

Type the customer, address and your name, then Print / Save as PDF for a clean one-page summary. On a phone, tap Open full screen at the top of the Tools tab first so the print dialog belongs to the tool, not the app.

Don't confuse it with

The Generator Load Selection section on a job's Home Runs tab and the homeowner Generator Link. Those pick which circuits ride the generator and carry no wattage on purpose. Sizing lives only here, under Tools, and does not read or write any job.

Good to know

Air-cooled fuel figures are from Generac spec sheets. Liquid-cooled fuel, surge, footprints and pressures are conservative planning estimates — confirm them per model.

Service Size — sizing the service at bid

Only shows for the people Koy has given it to (Settings → Team → Tool access). It reads plans with Claude, and every read costs money.

1

Start from plans (optional)

Drop a plan set PDF, or photos of the sheets, on Drop a plan set here (or tap it to pick a file). Claude reads up to 8 sheets, floor plans and electrical first, and fills in the calculator. That takes 30 seconds to 2 minutes and needs signal. Tap Stop to cancel.

2

Check what it read

What the plans show lists the floor areas (estimates are marked est.), what it found and on which sheet, questions to ask the owner, and loads it saw that the calc doesn't cover. Every field it filled has a plans tag. Fix anything that's wrong.

3

Mark the extras Yes / Maybe / No

Yes = known or on the plans. Maybe = could happen. No = not planned. You get two numbers: confirmed only and with maybes. Undecided fuels count as gas in the first and electric in the second. Adds shows how many amps each item adds.

4

Read the recommendation

The big number is the recommended service (NEC 220.82 optional method, 120/240 V single-family). Owner changes that bump the size and Check before you bid list what would push it up and what to confirm with the utility.

5

Copy the bid note

Copy bid note puts the size and its assumptions on the clipboard so you can paste them into the proposal. Nothing is saved to a job. Copy the note if you want to keep it.

Bid-stage only

Utility requirements (meter class, transformer, lead time) and local amendments still need checking. Use the mechanical sub's tonnage when you have it.

Tools that fill each other

Appliance Loads knows what is really going in the house. The two calculators can start from it, and from each other.

1

Open the job in Appliance Loads

Tap a job. Its appliances are now available to the calculators on this device. Tap Send to Generator Sizing or Send to Service Size to jump straight there.

2

Tap Fill from Appliance Loads

Each calculator has a Start from another tool bar near the top. The button names the job and how many loads it has. Nothing changes until you tap it, and Undo puts back what was there.

3

Check what landed

Fields filled this way are tagged loads (or generator when the generator fills Service Size). In Service Size, appliances with no row of their own show under From another tool in Extras, counted as fixed appliances; fridges, microwaves, hoods and the first dishwasher are listed as covered by the standard allowances and not counted twice. A load the sheet had no amps for arrives with a typical value and a flag. Fix those first.

4

Calculators both ways

Service Size gives the generator its recommended service size for the transfer switch pick; the generator gives Service Size the square footage and circuit counts. Use Send to Generator Sizing next to Copy bid note, or Open in Service Size on the generator page.

Same device only

The handoff lives on the phone or computer you are using, like the remembered last tool. It is not saved to the job and does not follow you to another device.

Questions

"Reading plans needs an internet connection."

The Service Size calculator works offline, but reading plans goes out to Claude. Fill it in by hand, or drop the plans again once you have signal.

I don't see Service Size.

It's limited to the people Koy has turned it on for. Ask Koy.

Does anything I enter get saved?

No. The tool recomputes in the browser and writes nothing to the app or to any job. Refresh and it resets to the example loads. Print or save the job sheet if you want to keep a result.

It says "Tool not cached on this device yet."

You opened the Tools tab offline on a device that had never loaded that tool. Open it once with signal and it works offline after that.

The Fill button isn't there.

Nothing has been sent on this device yet. Open the job in Appliance Loads first, or type something in the other calculator, then look again.

Can we add more tools?

Yes — that's what the strip at the top is for. Send Koy the tool as a self-contained web page and it gets its own chip.
