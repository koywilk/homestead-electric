Projects

One job with several houses or buildings? A project keeps each building separate and still groups them.

The short version

A project is a folder of normal jobs. Each house or building is its own job with its own punch, questions, crew, POs and milestones. On the Job Board, projects sit in one folded PROJECTS group at the top.

Find a project's buildings

1

Open PROJECTS on the Job Board

Each project is one row: its name, how many houses, and how many are in rough or finish. Tap it to unfold.

2

Tap a building

Every building opens a normal job card. Searching a building name or the project's Simpro # (every building shares it) opens the PROJECTS group and the project by itself.

3

Hop between buildings

Inside a building, the line at the top reads PROJECT › BUILDING. The two buttons under it jump to the previous and next building. Tap the project name for the project page.

Make a project (office)

1

Job Info → Project → Make this a project

Name it and tap Create project. The job you were on becomes the project's Site / Shared building and keeps everything it already had. Nothing is moved or deleted. Quotes, temp peds and quick jobs can't be made into a project.

2

Add each building

On the project page tap + Add Building. Name it ("House 3"). If the buildings share one Simpro job, pick the building's Simpro folder; the list shows the folders Simpro actually has. If the app can't read Simpro's folder list, it lets you type the folder name instead (it saves when you press Enter or leave the box) and shows a warning. If the folder isn't in Simpro yet you'll see a warning, and its plans wait in Shared / Unsorted until someone makes it. A building with its own Simpro # works like any job.

3

Pick its tabs

Switch off the tabs that building doesn't need, the same switches as Job Sections. On a commercial project you see the commercial switches (Materials, Job Start, Power, Lighting, Gear, Underground, Gear & Submittals, RFIs); on a residential project you see the residential ones (Panelized Lighting, Tape Light, Generator and so on). GC, address and foreman copy over from the project.

Good to know

Shared Simpro #: one building is the Simpro lead. Margins, scheduled crew and hours, and the Friday Packet count that Simpro job once, on the lead (or on Site / Shared). The crew planner does the same: a shared Simpro job's scheduled crew and hours are credited only to the lead building (or Site / Shared).
Subcontractors: contractor access doesn't show the project page, the project line at the top of a building, or the Project section. A subcontractor sees only their own buildings, under the project row.
Leave or archive: taking a job out of a project, or archiving a project, never deletes a job or anything on it.
