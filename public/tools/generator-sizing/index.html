01

General load of the served panel

NEC 220.82(A): 3 VA/ft² of conditioned area, plus 1,500 VA per small-appliance and laundry circuit. Use only the area and circuits the generator backs up.

02

Fixed appliances & 240V loads

Nameplate VA for everything fastened in place. Use Qty for identical units — the surge check assumes only the largest single motor starts at a time (staggered start). Lights, receptacles and the fridge are already covered above.

03

Calculated demand

220.82(B) takes 100% of the first 10 kVA of general + appliance load and 40% of the remainder. 220.82(C) adds the larger of heating or cooling at 100%.

04

Fuel & gas supply

The size number is only half the install. This checks the fuel the recommended unit draws at full load, the inlet pressure it needs, and — for natural gas — the minimum pipe to feed it.

Job sheet

Air-cooled (Guardian / Next Gen): 10·14·18·22·24·26·28 kW, one 48×25 in footprint. Liquid-cooled (Protector, 1Ø 120/240V): 22·25·27·30·32·36·38·40·45·48·60·80 kW. Above 80 kW single-phase → parallel units or 3-phase.

Fuel figures are full-load draws from Generac spec sheets (air-cooled published; liquid-cooled estimated). NG pipe uses the NFPA 54 / IFGC low-pressure schedule-40 table (0.60 SG, 0.5 in W.C. drop) plus any other gas load entered. Footprints and weights are representative by kW class.
