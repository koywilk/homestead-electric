Panel Builder

Clickable mockup. Loads live in one list; panels hold modules; modules hold zones. Tap any load to put it on a zone, tap any zone to move or clear it. No dragging anywhere, so it works the same on a phone and a laptop.

What's different from today's Panel Loads section.
  • A panel is a panel (LCP 1, LCP 2…), with a location and a module-slot count. Today a panel is a floor section, and extra panels are "extra floors".
  • A load is assigned by reference. Today assigning copies the load's name into the module's own list, so renames drift and one load can be on two modules.
  • Capacity is visible: zones used per module, slots used per panel, watts per dimmer zone, and unassigned count, all before anything is printed.
  • Suggest layout fills modules from the tray by room and type, and you correct it. Today every load is placed by hand through a dropdown.
