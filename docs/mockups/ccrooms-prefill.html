  • Checked by default: rooms with loads or a drawn zone on the plan. Unchecked: a name typed in FieldInk with no loads and no zone (usually an old spelling) — shown greyed so it's visible but not added unless you tick it.
  • on Rough / on Finish tags: the name is already on that list for this floor; the counts in the Add button skip it.
  • Hide: drops that room from this list (just a flag on the FieldInk doc; FieldInk is never told, nothing on the job changes). A "1 hidden · Show" line in the footer brings it back with Unhide, like the Dismissed chip on Incoming loads.
  • No floor: a room FieldInk sent without a floor can't be offered here — the footnote says how many and where to fix it.
