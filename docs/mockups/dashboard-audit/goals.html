How it is counted

A change order counts as booked when its own cost center (company cost center "… - Change Order (Rough In / Finish)") shows actual labor hours. Of the CO cost centers sold with estimated hours on this year's invoiced jobs, how many have any hours. A CO whose section was put on the Rough In cost center is not booked to its own, so it does not count here — that is the habit the goal is about. Material-only COs (no hours sold) are left out of the test.
