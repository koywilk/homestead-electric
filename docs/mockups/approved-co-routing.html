What changes

  1. Rough / Finish pick on every approved CO. Saved on the CO as punchPhase. Blank means "use the job's stage" (today's rule), so no existing CO moves until someone taps the other side.
  2. Crew can mark work completed on both the punch list and the COs tab. It writes the same Work Completed status the office button writes today, so the invoice task still gets made. Approve, Deny and the other statuses stay office-only.
  3. Hard to miss: a teal count on the Rough/Finish tab, a "2 change orders" badge on the Punch List header (visible even when the section is closed), and a solid teal bar that lists each open CO by name. The bar still starts closed, as you asked in v417. It just can't be overlooked now.
  4. Convert to Return Trip shows on every approved CO, not only when the crew isn't on site.
  5. Up Next is out.
