# Crew brief — App update v498 (2026-10-02)

App update v498. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings says v498. One change, on Job Info: a job can now keep two GC contacts, so the on-site super and the office PM both have a place to live.

EVERYONE (RESI AND COMMERCIAL)
- Two GC contacts per job (Justin's request). Design-build jobs have an on-site super and an office PM, and job setup needs both. On Job Info, after you pull from Simpro, the GC Contacts list lets you tap a name to mark who is running the job. You can now mark two people. The first still goes in GC Contact and GC Phone like always. The second goes in new GC Contact 2 and GC Phone 2 boxes right under them.
  Try: open a job with a Simpro number, tap Pull, then tap two names in GC Contacts. The two you marked move to the top and say Running this job.
- Super or PM in one tap. Each person you mark gets two small buttons, Onsite Super and Office PM. Tap one and it fills that person's label. Tap it again to clear it. You can still type any label you want.
- Type the second contact in yourself. Under GC Phone there is a line that says "+ 2nd GC contact (e.g. office PM)". Tap it to open two boxes and type a name and number. Use this when the job has no Simpro number yet or the PM is not in Simpro's list. It stays open once it has something in it.
- A third tap does nothing but tell you. If two people are already marked, tapping a third shows a note to clear one first, so nobody gets swapped out by accident. If you clear the first person, the second one moves up into GC Contact and GC Phone so those boxes are never left empty.
- Pull is safe. It still only fills blank boxes and never writes over what you typed or picked, including the second contact.

REMOVED OR CHANGED
- Nothing removed. Jobs with one contact look and work exactly the same.
