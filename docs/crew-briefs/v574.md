APP UPDATE v574

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v574.

EVERYONE (RESIDENTIAL JOBS)

- Approved change orders are easy to find now. Every CO the customer approved sits at the top of the punch list on the Rough or Finish tab as a solid teal bar that lists each one by name. You'll also see a teal "CO" count on the Rough or Finish tab and a "change orders" badge on the Punch List header, even when the punch list is closed.
  Try: open a job with an approved CO, look for the teal count on the Rough or Finish tab, open the punch list and tap Open on the teal bar.

- Anyone can mark a change order done. The green Mark Work Completed button is on the approved CO, on the punch list and on the COs tab. You don't have to tell the office anymore. If you tap it by mistake, hit Undo on the bar at the bottom of the screen within 10 seconds.
  Try: finish the work on an approved CO, then tap Mark Work Completed.

- Pick which punch list a change order goes on. Each approved CO on the COs tab has a Rough punch / Finish punch switch. The app still picks for you from where the job is. Tap the other side if it belongs there, like fixtures approved while you're still in rough. On the punch list itself, "Move to Finish punch" (or Rough) is at the bottom of the card.
  Try: open the COs tab, find an approved CO, check it's on the right punch list.

- A change order kept on Rough doesn't get lost. If it's still open after rough is done, the Finish punch list shows "still open on the Rough punch" with a link back to it.

REMOVED OR CHANGED

- Convert to Return Trip now shows on every approved CO, whether or not a crew is scheduled on the job today.
- The thin purple "Approved COs" line at the top of the punch list is gone. The teal bar replaces it.
