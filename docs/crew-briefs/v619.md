APP UPDATE v619

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v619.

NEW (anyone who works quick jobs)

- Quick jobs have a place for plans now. Open a quick job and tap Plans (under Photos). Upload Files adds a PDF, photo or drawing; Take Photo snaps a picture of a paper plan. Tap Open on a file to view it, or the X to delete it (it asks first).
- A quick job card shows "1 plan file" or "2 plan files" when plans are attached, so you know to look before you go.

These are the same as Uploaded Files on a full job's Plans & Links tab.

No job data was changed by this update.
