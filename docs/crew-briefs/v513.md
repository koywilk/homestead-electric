# Crew brief — App update v513 (2026-10-06)

App update v513. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings says v513. Messages from the office now look different from everything else, can carry photos and files, and come in three kinds.

EVERYONE (RESI AND COMMERCIAL)
- Office messages stand out (Koy's request). In the bell they sit in their own "From the office" group at the top with a colored tag, so they don't get lost between change orders and return trips. Tap one to read the whole thing, with any photos and files.
- Three kinds:
  - Announcement (blue): news from the office. Just read it.
  - Important (red): tap the big Got it button so the office knows you've read it. Until you do, it stays pinned at the top of My Day, and you'll get a reminder the next morning.
  - Discussion (purple): a group conversation. Type in Reply to the group, add a photo if you want, and tap Send reply. Every reply notifies everyone in it.
  Try: when an Important one comes in, open it and tap Got it — the card on My Day goes away.
- The phone banner says which kind it is, like "IMPORTANT · New PPE rule starts Monday".

OFFICE ONLY (KOY, JOSH, BRADY, JUSTIN, JEROMY)
- Sending. Bell, then Send. Pick What kind, pick a group or type names, write the headline and message, and use Add photos or files (on a computer you can drag files onto it). Send me a test first if you want to see it on your phone.
- Tracking. Under Recently sent, Important messages show Got it from X of N; tap it to see who confirmed and when, who opened it but hasn't tapped Got it, and who hasn't seen it. Remind the ones who haven't pings only the people still missing.
  Try: send yourself a test Important message, then open it from the bell and tap Got it.

REMOVED OR CHANGED
- Nothing removed. Plain notifications (change orders, return trips, tasks) work the same; they're listed under "Everything else" when office messages are present.
