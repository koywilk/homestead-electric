# Crew brief — App update v522 (2026-10-07)

App update v522. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings says v522. One small change on commercial Job Start.

COMMERCIAL ONLY
- Phase 4 items can be checked by hand (Justin's request): in Job Start, Phase 4 (Electrical Submittal Review) has three items that used to check themselves only from the logs: RFI LIST, SENT TO GC and ALL APPROVED. Before, tapping them just jumped to the log. Now you can tap them like any other item (open, done, not needed). They still check themselves when the RFI or Gear & Submittals log shows it, whichever comes first.
  Try: open a commercial job in Phase 4, go to Job Start, and tap SENT TO GC. It should check, and the percentage should go up.

Phases 2, 3 and 5 haven't changed. Their log-linked items still check themselves from the logs.
