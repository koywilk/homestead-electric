APP UPDATE v613

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v613.

NEW

- Every open punch item now shows the day it was added and how old it is, right after "added by", for example "added by Jacob Spackman · Aug 13 · 82 days".
  Try: open any job's Rough or Finish punch list and read the gray line under an item.

- When you tap Wait on an item, it now shows how long it's been waiting next to what it's waiting on, for example "since Nov 3 · today". Changing the reason keeps the date. Tapping Waiting × clears it.
  Items that were already waiting before today say "since before Nov 3", because the app didn't save that date before.

Nothing else on the punch list changed, and no job data was changed by this update.
