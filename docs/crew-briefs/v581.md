APP UPDATE v581

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v581.

OFFICE DASHBOARD ONLY (the /dashboard page admins use). Nothing on the crew's job cards changed.

SCHEDULE TAB

- Every start now says who is on it. Under each rough or finish start on the week grid and in the Starts list you'll see "Crew: Louis, Trever" pulled from the Crew Planner for that day, plus anyone Simpro has scheduled on that job. If nobody is on it, the line reads "Nobody assigned on the planner" in red. The Starts tile counts how many starts have nobody.
  Try: open Schedule, look at the next start on the grid. If it's red, put a crew on it in the Crew Planner and come back; the line updates on the next load.

- Inspections say whether they're called in. Each inspection on the grid reads "Called in · 11/4". The Inspections tile also says "N still to call in": jobs that are 85% or more through rough or finish with no inspection date on them yet. Tap the tile; those jobs are listed under the week's inspections.
  Try: tap the Inspections tile. Anything under "Not called in yet" needs a call to the inspector and a date typed on the job.

- Time off shows under the day. A quiet grey "Out: Abraham, James" line at the bottom of a day column is approved time off that day, from the same list the Time Off page uses.

- Matterport scans moved. The scans list is no longer its own box on the right. It's a folded group inside "Next 30 days" called "Matterport scans due" with the count on it. Tap it to open. The Scans due number on the right still opens the same list.

QUALITY TAB

- The three punch boxes are one. "Open punch by age", "Punch by foreman" and "Punch on jobs near final" now live inside one folded "Punch" section. The header shows the total open, how many are over 30 days, how many are serious, and how many sit on jobs near final. Tap it to open all three views.

- New: First-pass by foreman. Same rule as the first-pass number at the top (each job's first inspection of a phase, last 90 days), per person. A job counts for its foreman and for its lead. You see "3 of 4 inspected jobs passed first time" and the rate. Anyone with fewer than 3 inspected jobs is behind a second fold, because 1 of 1 isn't a rate.

- New: Return-trip causes. Open return trips grouped by where they came from: change order, QC walk, failed inspection, GC request, job note. Ones typed straight onto the Return Trips tab show as "Added by hand" if a scope was written, or "No reason" if nothing was. Tap a bar for the jobs. Right now most open trips are "Added by hand", so the scope is the only record of why.

- New: Re-inspection booked. Failed inspections that already have a re-inspection date, soonest first, grouped by day. Red "N d past" when the date went by with no result logged.

Everything new starts folded with a count on the header. Tap a header to open it.
