APP UPDATE v651

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v651.

NEW (Dashboard: Meetings tab, the Scramble calendar)

- Open /dashboard, tap Meetings. The Calendar · all crews fold is at the top of the Weekly Scramble.
- Five weeks, 4-10s (Monday to Thursday). Every block says Rough-in (blue), Finish (green) or Return trip (purple).
- Solid blocks are booked in Simpro, with who is booked. Light blocks are forecasts: the hours left from Job Costs at 10 hours a day.
- A dot marks the start date in the app. Filled means confirmed.
- Drag a light block to move it. Drag the grip on the right end of any block to add or take off days.
- The list on the right is everything not on the calendar yet. A red ! means it needs scheduling, a material PO, or a return trip date or crew.
- Drag a card onto a day (or tap it, then tap a day). Keep as plan saves it for everyone in the meeting.
- Book in Simpro: pick who, look at every entry it would book (hours over 40 go in at 1.5x), then tap Book to send it. Nothing goes to Simpro before that tap.

No job's stage or status is changed by this update. Booking only adds Simpro schedule entries.
