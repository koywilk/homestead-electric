# Crew brief — App update v505 (2026-10-03)

App update v505. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings says v505. This covers everything since v502: My Day pins what people send you, the app moves more, commercial punch lists by building, and quote folders for the office.

EVERYONE (RESI AND COMMERCIAL)
- New for you on My Day (Koy): anything someone else sends you (a need, a question, a punch item assigned to you) stays pinned at the very top in a New for you box, tagged with who sent it, until you tap it. Just having it on screen doesn't clear it.
  Try: open My Day and tap each row in New for you.
- The app moves more (Koy): screens slide when you change tabs, a checked-off punch item gets a strike line drawn through it, a green ring flashes on a field when your edit saves, and the header shows SAVING or NOT SAVED. When a new version is ready, a bar says "Update ready · tap to reload". Animations are on even if your phone has Reduce Motion turned on; Settings (the ⋯ menu) > Animations turns them off.
  Try: open a job and swipe left or right across it to change tabs. On a phone, the More menu now opens from the bottom.
- Who else is on the job: the job header shows a small initials bubble for anyone else who has had that job open in the last 10 minutes.

COMMERCIAL ONLY
- Punch list by building (Brady's request): Power, Lighting and the Underground tabs are now a punch list like residential. Add a building or area once per job and it shows on every one of those tabs. Each tab starts with a General area. Gear keeps its checklist.
  Try: open a commercial job, go to Power, tap + Add Building / Area.

OFFICE
- Quote folders from site walks: when Josh, Brady or Justin put a site walk on the calendar, the office side will make the job's Drive folder from the Simpro quote, and that same folder becomes the job's folder when the quote turns into a job. It starts in a test run, so nothing changes in the field yet. If Create Drive folder ever says "Linked the folder already made for this quote", that's this working.
- Walk scheduling habit: put the address in the Location box when you schedule a walk. Walks with no address can't be matched automatically.

Send what you find to Koy: which screen, which job, what you expected. Screenshots help.
