Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v659.

FIXED for the office: pairs on the Crew Board

- Anyone can be paired with anyone now: a foreman with an apprentice, two leads, or people on different crews.
- Settings › Crew Board: tap someone, then Paired with. Type a name to find someone fast.
- People marked "not scheduled" are Unassigned and never go on the crew calendar.

NEW on the crew calendar

- Approved time off shows on each day ("Off: names"). Nobody gets scheduled on a day they're off.
- Company holidays are shaded and named, and nobody is booked on them: Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Eve, New Year's Day, Memorial Day, July 4 and Labor Day. Admins can change the list beside the calendar.

No job data is changed by this update.
