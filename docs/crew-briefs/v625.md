APP UPDATE v625

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v625.

NEW (Finish tab: Punch List in the status dropdown)

- The Finish status dropdown has a new choice, Punch List, right under In Progress.
- Pick it when the job is down to the punch list. The job moves to the Punch List section on the Job Board.
- The app still moves a job there on its own at 90%+ with open items. When it does, the dropdown says Punch List too.
- If a job isn't really on punch list yet, pick In Progress and it stays in Finish In Progress.
- When everything is closed, set finish to Complete like before.

No job data was changed by this update. Picking Punch List saves one setting on that job and nothing else.
