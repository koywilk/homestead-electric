APP UPDATE v587

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v587.

JOB BOARD

- Crew Schedule cards are smaller, so you see more of the week without scrolling. Each person now fits on one line: name, hour bar, times. On a very wide monitor, each day shows two cards across.
  Try: open the Job Board and look at the busiest day in the Crew Schedule.

JOB PAGE

- The top of the job page has a new look. A colored band says what stage the job is in. All your pills and buttons are still there, including + Need, Refresh and Close.
  Try: open any job and read the band across the top.

- New progress strip. It shows Pre-Job Prep, Rough, Rough inspection, Finish, Final inspection and QC at a glance, with the Rough and Finish bars. Tap a tile to go to that tab. You still change progress on the Rough and Finish tabs, same as before.
  Try: tap Rough on the strip.

- On a big screen, the tabs are now a list down the left side, in the same order. On a phone they're still the row across the top.

Nothing inside the job tabs changed, and no job data was touched.
