# Crew brief — App update v525 (2026-10-08)

App update v525. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings says v525. Big one: you can now send POs to CED straight from the app. No more Simpro Mobile for CED orders.

RESIDENTIAL ONLY
- Order from the bid: on Rough or Finish, open Material Tracking and tap New order from the bid. Pick a cost center (orange shows what's still to go on the bid), tap Add on what you need and fix the count with − and +. Anything not in the bid, type it in the box on that cost center. You can type the whole order there too. Add from as many cost centers as you need, pick Will call or Deliver to job and the date, then Send to CED. A paper plane flies while it sends and a green check shows when it went. Each cost center becomes its own PO, charged to the right cost center, and CED gets them all in one email with the PO forms attached. You and bids@ are copied, and CED's reply comes to you.
  Try: on your next CED order, use New order from the bid instead of Simpro Mobile. Check the email in your inbox.
- Send from a PO card: if you already typed the list on a PO card, a CED card now has Send to CED. Home Depot, ACE and Amazon cards have Get a PO number, which gives you the number for the counter and saves it on the card.
  Try: on a Home Depot run, tap Get a PO number on the card.
- If it doesn't finish (bad signal, Simpro slow), the screen says what happened. Tap Send again. It never makes a PO twice or emails CED twice.

REMOVED OR CHANGED
- Copy for Simpro is still on the card, but you shouldn't need it for CED anymore.
