APP UPDATE v671

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v671.

NEW on the crew calendar (office)

- Fridays have a "Said yes" list. Tap "Add who said yes", tick the people, and their names show on that Friday to drag onto a job.
- Drag a name onto a job when they're busy and it now asks what you want: use the hours they're free, or move them here.
- An Upcoming job with a confirmed start now goes on the calendar. If it has no foreman or hours yet, it shows red on its day under Needs you.
- Each block says where its material stands. A return trip says who it's assigned to. A plan says who placed it and when.
- The Book preview shows "Check:" lines when a set pair is split or someone would be alone. You can still book it.

CHANGED

- Book now refuses someone who is off, not on a crew, or on a holiday or weekend, even if the screen missed it.
- The Crew Planner has a note at the top: booking happens on the crew calendar now.

No job data is changed by this update.
