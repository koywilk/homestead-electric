NEW on the crew calendar (office)

- Tick someone who is busy for only part of a job's time and you now get their free hours as a choice. Example: Jacob is on another job 10 to 12, this job is 7 to 11. You can add Jacob and make this job 7 to 10. The other job stays as it is.
- Moving them off the other job is still there as the second choice.
- This also works when the other job is booked in Simpro. Then the free hours are the only choice, because a Simpro booking can't be moved from here.

FIXED

- A job or trip that's already booked in Simpro for a day no longer also shows its old plan for that day.
- Someone Simpro has on another job at that time is left off a plan that day, and the block says so.

No job data is changed by this update.
