APP UPDATE v614

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v614.

NEW

- Cut sheets for the parts we use most are now on every job. Open any job, go to Plans & Links, and look under Drive Plans for a folded Cut Sheets block. It holds the shop's 157 manufacturer spec sheets: wiring devices, panels and breakers, lighting and wafers (Keystone, RAB, including the 2" and gimbal ones), fans, boxes, wire and more.
  Try: open Cut Sheets and type a part number or a few words, like HOM120, WFRX-2 or 4in wafer. Tap View to read the sheet right in the app, or Open to get it in Drive so you can download, print or send it to a GC.
  With nothing typed, the sheets are sorted by category, then by brand. Each group starts folded with a count.

The same sheets are in the Shared Drive under Homestead Job Organization › Cut sheets, with a Cut Sheet Index of all of them. This update only reads that folder. No job data was changed.

Can't find a part? Tell Koy which one, and the sheet gets added to the folder. It shows up in the app on its own.
