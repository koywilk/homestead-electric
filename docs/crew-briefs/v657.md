NEW for the owners: Friday Strategy on the dashboard

- Dashboard › Meetings has a fourth button, Friday Strategy · Fri 8:00. Only admins see it (owners and the office).
- It fills itself every Thursday at 9 PM for the next week: top items, next week on the crew calendar, starts at risk, return trips, what needs a date, dates that moved, inspections and walks, job numbers with dollars, and who's out.
- Every row can be noted, edited, hidden or added to. Action items go straight to someone's My Day.

NEW on every meeting

- Edit under any row changes its words for that week's meeting. The job itself doesn't change.

No job data is changed by this update.
