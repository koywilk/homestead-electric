# Crew brief — App update v528 (2026-10-09)

App update v528. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings says v528. Commercial jobs get a Materials tab.

COMMERCIAL ONLY
- Materials tab (Braden's request): every commercial job card now has a Materials tab, right next to Bid Items. It's the same Material Tracking the residential crew uses on Rough and Finish, with one list for the whole job: PO cards with the list, supplier, pickup date, and Picked Up.
  Try: open a commercial job, tap Materials, tap + Add PO, type a short list.
- Charge to: when you tap Send to CED on a card, pick the cost center the order is charged to (for example Site · Site Underground or Site · Power Feeds). Send stays grey until you pick one, so nothing lands on the wrong cost center. The pick is saved on the card.
  Try: on a real order you need, tap Send to CED, pick the cost center, send. The PO number lands on the card.
- Order from the bid: New order from the bid shows the job's bid lines by cost center. Tick what you need. Each cost center becomes its own PO, all on one email to CED.
- Cards update themselves from Simpro once the PO is received, same as residential. Open orders show in Open Items, marked Materials.

Heads up: Send to CED makes a real PO in Simpro and emails CED. Only send orders you actually need.
