APP UPDATE v580

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v580.

OFFICE DASHBOARD ONLY (admins). Nothing changes on the crew side of the app.

- Jobs tab: two new columns. "Days in stage" is how long a job has sat where it is, counted from a date the job already has: the start date for rough or finish in progress, the rough inspection pass for "rough done, finish next", the last fail for a failed rough. The small line under the number says which date it is. A dash means the app has no date for that stage (holds, prep, no status), so nothing is guessed. "Waiting on" says GC, Inspection or Us: a question out to the GC, designer or homeowner; an inspection booked or failed with no re-inspection; a question the office owes or an open task on the job. Blank when nothing is waiting. Both columns sort when you tap the header, and a crew fold's header says how many of its jobs are waiting.
  Try: open the dashboard, Jobs, tap "Days in stage" to put the longest-sitting jobs on top. Tap "Waiting on" to put the GC-blocked ones first.

- Jobs tab, removed: the Punch column and the "Where the work is" bars on the right. The chips above the table carry the same counts.

- Activity tab: Open questions now sit in two folds under the cards, "Waiting on me" (the office answers) and "Waiting on others" (out to the GC, a designer, the homeowner or a foreman). Each starts closed with its count; tap to open. Oldest first, and anything older than 2 days is marked red with its age. Tap a row to open the job.
  Try: Activity, tap "Waiting on others", and you'll see who each one is out to.

- Activity tab, removed: the "Everything logged" and "Punch closed" cards, the "What kind" bars and the "Most active jobs" list. The list of recent activity, its filters and the "Done by" picker are unchanged.
