APP UPDATE v680

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v680.

NEW on the crew calendar (office)

- **Take a job off one day and leave the rest.** Remove used to take the whole job off the calendar. Now, on a job of two or more days, Remove first asks about just the day you tapped. "Off this day, hours move later" keeps all the job's hours, so it ends a day later. "Off this day, drop its hours" makes the job a day shorter. The other days keep their people and times. Koy's request.
Try: tap a job on a day, tap Remove…, then pick one of the two "Off this day" buttons. The job box then lists Days taken off with Put back.

- **Book just one day in Simpro.** Next to "Book this week in Simpro…" there is now a button for each day: Just Mon, Just Tue, and so on. It books every job on that day, that day only. The other days stay planned. Inside a job's box, Book in Simpro has the same choice: All its days, or just the day you tapped. Koy's request.
Try: tap Just Mon, read the list, tap Book these entries.

- **Booking takes fewer taps.** Book in Simpro and Book this week now open with the list of entries already built, so it is one tap to see it and one tap to book. Nothing goes into Simpro before that second tap.

FIXED

- **Book in Simpro looked like it did nothing.** On a job with a long box, the booking part opened below the bottom edge where you couldn't see it. It now scrolls into view when you tap Book in Simpro. Same for Remove.

CHANGED

- Removing the whole job works the same as before, same number of taps.
- Days already booked in Simpro still change in Simpro.

No job data is changed by this update.
