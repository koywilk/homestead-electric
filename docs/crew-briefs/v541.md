# Crew brief — App update v541 (2026-10-15)

App update v541. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings says v541.

EVERYONE (RESI AND COMMERCIAL)
- **My Day looks different: four tabs.** To do · Waiting on others · Done (plus Team for Koy). One thing per screen, and nothing shows up twice.
  Try: open My Day and tap through the tabs.
- **To do is in order.** Overdue and Today are open at the top; This week and Later are folded underneath. Each row is short: a circle to finish it, the title, and one grey line with the job and who sent it.
  Try: tap the circle on something you've finished.
- **Tap a row for everything else.** Working on it, Reply, Snooze and Open job are right there. More… holds Urgent, Pin, Photo, Move and Edit. Swiping still works: right = done, left = snooze. Tap the job name to open the job.
  Try: tap any task and look at the buttons.
- **Snooze really hides things now.** Snoozed items, including the automatic reminders, go into a folded **Snoozed** spot at the bottom with the day they come back. **Wake now** brings one back early.
  Try: snooze something for 3 days, then open Snoozed at the bottom.
- **Waiting on others shows who you're chasing.** Everything you asked for, with the person's name on each row. Not started is at the top, with Nudge one tap away.
  Try: open Waiting on others.
- **Group button** (the lines icon next to search): sort To do by Time, Job, Type or Who sent it. Select several is in there too.
- **Crew POs: a Custom option.** When you send a PO to CED, "Get it" now has Will call, Deliver to job, or **Custom**. Pick Custom and type exactly what CED should do: something that isn't regular stock, who to call with an ETA, hold it at the counter. Your words go in the email, on the PO form and in Simpro, and show on the card as "Told CED".
  Try: next time you send a PO, look for Custom next to Will call.

SHARE LINKS (WHAT THE GC OR CUSTOMER SEES)
- **No more false "not available" on bad signal.** A punch, questions, notes or home runs link opened with weak signal now says "Weak signal — still loading…" and fills in by itself when signal comes back.
- **The GC can leave a note on a punch item.** On a punch list link, every open item has "Leave a note", and waiting items get a one-tap "Ready now". The note shows under the item in the app with a **Got it** button, and the lead and foreman get a phone alert. You can turn the alert off in Settings → Notifications ("Note left on a punch share link").
  Try: open a punch item that has a GC note and tap Got it.

MOVED OR REMOVED
- On My Day, these are gone: the View switch (Category/Job/Person), the Sort switch, the strip of job chips, and the "New for you" and "New since" groups. New items get a blue dot instead.
- Focus today is now **Pinned** (under More… on a row). Sent is now the **Waiting on others** tab. Done and Sent · finished are now one **Done** tab. Office messages are a fold at the bottom of To do.

The "?" guides for My Day, Rough and Finish are updated.

Send what you find to Koy: which screen, which job, what you expected. Screenshots help.
