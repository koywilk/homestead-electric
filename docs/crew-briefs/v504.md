# Crew brief — App update v504 (2026-10-03)

App update v504. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings says v504. This one is mostly motion: the app now shows you what just happened and where things went, instead of making you guess. Plus a few tool and commercial updates from the other sessions this week.

EVERYONE (resi and commercial)
- New for you on My Day (Koy's request): anything someone else sent you — a need, a task, a question, a punch item — sits in a blue "New for you" box at the very top of My Day until you tap it. Each row says who it's from, and the title shows "N new for you". It will not clear by itself; only tapping clears it.
  Try: have someone send you a need, open My Day, see the box, tap the row.
- Swipe on My Day: drag a row right to mark it Done (green), left to Snooze (amber). The buttons still work too.
- Things tell you where they went (Koy's request): tap Create Return Trip, Convert CO, promote a note line, or create a return trip from the QC prompt, and a chip flies from the button to the Return Trips tab, which bumps. Success messages pop up out of the button you pressed instead of the top corner. When a save lands, a green ring spreads out of the field you just edited.
- Check-offs you can see: the punch strike-through is now a thick dark line with a dot leading it. Android phones buzz. The Undo bar at the bottom shrinks as the ten seconds run out.
- Getting around: tapping a job on the Job Board zooms the card up into the job. Inside a job, swipe the page sideways to change tabs. Nav tabs slide in from the direction you're moving. On a phone, More is a bottom sheet. My Day dropdowns unfold instead of popping.
- Photos: tap a photo and it zooms up from the thumbnail, with ‹ › to step through the set. Each photo uploading shows a ring until it lands.
- Rough / Finish: record a Fail and the failed-items box unfolds; Pass and Fail buttons crossfade. Return trip cards, CO cards and inspection items are keyed so the app can point at them (Email on a CO flashes the card "Email sent"; checking an item on a return trip flashes the matching inspection item).
- Live: if someone else has the same job open, their initials bubble breathes in the job header. Counts roll to the new number. Stage bars under 100% carry a slow sheen. When a new version has downloaded, an "Update ready, tap to reload" bar slides up.
- Animations switch: Settings has an ANIMATIONS section (and the ⋯ menu on the Job Board) to turn all of this off on your device. Motion is on for everyone by default now, including phones with Reduce Motion on.

FROM THE OTHER SESSIONS THIS WEEK (v498–v503)
- Job Info: two GC contacts can run a job, an on-site super and an office PM.
- Commercial phase tabs get a punch list by building.
- Service Size: printable customer copy and office copy, and printing works from a phone.
- Generator Sizing: pull a job's generator-selected loads, with a job search box.
- The three Tools fill each other: Appliance Loads feeds Service Size and Generator Sizing, and the two calculators feed each other.

FIXES
- Nothing broken was fixed in this one; the black-screen fix shipped earlier as v491/v492.

REMOVED OR CHANGED
- Rows no longer slide around on their own. An earlier preview had lists re-sorting themselves and the crew schedule jumping; that engine is gone. Everything that moves now moves because you tapped or swiped.
- Not in this one: Safety (outside site), Service Size motion (separate tool), and a dozen smaller ideas from the walkthrough that need another pass.
