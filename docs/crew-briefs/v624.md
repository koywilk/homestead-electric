APP UPDATE v624

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v624.

NEW (Job Board: Punch List)

- The Job Board has a new Punch List section between Finish In Progress and Completed.
- A job moves there on its own when finish is at 90% or more (or marked Complete) and something is still open: a finish punch item, a QC item, a failed final inspection item, or a return trip that isn't done.
- The top of the job says what's left, like "2 punch items · 1 return trip".
- Close the last item and the job moves to Completed by itself. There's nothing new to tap.
- A job On Hold stays On Hold.

Some older jobs you finished will show up in Punch List again. They still have open punch items or a return trip in the app. If the work is done, check the items off. If an item isn't real, void it. If it still needs doing, tell Koy.

No job data was changed by this update.
