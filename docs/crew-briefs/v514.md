# Crew brief — App update v514 (2026-10-06)

App update v514. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings says v514. Office messages tidy themselves up, My Day keeps all of them in one place, and commercial Job Start shows every item at once.

EVERYONE (RESI AND COMMERCIAL)
- Office messages fold away when you're done with them (Koy's request). In the bell, an office message stays up top only while it still needs you: an Important you haven't tapped Got it on, an announcement you haven't opened, or a discussion with new replies. Everything finished folds into one "done" line at the bottom of that group.
  Try: open the bell. Anything you've already handled should be in the done line, not in your way.
- My Day has a new "Messages from the office" section (starts folded). Tabs for Important, Announcements and Discussions, with what still needs you on top. Tap a message to read it, then use the arrows to step through the rest in that tab.
  Try: open My Day, tap Messages from the office, and look through the Important tab.

COMMERCIAL ONLY
- Job Start shows the whole checklist (Justin's request). Each job's Job Start card now opens as a checklist of all 12 phases, one item per row, so you can see everything still to do at once. Check items in any order; later phases aren't locked anymore. A percentage under the job name shows how far along it is. Prefer the old look? Tap Phases above the bar.
- On the Job Start board, each job starts folded to one line: name, foreman, any OWED or GEAR LATE flag, and the percentage. Tap it to open.
  Try: open Job Start, tap a job, and check off something from a later phase.

OFFICE ONLY
- Sent tab in My Day (office only): every message you've sent, with who has seen it.
- Temp Peds Out on Job Prep (Justin's request). Every temp ped out on a job, in any stage: Ped #, job, foreman, days out (red past 90), and whether a pickup is scheduled. Problems go on top: no Ped #, or the same # on two jobs. Schedule pickup makes the pickup job; when that job is completed, the ped clears on its own. Picked up does it by hand, with Undo.
- Temp ped cards link to the real job card. A temp ped card is usually made before the quote is signed. Once the job card exists (imported or new), the ped's row and the top of that job's Job Info ask "is this ped on that job?" Link to this job ticks the ped on the job card and copies the number and out date. Not this one hides the match.
- The Temp Ped # list on a temp ped card now goes to 100 instead of stopping at 10.
  Try: open Job Prep, open Temp Peds Out, and link the Welliver ped once its job card is in.

REMOVED OR CHANGED
- Nothing removed. On commercial Job Start, the chips view is still there under Phases.
