# Crew brief — App update v548 (2026-10-16)

App update v548. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings says v548.

EVERYONE (RESI AND COMMERCIAL)
- **Home Depot, ACE and Amazon receipts go on the PO from the app.** Any Home Depot, ACE or Amazon card in Material Tracking (Rough, Finish, or Materials on a commercial job) that has a PO number now has an orange "No receipt yet" box. Tap Add receipt photo, then Take photo, or Choose photo if you already took it. The screen shows which PO it goes on. Tap Attach and the photo lands on that PO in Simpro with your name and the date, so the office can post the cost without chasing you for it. The box turns green with who and when. Long receipt? Add another photo. It works whether the PO number came from Get a PO number or from Simpro Mobile.
  Try: next store run, snap the receipt in the parking lot before you leave.
- **The app checks the PO number first.** If the PO box holds the job number, another job's PO, or a CED PO, it tells you and sends nothing. Fix the number on the card, then add the photo.
- **Picked up asks once.** Ticking Picked up on a store card with no receipt asks "Got the receipt? Snap it now." It's only a reminder. Picked up is already saved, and Not now is fine.

OFFICE
- **Receipt column on Bid Items → Orders.** Each Home Depot, ACE and Amazon row shows Receipt (green), No receipt (orange), or Not a PO # when the number on the card isn't a store PO on that job.
  Try: open Bid Items on a job with store runs and look for orange No receipt rows.
