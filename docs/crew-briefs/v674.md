APP UPDATE v674

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v674.

FIXED on the crew calendar (office)

- Someone on leave in Simpro now shows as Off on the calendar, even if their time-off request in the app isn't approved yet. Before, they showed as open.
- A job put back on the calendar after being taken off now stays put.

CHANGED

- Warranty work books to the job's Warranty cost center. If Finish or Rough In is already invoiced, the booking goes to Warranty instead and the preview says so. If the job has no open Warranty cost center, Book tells you to add one in Simpro.
- A foreman, junior foreman or lead whose partner is off that day can be scheduled alone. A crew member or apprentice is never scheduled alone.
- Duet (Koy's assistant) can now change a block on the calendar, but only after Koy says yes to a preview.

No job data is changed by this update.
