APP UPDATE v644

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v644.

NEW (Quick jobs: Bid items and Material orders)

- Open any quick job. Under Material there are now two new folded sections: Bid items and Material orders.
- Bid items is the job's Simpro bid, the same one you see on a full job's Bid Items tab. Tap it to see every cost center and line, and what's required vs assigned.
- Material orders is the same Material Tracking as the Rough and Finish tabs. Tap New order from the bid, pick the cost center, pick what you need, and Send to CED. The PO number lands on the card.
- Shortcut: with Bid items open, tap Order from the bid at the top. It opens the order screen for you.
- Both need the Simpro job # filled in on the quick job (Job details). If it's blank you'll see a note saying so.

No job data was changed by this update.
