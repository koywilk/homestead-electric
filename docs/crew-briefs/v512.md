# Crew brief — App update v512 (2026-10-06)

App update v512. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings says v512. A phone fix: wide lists inside a job scroll sideways again, and Bid Items shows full names.

  Try: open a job, tap Bid Items, open the Wire & cable — whole job box and drag it to the right.
- Swiping between tabs still works. Swipe on the open part of the page to change tabs. If you start the swipe on a list that scrolls sideways, it scrolls the list instead of changing tabs.
