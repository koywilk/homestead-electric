# Crew brief — App update v510 (2026-10-05)

App update v510. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings says v510. This covers v509 and v510: Bid Items moved to its own tab, you can drag files onto any upload button on a computer, and notifications show up more reliably.

EVERYONE (RESI AND COMMERCIAL)
- Bid Items has its own tab (Koy's request). It is the tab right after Plans & Links on every job. Same Simpro bid as before: search a part or task, Required vs Assigned, the whole-job wire box, Refresh stock. If you still go to Plans & Links out of habit, there is a line at the top that jumps you over.
  Try: open any job with a Simpro number, tap Bid Items, search "wire".
- Drag files onto upload buttons (Koy's request). On a computer or iPad, drag photos or files from your desktop or a folder and drop them on any spot that has an upload or add-photo button: Plans & Links files, punch and QC items, job notes, return trips, My Day tasks, and the rest. While you drag, a blue dashed box shows the area and the button that gets the files lights up, with a label saying where they're going. Let go and they upload like you picked them.
  Try: open a job, go to Plans & Links, drag a PDF from your desktop onto the tab.
- A photos-only spot won't take a PDF; it tells you instead. A spot inside a folded section only takes a drop once that section is open. Phones can't drag files, so on a phone it works like always.
- Notifications. A notification now shows as a banner even when you have the app open, each one gets its own banner instead of replacing the last one on the same job, and tapping it opens the right job or task and marks it read in the bell. The red count on the bell and the app icon now matches what is unread.

REMOVED OR CHANGED
- The Bid Items section is no longer at the top of Plans & Links. It moved to its own tab next door.
