# Crew brief — App update v509 (2026-10-05)

App update v509. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings says v509. Then close the app all the way and open it again so the new notification setup takes over.

EVERYONE (RESI AND COMMERCIAL)
- Notifications show up even with the app open: a push used to pop up only when the app was closed. On phones, if you had the app open you got a small box inside the app and nothing else. Now you get the normal phone notification either way.
  Try: keep the app open on your phone and have someone assign you a punch item or a task. You should get a real notification.
- Tapping a notification takes you to the item: tapping a task, My Day or Huddle notification while the app was already open used to just bring the app forward. Now it opens the task or job it's about, every time.
  Try: tap the next task notification you get and check it lands on that task.
- Tapping a notification clears it from the bell: once you tap it, it shows as read in the bell, so the red number stays honest.
- The bell number is right, and it's on the app icon: the red count on the bell counts every unread notification, not just the newest 50. On phones that support it, the same number shows on the app's Home Screen icon.
  Try: look at the bell number, then the app icon.

FIXES
- iPhones that kept losing notifications: with the app open, iPhones were quietly treating some pushes as empty and turning notifications off for the app after a few. That should stop. If notifications are already off on your iPhone, open the app, go to Settings, Notifications, and tap Enable notifications once.
- The Notification Doctor's phone test: the "test the phone itself" button in Settings always failed on Android and iPhone. It works now, and the Doctor lists your last 10 notifications and whether each one reached your phone.

Anything sent to you is always saved in the bell, even if your phone never buzzes. If you think you missed something, check the bell first.
