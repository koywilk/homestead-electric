APP UPDATE v682

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v682.

NEW on the crew calendar (office)

- **A what-if view: "If all start on estimate".** A new button in the calendar's top bar lays every upcoming job that has an estimated start date out on that date, even though the start isn't confirmed. A job with no foreman gets the least-booked crew, marked as a guess. A banner shows how many idle crew hours there are over the next 4 weeks now, and with those jobs started. It is a look only: nothing can be changed or booked while it is on, and "Back to the real calendar" returns to normal. Koy's request.
Try: tap "If all start on estimate" in the calendar's top bar, then "Show what was added".

- **"Why it's here" now says where a job's hours came from.** It says whether the hours left are from the bid, worked out from the stage percent because the hours used ran out, or a guess (a week when there are no bid hours). A return trip says whether its time was set on the trip or is the 4-hour default guess.
- **Duet can now explain why a job is, or isn't, on the calendar.** It gets the reason in plain words and what a person could change. It only reads; it changes nothing.

FIXED on the crew calendar (office)

- **Duet no longer counts a company holiday as a day everyone is idle.** Its calendar read listed the whole crew as open on Thanksgiving. It now marks the holiday and lists nobody open that day.

- **Two people with the same first name no longer look like one.** Where the calendar shows first names, two people named James now show as James C. and James L. Names only one person has stay the same.
- **A return trip time like "1 hr 30 min" now counts as an hour and a half.** It was counting as one hour, so the trip got less time than it needed.
- **Two people can't book the same person on the same day at the same moment.** If it happens, the second one is told to wait a minute and look at the new preview, and nothing is written.

No job data is changed by this update.
