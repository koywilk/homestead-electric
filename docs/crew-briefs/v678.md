APP UPDATE v678

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v678.

NEW on the crew calendar (office)

- **Each person on a job can have their own time.** People can now be on the same job for part of the day. Say two guys finish a short visit somewhere and join a crew at 11:30: they go on the job 11:30 to 5, and everyone already there keeps their 7 to 5. Their time shows next to their name in the job box and on the block. Koy's request.
Try: tap a job on a day, then tap Time next to a name and set that person's Start and Finish. Same as the job puts them back.

CHANGED

- Full day, Half day and Custom in the job box now set everyone on that day at once.
- Picking someone who is busy for part of the day now adds just them for the hours they're free, without changing anyone else's time.
- Book in Simpro books each person at their own times, and overtime counts each person's own hours.
- A crew member or apprentice left alone on a job for part of the day now shows under Needs you, the same as a whole day alone.

No job data is changed by this update.
