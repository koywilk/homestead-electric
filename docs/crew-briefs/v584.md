APP UPDATE v584

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v584.

QUICK JOBS

- "New" is now "Needs to be scheduled." Koy's request. Quick jobs that need a date have an orange band across the top and show how many days they've been waiting. The Job Board section has the new name too, with an orange count, and the longest wait is listed first.
  Try: open the Job Board and find Quick Jobs, Needs to Be Scheduled.

- The card shows the work. You can read the scope, address, customer, who's on it and the access note without opening the job. If nobody is on it, it says "No one assigned." A late job turns red and today's jobs turn solid blue.

- One button for the next step. Schedule, Start job, Mark complete, Invoice sent. Schedule asks for the date and crew right on the card. If you tap the wrong one, use Undo on the bar at the bottom.
  Try: tap Schedule on a card, pick a date, save. It moves to Scheduled.

- Delete and the status note moved into the ⋯ menu on the card. The ⋯ menu also has "Start now without a date" and "Already done: mark complete" for small jobs that never got scheduled.

- Opening a quick job looks different. It reads like a page now, with the date, crew, scope and how to get there at the top. Tap Edit on a section to change it. Material, Notes, Photos and Job details start folded, so tap to open them. Call, Directions and Add photo are at the top, and the next step stays at the bottom of the screen.
  Try: open a quick job and tap Add photo, then tap the photo to see it full size. Delete is in there now.

Nothing else changed in this one. Regular jobs and temp peds are not affected.
