APP UPDATE v575

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v575.

EVERYONE (RESIDENTIAL AND COMMERCIAL)

- Milestones on every job. Job Info has a new Milestones section. It starts folded and empty. Tap + Add, type what happened in the field ("Blockouts in", "UFR in"), and tap Add. It's stamped with your name and the date. No due dates. If you add one by mistake, Remove shows an Undo for a few seconds.
  Try: open any job, Job Info, Milestones, add today's milestone.

- Plans & Links starts folded. Each block shows what's in it before you open it (Drive Plans · 14 files, Links · 3 of 4 set). The folders inside Drive Plans start closed too. Tap a block or a folder to open it.

PROJECTS: SEVERAL BUILDINGS ON ONE JOB (the office sets these up)

- A project groups buildings. Each building is its own job with its own punch list, questions, photos, crew, POs and milestones. On the Job Board there's a PROJECTS group at the top. Tap it to see the buildings, then tap a building like any job. Searching a building name or the project's Simpro number opens it.
  Try: once Battle Creek is set up, search "Building A".

- Inside a building the line at the top reads PROJECT › BUILDING, with two buttons to hop to the building before and after. Tap the project name for the project page.

- Office: open the job, Job Info, Project, Make this a project. That job becomes the project's "Site / Shared" building and keeps everything it already has. Then on the project page tap + Add Building, name it, pick its Simpro folder, and choose which tabs it shows.

- Subcontractors see only the buildings they're on.

COMMERCIAL ONLY

- The nine GC schedule dates (temp power through final) moved out of Milestones into their own section on Job Info, GC Schedule Dates. Same dates, same reminders.

- Job Sections can now switch off Materials, Job Start, Power, Lighting, Gear, Underground, Gear & Submittals and RFIs on a job that doesn't need them. It asks first if the tab has anything in it, and nothing is deleted. Switching off Gear & Submittals also stops that job's gear reminders.

COMING NEXT

- Each building's own Drive folder, and plans sorted into the right building automatically. Until then, plans stay on the project's main folder.
