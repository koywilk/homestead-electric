APP UPDATE v564

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v564. Nothing changes on the crew app this time; this one is for the office.

OFFICE ONLY

- Dashboard › Job Costs: rough and finish numbers counted the way the job was sold. Every cost center counts, the base bid and every change order, and each item goes to rough or finish by the "(Rough In)" or "(Finish)" at the end of its name. Before, rough only counted the Base Rough In cost center, so a job with extras looked over even when it wasn't: Pierce read 129% of its rough hours, and counted fully it is 94%. Pick Hours, Materials, Labor $ or Margin, and All jobs, Active or Invoiced. Margin is total profit over total sales, with the plain average next to it.
Try: open homestead-electric.vercel.app/dashboard, tap Job Costs, then tap a job to see how each of its cost centers splits.

- By foreman and By lead: the right side adds up each person's jobs. Tap a name and the boxes at the top turn into that person's numbers, with the company's under each. A foreman counts only when they are titled Foreman on Team Members; a job with a jr foreman or lead in the foreman field shows as No foreman with the name next to it, so fix the foreman on the job to move it.
Try: tap Keegan, then Colby, and compare their rough margin to the company's.

- What-if: untick Count on any job to leave it out of the totals and the foreman numbers, so you can say "without this one your number would be this." A bar at the top lists what's off, with Turn all back on. Nothing in Simpro or the app changes.
Try: tap a foreman, untick their worst job, and watch their boxes change.

- The numbers come from Simpro every night. The first full pull needs a few runs, so give it a night or press Pull from Simpro now a few times.
