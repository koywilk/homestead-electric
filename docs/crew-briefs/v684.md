APP UPDATE v684

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v684.

FIXED on Job Prep (office)

- **Temp peds don't disappear on a missed tap.** Tapping the TEMP PED chip anywhere now opens its menu instead of flipping the ped on or off. Before, missing the little arrow could clear the Ped # and drop the ped off Temp Peds Out.
Try: tap the word TEMP PED on a row. The menu opens and nothing changes until you pick.

NEW on Job Prep

- **Every job is on the board.** Quick jobs (with or without a job #), temp ped pickups and temp ped jobs now show in Office Admin until they're done, each with a small tag by its name.

NEW on the Foreman Meeting (office and foremen)

- **Each job's reports open right from the meeting.** Under each job in Jobs, tap **Reports** to see that job's reports, the newest Job Report first, then Margin Watch and post-rough-in. Tap one to read it right there. Reports show percentages and hours only, never dollar figures. Koy's request.
Try: on the Foreman Meeting, tap Reports under a job, then tap its Job Report.

FIXED on the crew calendar (admins)

- **Book in Simpro handles split days and the right cost center.** It adds a second piece of a day to the same Simpro schedule instead of failing, and books to the cost center the work belongs to (a change order's own, for example). When more than one could fit, the job box asks which one, once per job.
Try: book a job that has a change order section and check the cost center it names before you tap Book.

No job data is changed by this update.
