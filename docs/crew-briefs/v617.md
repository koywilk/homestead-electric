CHANGED (anyone who logs inspections)

- Tapped Pass or Fail by mistake? Tap the right one the same day. It now corrects that day's attempt instead of adding another one.
- Next to Pass / Fail on the rough (4-way) and final inspections there's a new History button. It lists every attempt. Tap Remove on one that was logged by mistake. It asks first, and Undo brings it back. The Pass / Fail result and the failed items don't change.

Real repeat failures still go through Failed again, same as before.

No job data was changed by this update.
