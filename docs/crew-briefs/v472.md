# Crew brief — App update v472 (2026-09-30)

App update v472. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings says v472.

EVERYONE (resi and commercial)
- Completed tab on every job: last tab on the card. Every need or task finished on that job, who did it, when, who asked, photos.
- Status update history: under the Status Update box on Job Info, tap History to see old updates with date, time and who wrote them.
- Questions on My Day: a job question assigned to you now shows in your Mine list under Questions. Tap to answer or mark Done.
- Mark anything urgent: every My Day row has a "!" button. Tap it and the row goes red and moves to the top for everyone. Tap again to clear.
- Tools tab: new tab at the top with Josh's generator sizing calculator. Jobs with a generator also get a "Size the generator" link on Home Runs.
- Panelized Lighting: picking a load for a zone now has a search bar and is grouped by floor and room.

FIXES
- Panel schedule fill could list one load twice and drop another when the panel was too small. Fixed. Hit Re-fill if a schedule looks wrong.
- Panelized Lighting loads could get rolled back by an old copy on another device. Fixed.

COMMERCIAL ONLY
- New tabs on the job card: Power, Lighting, Gear, Underground (Underground splits into Utility work, Building site work, Building underground). Each has status, dates, notes, a checklist that records who checked what, and photos.
- Blue Stakes on Job Info: ticket number, called-in date, expiry. Green while good, amber in the last 3 days, red EXPIRED after.
- Job type: Ground-up, TI or Commercial service on Job Info.
- Job Start can be turned off per job (TI and service jobs): Job Info, bottom, Job Sections. Nothing is deleted.
- Notes and documents on each Job Start step: tap the small note mark on the right edge of any step chip.
- Gear systems now include Lighting package, Vaults, Light poles.

REMOVED OR CHANGED
- QC tab is gone. The tracker lives inside My Day under QC walks (Koy and Josh).
- Commercial mode shows nothing residential. Flip to RESI for that.
- Team pulse lists everyone, including people with nothing on them.

Send what you find to Koy: which screen, which job, what you expected. Screenshots help.
