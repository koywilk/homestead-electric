APP UPDATE v661

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v661.

NEW on the crew calendar

- Nobody can be double-booked anymore. If someone is already on a job at that time, or off, they're grayed out and can't be added, and Simpro booking refuses it too.
- Jobs in progress that ran past their bid hours, like Ashcraft and Rose, show on the calendar again.
- Tap a job on the calendar and the box is just about that day: who's on it that day, and the time. Pick Full day (7 to 5), Half day (7 to 12) or Custom to set your own start and finish, like 7 to 4.
- Changing who's on a job or its time that day leaves its other days alone. Booking in Simpro books each day with that day's people.
- When you remove a job, it opens its spot on the list so you can see where it went.
- The lists on the left start closed. Tap a type to open it.
- A Full screen button at the top right of the calendar makes it fill the whole screen, so you see more of the week.
- Exit full screen, or press Esc on a keyboard, to go back.
- Everything works the same in full screen: dragging, tapping a job, the day checklist and booking.

No job data is changed by this update. Booking in Simpro still shows every entry before anything is written.
