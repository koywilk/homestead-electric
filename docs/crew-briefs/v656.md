Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v656.

NEW for the office: the crew calendar

- Dashboard › Meetings and Forecast › Calendar now show a real calendar: a week view with each day's jobs, and a Month view.
- Solid jobs are booked in Simpro. Dashed jobs are laid out by the auto-schedule. Striped red ones need a call.
- Drag a job to another day to move it. On a phone, press and hold it, then tap the day.
- Tap a job to change its days or who's on it, or to copy it to other days. Changes save for everyone right away.
- Don't want a job on there? Tap it, then Remove. It stays on the list as needing scheduling until you put it back.

HOW THE AUTO-SCHEDULE WORKS

- Confirmed start dates first, then jobs already going, then rough-ins before finishes. Monday to Thursday, 10 hour days, no holidays.
- Crews come from the Crew Board: 4 on a job when we have them. When a foreman has two jobs, the jr foreman leads the second one.
- Unassigned people are never scheduled. Nothing goes to Simpro until someone books it.

No job data is changed by this update.
