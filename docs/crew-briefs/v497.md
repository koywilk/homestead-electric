# Crew brief — App update v497 (2026-10-02)

App update v497. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings says v497. This one is all in the Tools tab: the three tools can now fill each other in, so you stop retyping the same appliances three times.

EVERYONE (RESI AND COMMERCIAL)
- Appliance Loads feeds the calculators (Koy's request). Open a job in Appliance Loads and its appliances, plus the job's whole Home Runs list, are ready for the other two tools on that phone or computer. Lighting and outlet circuits get counted, not sized; a dedicated circuit the sheet has no specs for comes in at a typical value with a flag, so fix those first. New buttons on the job page: Send to Generator Sizing, and Send to Service Size for the people who have that tool. Tapping one jumps you to that tool.
  Try: Tools, Appliance Loads, tap a job, tap Send to Generator Sizing.
- Fill from another tool. Generator Sizing and Service Size each have a new Start from another tool box near the top. The button names the job and how many loads it has, like "Fill from Appliance Loads · #1438 Miller Residence · 14 loads". Nothing changes until you tap it, and Undo puts back what was there.
  Try: after sending a job over, tap the Fill button and watch the loads list fill in with the real volts and amps.
- What landed is marked. Anything a fill set is tagged "loads" or "generator" so you can see where a number came from. A load the sheet had no amps for comes in with a typical value and a flag, and one whose spec the sheet could not confirm says "confirm spec"; fix those first.
- Service Size counts the odd ones too. Appliances that have no row of their own (a kiln, a 240 V heater) show up under From another tool in the Extras list and count toward the service. Fridges, microwaves, hoods and the first dishwasher are listed as already covered, so they are never counted twice.
- The two calculators talk both ways. Service Size can send its house and loads to Generator Sizing (Send to Generator Sizing, next to Copy bid note) and gives it the service size for the transfer switch pick. Generator Sizing can send square footage and circuit counts back (Open in Service Size on the generator page).
