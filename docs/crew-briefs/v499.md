# Crew brief — App update v499 (2026-10-02)

App update v499. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings says v499. One addition to the Tools tab, for the people who have it.

EVERYONE WITH THE TOOLS TAB
- Generator Sizing can pull a job's generator loads (Koy's request). Above the generator tool there is a new bar: pick the job, tap Pull. It reads what that job's Generator Load Selection has marked on the generator and gives you a Fill from Generator Selection button in the tool. Tap it and only the loads that ride the generator land, in the homeowner's order, with real amps where the appliance sheet has them and a flag where it doesn't. The transfer switch flips to Essential circuits when only part of the house is on it.
  Try: Tools, Generator Sizing, pick Miller in the bar, Pull, then Fill from Generator Selection. Type the square footage the generator serves; the job doesn't know that number.

REMOVED OR CHANGED
- Nothing removed. Service Size does not get this button on purpose: a service has to carry the whole house, so it keeps filling from Appliance Loads.
