APP UPDATE v585

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v585.

JOB BOARD

- The Crew Schedule is easier to read. Full job names now show instead of getting cut off. On a computer the week fills the screen. On a phone it still scrolls sideways.
  Try: open the Job Board and find a job with a long name in the Crew Schedule.

- Each schedule card shows who's on it at a glance. The colored strip across the top shows which crews are on that job. The small bar under each name shows when that person was there, from 5 AM to 9 PM, with a tick at noon.
  Try: pick a job with two crews on it and look at the strip.

- Some cards say "No job card on this board." That means Simpro has the time but no job on this board matches it, so the card doesn't open. Tell Koy if a job you need shows up this way.

- Pipeline numbers changed, and that's on purpose. The top row and the All Jobs list used to count differently, so they disagreed. Now they match. Rough and Finish include their On Hold jobs.
  Try: tap a number in the Pipeline row. The list that opens has that many jobs.

- Crew cards are all the same shape now. Tap a card to open that foreman's page. Crew Access is still at the bottom of the card.

Nothing else changed. No job data was touched. This is only how the board looks and counts.

Send what you find to Koy: which screen, which job, what you expected. Screenshots help.
