# Crew brief — App update v549 (2026-10-16)

App update v549. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings shows v549. This one is about the app telling you what just happened, instead of doing something big and staying quiet.

EVERYONE (RESI AND COMMERCIAL)
- **No signal is calm now.** Lose signal inside a job and you'll see an **Offline** chip at the top of the job, and the pill at the top of the screen says "Saved on this phone · 1 job waiting". Your changes are safe and go up when signal comes back; you'll see "Back online · (job) synced". Red "Save failed" now only shows when a save really was refused, and tapping it tries again.
  Try: next time you're in a basement with no bars, make a change and watch the pill. It should stay calm, not flash red.
- **Time off answers come to you.** When the office approves your time off or doesn't, you get a notification with the answer. No more checking the Time Off tab.
  Try: next time you ask for a day off, wait for the notification.
- **Office: approving time off.** Approve and Deny are big buttons with Undo for ten seconds, and a decided request has **Change to approved / not approved**. The head of residential and admins approve, the same on the Time Off page and on My Day.

RESIDENTIAL ONLY
- **Stage % says what it did.** Change the Rough or Finish % and a short note shows up under it, like "Ready to invoice: on. The office and Keegan get a push. Matterport scan: needed now." The dark bar at the bottom has **Undo** for ten seconds. Pick 100% while punch is still open and the note says how many are open, with **Show open punch** to jump to the list.
  Try: on one of your jobs, move the Rough % one step, read the note, then tap Undo.
- **QC verdict asks once, plainly.** On QC Fail or Passed with Items, a sheet asks who fixes the walk's items: **Make a return trip** or **Crew on site has it**. Tapping outside doesn't count as an answer anymore, and **Go back** keeps the old verdict. If the job already has an open QC trip, it offers to add the new items to that trip.
  Try: office, next QC fail, read the sheet before you tap.
- **Return trip texts have the date.** Text Trip and Email Trip now say "Scheduled for Tue, Oct 27" (the date on the trip card), or "Schedule by…" if it still needs a date.
  Try: open a return trip that has a date, tap Text Trip, check the second line.
- **Photo didn't upload? It says so.** A punch or QC walk photo that fails shows a message and a red **Didn't upload · Retry** on the item. Tap it once you have signal.

FIXES
- Every return trip text used to say "Scheduled: not yet set", even with a date on the card.
- Moving a stage back below 85% now turns Ready to invoice off again.
- Moving the % on a job that's on hold keeps it on hold instead of flipping it to In progress.
- A rough QC fail no longer drags finish punch onto the rough trip.
- "Tap to retry" on a failed save now actually retries.

REMOVED OR CHANGED
- The dark "Update ready · tap to reload" bar is gone. The **A new version is ready** card is the only update prompt, and it saves your work first.
- "Denied" on time off now reads "not approved".
