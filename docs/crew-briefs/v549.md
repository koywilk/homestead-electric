# Crew brief — App update v549 (2026-10-16)

App update v549. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings says v549. This one is about the app telling you what just happened, instead of doing something big and staying quiet.

FIELD
- **Stage % says what it did.** Change the Rough or Finish % and a short note appears under it saying what that turned on, like "Ready to invoice: on. The office and Keegan get a push. Matterport scan: needed now." The dark bar at the bottom has **Undo** for ten seconds.
  Try: on one of your jobs, move the Rough % one step, read the note, then tap Undo.
- **100% with punch still open.** Instead of a warning that disappears, the note says how many items are open and **Show open punch** opens the list.
- **On hold stays on hold.** Moving the % on a job that's on hold no longer flips it to In progress. Take the hold off in the status when you're back on the job.
- **Return trip texts have the date.** Text Trip and Email Trip now say "Scheduled for Tue, Oct 27" (the date on the trip card), or "Schedule by…" if it still needs a date. Before, every trip said "Scheduled: not yet set".
  Try: open a return trip that has a date and tap Text Trip; check the second line.
- **No signal is calm now.** Inside a job you'll see an **Offline** chip, and the pill at the top says "Saved on this phone · 1 job waiting". Your changes are safe on the phone and go up when signal comes back; you'll see "Back online · (job) synced". You'll only see red "Save failed" when the server actually refused a save, and tapping it really tries again now.
- **Photo didn't upload? It says so.** A punch or QC walk photo that fails now shows a message and a red **Didn't upload · Retry** on the item. Tap it when you have signal.
- **Time off answers come to you.** When the office approves or doesn't approve your request, you get a notification. No more checking the Time Off tab.

OFFICE
- **QC verdict asks once, plainly.** On QC Fail or Passed with Items, a sheet asks who fixes the walk's items: **Make a return trip** or **Crew on site has it**. Tapping outside no longer counts as "crew on site has it". **Go back** leaves the old verdict. A rough walk only puts rough QC items on the trip. If the job already has an open QC trip, the sheet offers to add the new items to that trip instead of silently doing nothing. Every answer ends in a message.
- **Below 85% turns Ready to invoice off.** If a stage goes back under 85%, Ready to invoice clears (unless the other phase still needs invoicing).
- **Time off: one approver rule.** Head of residential and admins approve, on the Time Off page and on My Day. Approve and Deny are big buttons with Undo, decided requests have **Change to approved / not approved**, and the person always gets a notification.

REMOVED OR CHANGED
- The dark "Update ready · tap to reload" bar: removed. The **A new version is ready** card is the only update prompt, and it saves your work first.
- "Denied" on time off now reads "not approved".
