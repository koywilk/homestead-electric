APP UPDATE v662

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v662.

FIXED on the crew calendar

- The auto-schedule can no longer put someone on a job when they're already booked somewhere else that day. Two rare cases could still do it: a job that started late after a return trip and ran into an extra day, and the days laid out after a job's last Simpro booking.
- On those days after a Simpro booking, anyone off or already booked elsewhere comes off that day, and the hours move on to the next day.

No job data is changed by this update. Nothing books in Simpro until you preview it and tap Book.
