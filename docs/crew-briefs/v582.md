APP UPDATE v582

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v582.

OFFICE ONLY (the /dashboard page, admins)

- People tab: everyone by crew, with where they are today. The list is now grouped the way Settings › Team is: a crew is a foreman and the people under him, with the foreman on top, then jr foremen, leads and crew. Office and No crew are their own groups at the bottom. Every group starts folded with a count, and the header tells you how many of them are on a job today and how many have gone quiet. Open a crew and each row shows: who they are, the job they are on today (from the Crew Planner, with the hours), when they were last on a job, and the app version on their phone. "Not scheduled" means the planner has nothing for them today. Tap anyone for their panel, same as before.
  Try: open /dashboard, People, open Daegan's crew. Check the Today column against the Crew Planner.

- People tab: the clutter is gone. The activity heat map, the "On the app, no job touched" list and the My Day load bars are removed. Time off waiting, Phones on latest, Quiet 3+ days and Out this week are still there. The device list is now a folded "Devices behind" with a count on the right; open it to see which phones need an update, or tap Show all.

- Automations tab: shorter. The automated docs with their Run now buttons and the plans waiting (File it / Not ours) are unchanged. The Appliance Loads card is one line now: when the nightly pull last ran, when the next one is, how many jobs and items are on the sheet, and an Open sheet button. The per-job appliance table, the "When they run" list and the plans-filed chart are gone. The two appliance tiles at the top still open their lists. Nothing about what the automations do has changed.

Nothing changed for the crew app. Jobs, My Day, the Crew Planner and the punch lists are exactly as they were.
