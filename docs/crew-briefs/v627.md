APP UPDATE v627

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v627.

EVERYONE

- On Schedule / Ongoing pill keeps itself right. On a job that is In Progress, the small pill next to the Rough or Finish status says whether a crew is booked. It now reads Simpro's schedule by itself every few minutes, so it is right without tapping the little refresh arrow. It is the same on the job page, the job card, the Job Board and the Crew Planner.
  On Schedule = the job is on Simpro's schedule some time in the next 14 days.
  Ongoing = it is not.
  Try: open a job you know is booked this week. The pill should say On Schedule without touching anything.

- Tapping the pill. Tap it to flag Needs Sched when a job needs a crew and is not booked. Tap again to clear it. The flag clears itself once the job is on Simpro's schedule. If the job is already on Simpro's schedule, the tap tells you so: to change it, change the schedule in Simpro.

FIXES

- The Crew Planner, Forecast and Huddle could miss scheduled days when a lot was booked ahead, because the app only read the newest 250 schedule entries. It now asks Simpro for the exact dates it needs.

REMOVED OR CHANGED

- Nothing removed. The refresh arrow is still there if you want to force a re-read.
- You can no longer set On Schedule by hand on an In Progress job. It follows Simpro.
