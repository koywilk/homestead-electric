# Crew brief — App update v539 (2026-10-13)

App update v539. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings says v539.

EVERYONE (RESI AND COMMERCIAL)
- **Working on it.** Any task or need on your My Day now has a "Working on it" button. Tap it when you start, and whoever sent it sees a blue "Working · your name" tag on it. They don't get a buzz; it just shows up where they already look. Tap again to stop. Checking it off, or handing it to someone else, clears it.
  Try: open My Day, open Tasks on me, tap Working on it on one you're doing today.
- **See where your asks stand.** On My Day, the Sent list now shows each thing you asked for as Working (with who), Waiting on (with what), or Not started yet.
  Try: open My Day, open Sent.
- **Sort inside your lists.** My Day has a new Sort switch next to the view buttons: Urgency (the normal order), Job, or Person. Job lines your tasks up job by job, and Person lines them up by who sent them, with a small label over each bunch. Your phone remembers the choice.
  Try: My Day, tap Sort: Job, open Tasks on me.
- **Anything someone is working on now sits at the top** of every list, above urgent.

OFFICE (THE NEEDS TAB)
- **Group by job or person.** The Needs tab has a Group switch: Due (the Tomorrow and This week lanes, as before), Job, or Person. Job and Person fold everything into one group per job or person, each showing how many are working, urgent or waiting. Under All, Person means who it's on; under Mine, it means who sent it to you.
  Try: Needs, tap All, then Group: Person.
- **Working on it** shows on Needs cards too. You can start or stop it on cards that are on you.
- The Tomorrow and This week lanes are now in order: working first, then urgent, then oldest.

CHANGED
- The Mine / All buttons on the Needs tab are easier to read (white on blue).

The "?" guides for My Day and Needs are updated.
