# Crew brief — App update v546 (2026-10-15)

App update v546. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings says v546. This one is about taps that used to be final: a bunch of things now give you ten seconds to undo, and a few things that could quietly write the wrong data can't any more.

FIELD
- **Undo bar.** After a delete or a move, a dark bar at the bottom says what happened with an **Undo** button for ten seconds. Same bar My Day already uses. You'll see it after deleting a photo, removing a link, deleting a need, changing a job's foreman, and a few office actions.
  Try: open a photo on a punch item, tap Delete photo, then tap Undo on the bar. The photo comes back.
- **Deleting a photo.** The little × on the corner of thumbnails is gone (gloved thumbs kept hitting it). Tap the photo so it opens full screen, then **Delete photo**. PDFs and files have a **Remove** link under them. Both give you Undo.
- **Cancel on the punch sheet means cancel.** If you typed something and tap Cancel, it asks "Discard this item?" with Keep editing / Discard. Discard adds nothing and orders nothing. Before, Cancel saved the item anyway.
- **Change orders: one New change order sheet.** Tap + New change order, fill it in, tap **Submit**. Nothing is saved and nobody is pushed until Submit. Before, the add button saved a blank CO at the top of the list and pushed Jeromy before you typed a word.
- **Change orders: status is the office's.** The row of seven status pills is gone for crew. You see one pill with where it stands. Finished the work? Say so on My Day and the office marks it complete. (Approved and Completed push people and make invoice tasks, so they're office calls.)
- **Sign-in on a new phone with bad signal.** You'll see "Loading the team…" with a Retry button instead of only Koy's name. Your PIN is saved before you're let in; if the save is refused it tells you and asks you to reload.
- **Drafts survive a tap outside.** Typed a need or a reply and brushed the dark area? It asks "Discard this draft?" instead of throwing it away.

OFFICE
- **Crew Board and Team members keep your work when a save is refused.** If someone else saved the team first, a red "Not saved" note appears, your moves or your typed new person stay on screen, and **Save again** puts them on top of the new list. Before, they vanished.
- **The team list can't shrink by accident.** The app and the database both refuse a save that drops two or more people at once (removing one person still works). This closes the hole where a phone with an unloaded list could write a one-person team over everyone.
- **Change order status: one button, big picker, Undo.** Tap the status pill on a CO, pick from a sheet that says what each status does, and the bar gives you ten seconds to undo.
- **Confirm boxes say what they do.** Restore 4 jobs, Reset counts, Unlink folder, Revoke link, Erase & resend, Delete forever. No more red "Delete" on things that weren't deletes.
- **Upcoming → Start as job.** One button (managers only) opens a sheet showing what carries over: the street address (City only as a fallback), projected rough start and whether it's confirmed, GC, foreman, photos. Then Move to Job Board or Make a Quote instead, with Undo. Before, the ✓ put the city in the address field and dropped the rest.
- **Foreman changes happen on Job Info.** The dimmed "→ Name" buttons on every Job Board row are gone. Pick the foreman on Job Info; Undo bar.
- **Remove a person** now asks "Permanently delete {name}?" with a Delete forever button and reminds you Deactivate is for layoffs.
- **Plan changes** (Panelized Lighting): deleting a logged change gives Undo; Add item waits until a location is typed.

HOMEOWNER LINK
- **Generator page reviews before sending.** The homeowner picks, taps Review my picks (it won't send an empty list), sees the list once more, signs, and sends. Afterwards the page shows what they picked, when, and a **Change my picks** button. Reset & resend on your side is now **Erase & resend** and says whose signed picks it erases.
- **Office number on the thank-you page.** It's blank until Koy fills in `HE_OFFICE_PHONE`; until then the page says to reply to the message that sent the link.

REMOVED OR CHANGED
- Per-row "→ Name" foreman buttons on the Job Board: removed.
- Thumbnail corner × on photos: removed (delete from the full-screen view).
- Crew status pills on change orders: removed (office sets status).
- Blank CO on "+ Add Change Order": removed (sheet + Submit instead).
