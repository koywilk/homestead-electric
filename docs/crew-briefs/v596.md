APP UPDATE v596

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v596.

FOR THE CREW

- A PO made in Simpro or Simpro Mobile shows up on the job by itself within about 5 minutes. You don't have to type the number on a card.
  Try: make your next PO in Simpro Mobile, then open that job's Material Tracking. Look for the card marked Simpro.
- If you already typed the number on a card, nothing changes on that card. If you wrote a card with no number and the same list, the number gets filled in.
  If you see two cards for one order, tap Remove on the one you don't need. It stays gone.
- When a receipt goes on the PO in Simpro, that card gets Picked Up, by Simpro, within the hour. Uncheck it if that's wrong and it stays unchecked.
- ACE is now in the Source list on a PO card.

FOR THE OFFICE (ADMINS)

- Warranty, Generators and other cost centers that aren't Rough or Finish land on the Finish list with the cost center as a label. Any PO on a commercial job lands on its Materials tab.
- A PO voided in Simpro stays on its card with a Voided in Simpro label. Nothing is deleted.
- POs on a job that isn't in the app, or on a Simpro number two job cards share, don't show until that's fixed.
- Cards sent from the app still get marked Picked Up by hand.

No job data was overwritten. It only adds cards and fills in blanks.
