# Crew brief — App update v493 (2026-10-01)

App update v493. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings says v493. If you hit a black screen earlier today, that was v490 and it's fixed: force-close the app, reopen, pull to refresh. This one is all My Day: you can see a question's discussion right on the row, and anything new since your last look is called out at the top and on every dropdown header.

EVERYONE (resi and commercial)
- See the discussion on a question (Koy's request): a Question row on My Day now shows the latest message from that question's discussion underneath it, with a "N in discussion" line you can tap to open the whole thread. Reply still posts into that same thread; Answer still closes the question on the job.
  Try: open My Day, find a Question row, tap "in discussion" to expand it, then Reply and watch your post show up there.
- New since you last looked, at the top (Koy's request): a new group at the top of My Day, "New since <time>", lists everything that showed up or got a fresh reply since the last time you opened My Day on that phone or laptop. It's open by default and disappears when there's nothing new.
  Try: open My Day, close it, have someone send you a task, open it again. It should sit in the New group with a blue New tag.
- The new count on every dropdown (Koy's request): the blue "3 new" badge now shows on each dropdown header, not just the top: Mine, each category inside it (Tasks on me, Punch, Questions, and so on), and each job in the Job view. A header with nothing new shows no badge, so a glance tells you where to look.
  Try: collapse everything on My Day and look for the blue badges on the headers.

FIXES
- Black screen on open (v490, about 25 minutes this afternoon): My Day crashed before it could draw. Fixed in v491 and v492. Nothing was lost; the crash happened before any save could run. v493 adds a check to the build so that kind of bug can't ship again.

REMOVED OR CHANGED
- Nothing removed. The "N new" count moved from only the category headers to every dropdown header.
- Reminder: New is remembered per device. Your phone and your laptop each keep their own last look.
