APP UPDATE v670

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v670.

NEW on the crew calendar (office)

- Duet (Koy's assistant) can now suggest a block on the calendar. It shows with a dotted green outline and says "Duet suggests", with the reason.
- Tap it: Keep it makes it a plan. Remove suggestion drops it. Or change the day, time or who, and it becomes your plan.
- A suggestion books nothing. Nothing goes to Simpro until someone taps Book.
- A suggestion never replaces a block a person placed.
- Duet can also put a job under Waiting with a reason, the same as Remove with a reason. The job's status doesn't change.

No job data is changed by this update.
