APP UPDATE v554

Pull down to refresh. Settings should show v554.

This one is mostly for whoever runs Panelized Lighting in the office. It works with the new FieldInk (v686), so update the iPads that draw lighting on the plans too.

EVERYONE (RESI AND COMMERCIAL)

- **RA3 and regular switch loads now come over from FieldInk.** Before, only panel loads could come over, so an RA3 wallbox job had nothing to bring in. Each load in "Incoming from FieldInk" now has a second chip saying how it's controlled: Panel, RA3, Standard or Tape. Import sends each one where it belongs: panel loads and tape (with its feet) go to Loads, RA3 and Standard go to the new Wallbox Devices list.
  Try: open a Panelized Lighting job that has FieldInk loads, tap Import all, check the toast says where they went.

- **Wallbox Devices = the dimmer and switch order.** New list under Loads. Four counts on top (RA3 dimmers, RA3 switches, Standard dimmers, Standard switches), then floor and room. You can change Dimmer or Switch on any row. If the plan flips one later, it says so in orange.
  Try: open a floor in Wallbox Devices and check the counts against the plan.

- **Keypads fill in from FieldInk.** At the bottom of the FieldInk inbox there's "Keypads from FieldInk". Fill puts each keypad on its floor's Keypads list with its buttons. A floor you already confirmed is left alone. Open a keypad's buttons to type the engraving, and tap Confirm keypad when it's checked.
  Try: tap Fill on a job with keypads drawn in FieldInk, then open one keypad's buttons.

- **Keypad wire, per job.** Under the Keypads heading: "We pull it" or "Not on this job". When we don't pull the low-voltage keypad wire, the keypads stay listed but lose the Pulled box. Every job starts on "We pull it".
  Try: on a job where the LV company pulls keypads, tap "Not on this job".

- **Ran box on Wallbox Devices.** Tick it when the wire to that box is pulled; it shows your name and date and counts on the Loads Ran card like every other load.
  Try: open Loads Ran on an RA3 job and tick one.

FIXES

- **Lutron panels won't take a dimmed load on a switching module.** A 4S8 only switches, so dimmed, ELV, MLV and LED loads can't go on it anymore (it used to just warn). ELV dimming goes on the 4A5.

REMOVED OR CHANGED

- A keypad deleted in FieldInk is not removed from the job. It stays with a red "removed in FieldInk" tag; delete the row yourself if it's really gone.
- An iPad still on an older FieldInk shows "Update FieldInk" on its loads, and those loads wait until it updates.
