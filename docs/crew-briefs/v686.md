APP UPDATE v686

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v686.

NEW for foremen, jr. foremen and leads: My Jobs

- **Your jobs, their job costs and their reports, in one place.** Tap **My Jobs** in the top bar of the app. Foremen see their own jobs and every job their crew runs or leads, grouped by person. Jr. foremen and leads see the jobs they run or lead.
- **Every job starts closed.** Each one shows the job number and name with its margin % and hours used right beside it, then where it's at. Tap it to open **Job costs** (hours, materials, labor and margin as percentages, and the cost centers) and its **Reports** (finish plans, foreman packets, post-rough-in reports, margin watch).
- **No dollar amounts anywhere on it.** Percentages and hours only.
- **Put it on your home screen.** Open My Jobs in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. The new My Jobs icon opens straight to your jobs.

Try: open My Jobs, open one of your jobs, then open Job costs.

No job data is changed by this update.
