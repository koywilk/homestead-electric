APP UPDATE v660

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v660.

NEW on the crew calendar

- Every job on the calendar shows its time, like 7:00 AM–9:00 AM.
- Return trips use their estimated time instead of a whole day, so a pair can do a short trip and then go on to their job the same day.
- Tap a job to set its start time or hours. Booking in Simpro uses that time.
- New blue colors: navy for rough-ins, medium blue for finishes, white with a blue outline for return trips. Held jobs have a gray dashed outline.
- Under each date: how many are on and how many are open, plus the names of who's open. Drag a name onto a job to add them.
- The week is one board: what needs scheduling on the left, the days in the middle.
- Tap a held job to Clear hold. Remove asks why and keeps the job under Waiting until the wait is cleared.
- QC walks, quick jobs, temp peds and scans that need a date now show on the list too.
- Book this week in Simpro books the whole week after showing every entry.
- New Crew week view: everyone on the Crew Board, day by day, showing their jobs, Open or Off.
- Only jobs in progress or with a confirmed start get scheduled on their own. Jobs with only an estimated date wait on the list.

No job data is changed by this update.
