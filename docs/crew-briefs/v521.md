# Crew brief — App update v521 (2026-10-07)

App update v521. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings says v521. One addition, mostly for the office: you can now see whether a link you sent was ever opened.

EVERYONE (RESIDENTIAL AND COMMERCIAL)
- Did they open it? (Koy's request): under the links we send out there is now a small line that tells you if anyone opened it. It reads like "Opened 3 times, 2 devices, last today 9:12 am." For saved links in the Share window (Questions and Punch), a link nobody has opened says "Not opened yet," and if it went out 3 or more days ago and still nothing, the line turns red so you know to call them. The single links (Home Runs, homeowner, lighting, loads, Plan Changes) say "No opens since Oct 7" until someone opens them, then turn green or blue. Tap a line to see when it was first and last opened and on what kind of phone or computer. It never shows a name, just the device.
  You'll see it on: the Share window for Questions and Punch (under each saved link), the Share button on Home Runs, the homeowner generator link, the lighting collab link, Share loads, the Plan Changes page on Lutron jobs, and the share link on a Job Note.
  Try: open a job, tap Share on Home Runs, and look right under the button. Opens from a phone signed into the app don't count, and neither does Preview, so test it from a phone that isn't signed in. On iPhone, the home-screen app and Safari are separate: a link you tap in a text opens in Safari and will count unless you've signed into the app in Safari once.
  Note: links we made before today say "No opens since Oct 7" until someone opens them. That doesn't mean nobody did before. It just means we weren't counting yet.
