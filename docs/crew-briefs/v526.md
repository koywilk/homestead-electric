# Crew brief — App update v526 (2026-10-08)

App update v526. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings says v526. This one makes the app easier to read and tap in the field. Nothing about how you use it changes.

EVERYONE
- **Pinch to zoom works.** You can pinch-zoom anywhere in the app now to read a small number or badge.
Try: open a job and pinch out on the job number.
- **Crew names are easier to read.** Names on the crew schedule and foreman headers that used a bright color (the lime green was the worst) now show in a darker shade of the same color. Your crew keeps its color.
- **Faint gray text is readable.** The light-gray hints, labels and "no items" text were nearly invisible in the sun. They are a darker gray now.
- **Job card tabs are bigger on phones.** Job Info, Activity, Photos and the rest, plus the Close and Refresh buttons, are taller and easier to hit with gloves on.
Try: open any job on your phone and tap through the tabs.
- **Blue buttons have white text.** The blue New Job, Save and active-tab buttons had black text that was hard to read. It's white now.

Send what you find to Koy: which screen, which job, what you expected. Screenshots help.
