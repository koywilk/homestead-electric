NEW for admins: see anyone's My Jobs page

- **Admins now have My Jobs in the top bar.** Open it and use **Viewing as** at the top to pick any foreman, jr. foreman or lead. You see their page exactly as they see it: their jobs (a foreman's whole crew), margin and hours, job costs and reports.
- **Only admins see the picker.** Foremen, jr. foremen and leads still see only their own page. Nothing on it can be changed from there.

Try: open My Jobs, pick a foreman under Viewing as, then open one of their jobs.

No job data is changed by this update.
