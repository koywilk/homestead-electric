APP UPDATE v641

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v641.

CHANGED (Rough and Finish: Complete with punch still open)

- You can now set Rough or Finish to Complete, or to 100%, even if punch items are still open. Before, the app said "Cannot mark as complete" and stopped you.
- The app tells you how many items are still open when you do it.
- Rough: the job's row on the Job Board gets a red "4 rough items left" chip. Tap it to open the job on Rough. It goes away when the last item is checked off or voided.
- Finish: the job moves to Punch List on the Job Board, not Completed, until the last item is closed.
- Your My Day still shows the red "Open Rough Punch Items" task until they're done.

Only mark a phase Complete when the main work really is done. The leftovers stay on your punch list, so keep checking them off.

No job data was changed by this update.
