APP UPDATE v653

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v653.

NEW (My Day: My jobsite)

- Leads, jr. foremen and foremen: if you lead a job and someone is booked on it in Simpro today or tomorrow, a My jobsite card shows at the top of My Day.
- It lists everyone booked on your job that day with their hours. Tap a name to see the open punch items assigned to them. Show done this week lists what they finished.
- Unassigned: items nobody has yet. Pick a name in Assign to hand one out; it lands on their My Day.
- Waiting on others: items flagged waiting, with the reason. Not here: people with open items who aren't booked that day.
- Today / tomorrow switch at the top right, so you can hand out tomorrow's items the night before.
- Tap the box to check an item off. Tap it again to reopen it.

No job data is changed by this update.
