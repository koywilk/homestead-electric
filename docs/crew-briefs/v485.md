# Crew brief — App update v485 (2026-10-01)

App update v485. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings says v485. This one covers everything since v476: a batch of My Day work, two new tools in the Tools tab, appliance import on Home Runs, and some motion polish.

EVERYONE (resi and commercial)
- Answer a question right from My Day (Koy's request): a question assigned to you on a job's Rough or Finish tab shows on My Day. Tap Answer on the row, type it, Save answer. The answer lands on the question in the job and marks it answered. Undo for ten seconds if you fat-finger it.
  Try: open My Day, find a row tagged Question, tap Answer, type a one-word answer, Save. Open that job's Rough or Finish tab and confirm it's there.
- Reply instead of answering (Koy's request): the same row has Reply. That posts in the question's discussion thread without closing the question, for "which room do you mean?" type stuff. The crew on the plan in FieldInk sees it too.
  Try: tap Reply on a Question row, post something, then check the question's discussion on the job.
- New since you last looked (Koy's request): anything that wasn't on your My Day list the last time you opened it on that phone or laptop gets a blue New tag. A task that got a fresh reply gets New reply. Each category header counts them, like "3 new". It's per device, so your phone and your laptop each remember their own last look.
  Try: open My Day, close it, have someone send you a task, open it again. The new row should be tagged New.
- Approve time off from My Day (Koy's request, office only): a time-off request on the board now reads Time off and has Approve and Deny right on the row. Same result as the Time Off page: the request gets decided, the calendar gets the PTO entry, and the row closes itself.
  Try: next time a request lands, Approve it from My Day and check the Time Off page shows it approved.
- Tools tab, Appliance Loads (Koy's request): the master sheet of every job's appliance and equipment loads, one tab per job, with volts, amps and breaker, and a column that says whether each one is already in that job's Home Runs. It refreshes itself overnight.
  Try: Tools tab, Appliance Loads, find your job's tab.
- Tools tab, Service Size (Josh built it): the NEC 220.82 service calculator. Mark appliances Yes, Maybe or No, see what size service you need either way, and copy a bid note. Drop in a plan set PDF and it reads the sheets and fills the calculator for you. Limited to Josh, Brady, Koy and Jeromy for now.
  Try: Tools tab, Service Size, drop a plan PDF on it.
- Home Runs, Import appliances (Koy's request): on the job's Home Runs tab, next to By Panel / By Floor. It pulls that job's appliances from the master sheet, only that job number, shows you a preview, and adds them as home runs when you confirm. Green rows come in ready with a wire size, yellow rows come in with a CONFIRM note, red rows come in marked Need Specs. Pressing it twice doesn't double anything.
  Try: open a job's Home Runs, tap Import appliances, check the preview, Confirm.
- The app feels more alive: the Saving / Saved text is now a small chip with a spinner and a check; tap it if a save fails and it retries. When someone else edits a job, its row on the Job Board glows and shows who. Tabs slide, counters count up, progress bars fill in. Nothing about how the app works changed.

FIXES
- Import appliances window was see-through and unreadable on top of the page. It's a solid white card now.
- My Day rows read properly on a phone again. The buttons had been squeezing the text to one word per line.

REMOVED OR CHANGED
- Nothing removed. Time-off rows on My Day are tagged Time off instead of Task so they stand out.
- One thing to watch: the punch check-off was meant to draw a strike-through across the text left to right. It didn't show on Koy's iPhone, so treat that as not working yet. The normal strike-through still appears and nothing is lost.
