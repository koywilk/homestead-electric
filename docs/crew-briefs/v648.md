Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v648.

NEW (Dashboard: Meetings tab, the Weekly Scramble)

- Open /dashboard and tap Meetings (between People and Automations).
- Seven sections in the Scramble's order, all closed with a count: Schedule this week (with this week's Simpro booked days and names), Small jobs and return trips, Waiting on, Upcoming starts, Open items from the watches, Upcoming material, Last Scramble's next steps.
- + Note under any job saves a note on that job for this meeting. Each section also has a Section notes box.
- Action items: type it, pick who and a due date, then Send to their My Day in one tap. Undo takes it back while they haven't touched it.
- Add a job or item to any section, or Hide a row. Both stay until you remove or unhide them ("N hidden · show").
- The week picker opens earlier meetings to read, not edit.

No job data is changed by this update. Notes and action items save in their own place.
