Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v663.

NEW on the crew calendar (office)

- Untick everybody on a job and it stays on its day in red, "Nobody on it". Tap it to add people.
- Tick someone who is on another plan and it asks if you want to move them here instead.
- People on an auto-scheduled block show "free to pick". Pick them and the auto-schedule works around it.
- Every block says why it's on the calendar, and has an Open job card button. So do the rows under the calendar.
- Set pairs from the Crew Board stay together. Nobody gets pulled out of a pair to fill a seat.
- Fridays can be placed by hand. Estimated starts show on the calendar and can be taken off.

FIXED

- Two half days on two jobs no longer show as double-booked.
- The auto-schedule no longer puts someone on two afternoon jobs at once.
- A return trip booked from the calendar marks itself Scheduled on that day. If it comes off Simpro, it goes back to needs scheduling.
- Return trips book to Rough In or Finish, whichever the work is.
- Someone with time off is left off that day.
