FOR THE CREW

- You can copy someone else on a PO email to CED.
  Try: on a CED card tap Send to CED, or use New order from the bid. There's a CC box on the send screen and on the review screen. It's optional. Type up to 5 emails with a comma between them. bids@ and you are still copied on their own.
- If the app has been open more than 24 hours since you last typed your PIN, it now asks for your PIN again. Before this, an open app could quietly lose buttons like New order from the bid and Send to CED until you reloaded. Your work is saved first, the job closes, and you open it again after your PIN.
