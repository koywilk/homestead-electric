NEW (Rough punch list: Move items)

- When the Rough punch list has open items, a bar above it says "N open · Move items".
- Tap Move items, pick where they go, tick the items (or Select all), then tap Move.
- Finish punch list: the items show up on Finish in the same floor and room, tagged "From Rough". On Rough they stay greyed out, tagged "Moved to Finish", and no longer count as open. Check them off on Finish.
- Return trip: pick a new trip or one that's already open. The items go on the trip and stay on Rough tagged "Return Trip #N" until they're checked off. Checking one off on the trip checks it off on Rough too.
- Made a mistake? Tap Undo on the bar at the bottom within ten seconds.
