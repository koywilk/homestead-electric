# Crew brief — App update v523 (2026-10-08)

App update v523. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings says v523. One new list on Bid Items, and the first piece of sending POs from the app.

RESIDENTIAL ONLY
- Orders on Bid Items: the Bid Items tab now starts with an Orders section, folded, with the count on its header. It lists every PO card from Rough and Finish Material Tracking on that job: PO number, supplier, Need to Order, Order Sent or Picked Up, and who and when. Open ones sit at the top. Order material asks Rough-In or Finish, then adds a new PO card on that tab and takes you there to fill it in.
  Try: open a job you've ordered material for, go to Bid Items, and tap Orders. Every PO card from both phases should be there.
