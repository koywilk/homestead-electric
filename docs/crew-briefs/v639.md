NEW on printed panel schedules

- The printed schedules have a new look. Every sheet starts with a title block: the Homestead logo, the panel name big, then the job, address and counts.
- Lighting control schedule (Control 4, Lutron, Crestron): each module prints as its own box with the module number big on the left and its channels listed beside it. When nobody has filled in Type, Watts or Keypad on that panel, the boxes print two across, all the same size.
- Type, Watts and Keypad only print when someone has filled them in on that panel. An empty column is left off.
- The Pulled column is gone from the printed lighting schedule on every system. Pulled is still tracked in the app, it just does not print.
- Electrical panel schedule on Home Runs: the slot numbers are large down the middle. A 240V two-pole breaker has a bar down the outside edge of both its slots and a small 240V tag, so you can see the pair at a glance.
- Savant panel sheets and the Savant pull list have the same title block.
- It is made for our black and white printer and uses very little toner.
- Print and Download look the same. Nothing about the schedule's information changed.

If something looks off

- A schedule that used to fit one page now takes two, or a box is cut in half at a page edge: tell Koy which job and panel.
- The lettering looks plain or the title is squeezed: you were probably offline when you printed. Print again with signal.
