# Crew brief — App update v553 (2026-10-17)

App update v553. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings says v553.

CREW
- **No more "Ready to invoice."** Moving a job's rough or finish % no longer turns on Ready to invoice, and nobody gets that push or My Day task any more. The office tracks invoicing straight from Simpro.
  Try: set a job's rough to 85%. The note under it no longer mentions invoicing.

OFFICE
- **Invoicing comes from Simpro.** Every weekday morning the app reads Simpro. Under a job's Simpro # in Job Info it shows "Invoiced 62% so far" or "Invoiced in full" (no dollar amounts in the crew app; the dashboard's job card shows $ billed and $ left to bill). At 99.5% it counts as invoiced in full.
- **Jobs Simpro has closed get completed here.** When Simpro shows a job as Invoiced or Complete and billed in full, the app marks it complete. Punch, return trips and failed inspections on it stay as they are.
- **Billed change orders get completed.** On a job invoiced in full, approved change orders are marked completed and show as done on the punch list and the COs tab, signed "Simpro sync".
- **You can see and undo every change.** The dashboard's Needs you has "Changed by the Simpro sync this week". Open a job to see what changed and tap Undo. The sync won't redo anything you undid.
  Try: open the dashboard Monday after 6:15 AM, tap that line.
- **Dashboard (from v551–v552, released here):** "Closed in Simpro — close it here?", "Billed — mark done?", quote $ on change orders waiting 60+ days, Reassign to the Simpro technician on jobs with no foreman, a Coordinator on each job (Job Info, admins) with a dashboard filter, Move / unconfirm on starts in the Schedule tab, and one "Needs a date" number on every tab.
