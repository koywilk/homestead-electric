Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v658.

NEW for the office: pairs on the Crew Board

- Settings › Crew Board: tap someone, then Paired with, to set who they go out with. A foreman, jr foreman or lead pairs with an apprentice or crew member on the same crew.
- Pairs show as one linked card on the board. The crew calendar now sends pairs out together.

NEW on the crew calendar

- Tap + more on a busy day and that day's jobs open right over the calendar. Esc or a tap outside closes it.
- Tap a day's date to see who's scheduled that day and who isn't yet, crew by crew.

No job data is changed by this update.
