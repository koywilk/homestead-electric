# Crew brief — App update v515 (2026-10-06)

App update v515. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings says v515. This one is about keeping everybody on the newest version of the app, so nobody saves from an old copy.

EVERYONE (RESI AND COMMERCIAL)
- New "A new version is ready" card. When an update comes out while you're in the middle of something, a small card shows at the bottom with Update now and Later. Update now saves what you were doing, then reloads. Later hides it for 30 minutes. If the app isn't busy, it still updates by itself like before.
  Try: nothing to do. When you see the card, tap Update now when you're at a good stopping point.
- Sometimes the office will require the newest version. Then you get a full-screen "Update now" that you can't close. If you're typing, a red bar at the top gives you up to 5 minutes to finish and save first. Your work is saved before it reloads, so nothing you did is lost.
  Try: if you ever see it, tap Update now. If it keeps coming back after updating, close the app all the way and open it again.

OFFICE ONLY
- Settings has a new "?" guide, and Settings → Devices shows each phone's version, which ones are behind, and a Require button to make everyone update.

REMOVED OR CHANGED
