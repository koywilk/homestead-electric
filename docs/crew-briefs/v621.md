CHANGED (anyone who makes POs on Material Tracking)

- Source on a PO card now lists every supplier in Simpro. Lowe's is near the top. The rest (Codale, White Cap, Sunbelt, the cities and more) are under "More suppliers in Simpro".
- Pick any of them and tap Get a PO number. You get the number on the card, the same as a Home Depot run. The app only emails CED. For anyone else, give or send them the number yourself.
- Lowe's cards with a PO number take a receipt photo, the same as Home Depot, ACE and Amazon.
- Other means the supplier isn't in Simpro, so there's no PO number. The card says so. Ask the office to add the supplier.

No job data was changed by this update.
