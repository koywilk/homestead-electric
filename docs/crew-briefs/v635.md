APP UPDATE v635

Pull down to refresh and check Settings shows v635.

NEW for everyone who orders material

- No Rough In or Finish cost center on the job? Send no longer stops at an error. Tap Send on the PO card like always. If the job has no Rough In or Finish in Simpro (a small job, a generator, a service call), the send screen shows Charge to with that job's own cost centers. Pick one, tap Send again, and the PO goes on the one you picked.
- Nothing is made in Simpro until you pick, and a second tap never makes a second PO.
- Jobs that have their Rough In and Finish work exactly like before. No extra step.

NEW on printed panel schedules

- Every printed schedule now has one big, faint Homestead logo behind the page and a sharp, larger Homestead logo in the header. The old header logo was tiny and blurry.
- This covers the lighting control schedule (Control 4, Lutron, Crestron, Savant), the electrical panel schedule on Home Runs, the Savant panel cover, and the Savant panel cover and pull list. Both Print and Download.
- The schedule itself is the same: same rows, same layout, same text. The watermark is light enough that every circuit still reads on a black and white printer.

If something looks off

- Charge to list is empty or the old error still shows: pull down to refresh, then tell Koy the job number.
- Watermark missing on a print: tell Koy which schedule and whether it was Print or Download.
