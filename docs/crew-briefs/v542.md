# Crew brief — App update v542 (2026-10-15)

App update v542. Pull down to refresh once and check Settings says v542.

EVERYONE (RESI AND COMMERCIAL)
- **Say what a job is waiting on.** When Rough or Finish is set to On Hold or Awaiting Start Date, a "Waiting on…" line shows right under the status. Type who or what it's waiting on (cabinets, the GC's date, the homeowner's decision). The office sees it on the job without calling you.
  Try: open a job that's on hold, tap the line under the Rough or Finish status and type what it's waiting on.
- **"Didn't happen" for an inspection that never came.** If the 4-way target or the final inspection date has passed and nobody logged Pass or Fail, a "Didn't happen" button shows next to Pass / Fail. It clears the date so the job stops showing as an overdue inspection. Put the new date in when it's booked.
  Try: if you have an inspection date that passed without the inspector, tap Didn't happen, then set the new date.

REMINDER
- **Type the correction items when an inspection fails.** Use + Item under the inspection. Seven failed inspections right now have no list of what to fix, so nobody can tell what's left.

The "?" guides for Rough and Finish are updated.
