NEW for admins: copy and share Claude reports

- Dashboard, Claude tab, Reports: open a report and tap Copy. Pick Foreman / crew or Owners / admins, and Text or Email, then Copy and paste it anywhere.
- The Foreman / crew copy leaves out every dollar and cost line. Margin % and hours stay.
- Share link copies a link to the report. Anyone on the team can open it in the app after signing in. Admins see the full report, everyone else sees the foreman copy.
- Turn sharing off to stop a link. Owners-only reports can't be shared.

NEW for everyone

- If someone sends you a report link, open it on your phone, sign in if asked, and the report opens over the app. Copy text is at the bottom.
